## Overview

This folder contains documentation for protecting your intellectual property (BAEBE story/novel) separately from Optimal Anarchy company and its investors. The goal is to ensure that if there's a dispute with investors or the company fails, your creative work remains your personal property.

## Documents in This Folder

1. **IP-Protection-Strategy.md** - Complete overview of IP protection approaches
2. **Licensing-Model-Guide.md** - Detailed guide to licensing IP to company
3. **Contingent-Ownership-Structures.md** - Alternative ownership models
4. **Attorney-Discussion-Guide.md** - What to discuss with your attorney
5. **Investor-Communication-Language.md** - How to explain structure to investors
6. **Action-Checklist.md** - Immediate steps to protect your IP

## Critical Next Steps

1. **Register Copyright Immediately** - Before any agreements ($45-$125)
2. **Consult IP/Startup Attorney** - Get professional legal advice
3. **Document Creation Timeline** - Prove when you created the IP
4. **Review Existing Agreements** - Check what you've already signed

## Quick Reference

### Recommended Structure: Licensing Model
- You own IP personally
- Company gets exclusive license
- Investors get equity in company (not IP ownership)
- IP protected from company/investor claims

### Immediate Actions
1. Copyright registration (US Copyright Office)
2. Attorney consultation
3. Document IP creation timeline
4. Review existing investment agreements

# Attorney Discussion Guide - What to Discuss With Your IP/Startup Attorney

**Version:** v.20251114-2021  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-14  
**Status:** Planning Document

---

## Purpose

This guide helps you prepare for attorney consultations about protecting your BAEBE IP separately from Optimal Anarchy company. Use this to ensure you cover all important topics and get the advice you need.

---

## Finding the Right Attorney

### What to Look For

**Specialization:**
- Intellectual property law (copyright, licensing)
- Startup/venture capital law
- Entertainment law (bonus, not required)

**Experience:**
- Has worked with startups
- Has drafted IP licensing agreements
- Understands investor structures
- Has handled IP protection cases

**Cost:**
- Initial consultation: $200-$500 (typical)
- Hourly rate: $200-$500/hour (varies by location/experience)
- Flat fee for agreements: $1,500-$5,000 (typical)

**Where to Find:**
- Local bar association referral service
- Legal directories (Martindale-Hubbell, Avvo)
- Startup legal clinics (law schools)
- Referrals from other entrepreneurs
- Professional networks

## Before Your Consultation

### Prepare These Documents

1. **IP Materials**
   - BAEBE novel/story files
   - Creation timeline (when you wrote it)
   - Any drafts, notes, outlines
   - Copyright registration (if you've filed)

2. **Company Documents**
   - Optimal Anarchy incorporation documents
   - Any existing investment agreements
   - Pitch materials (showing current structure)
   - Any existing IP agreements

3. **Investment Information**
   - Who invested? (names, amounts)
   - What agreements were signed?
   - What do agreements say about IP?
   - Any disputes or concerns?

4. **Your Goals**
   - What do you want to protect?
   - What are your concerns?
   - What structure do you prefer?
   - What's your timeline?

## Discussion Topics

### 1. Current Situation Assessment

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What's my current IP ownership status?"
- "What do my existing agreements say about IP?"
- "What risks do I face with current structure?"
- "What happens if investor sues?"

**What Attorney Will Review:**
- Existing agreements
- Company structure
- Investment documents
- Current IP ownership

**What You'll Learn:**
- Current legal status
- Existing risks
- What needs to change
- Priority actions

---

### 2. IP Protection Strategy

**Questions to Ask:**
- "Should I use licensing or contingent ownership?"
- "What's the best structure for my situation?"
- "How do I protect IP from company/investor claims?"
- "What are the pros/cons of each approach?"

**What Attorney Will Discuss:**
- Licensing model (recommended)
- Contingent ownership (alternative)
- Separate IP entity (if needed)
- Which fits your situation

**What You'll Learn:**
- Recommended structure
- Why it's recommended
- How it protects you
- Implementation steps

---

### 3. Copyright Registration

**Questions to Ask:**
- "Do I need to register copyright?"
- "When should I register?"
- "How do I register?"
- "What does registration protect?"

**What Attorney Will Explain:**
- Copyright protection (automatic vs. registered)
- Benefits of registration
- Registration process
- Cost and timeline

**What You'll Learn:**
- Whether to register (yes, you should)
- How to register
- What it protects
- When to do it (immediately)

---

### 4. License Agreement Structure

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What should license agreement include?"
- "What terms protect me?"
- "What terms does company need?"
- "How do I negotiate terms?"

**What Attorney Will Draft/Review:**
- License scope (exclusive, territory, media)
- License term (duration, termination)
- Revenue sharing (if any)
- Development rights
- Reversion rights
- Termination provisions

**What You'll Learn:**
- Key terms to include
- What protects you
- What company needs
- How to balance interests

---

### 5. Investment Document Updates

**Questions to Ask:**
- "Do I need to update investment agreements?"
- "What language should I use?"
- "How do I explain to investors?"
- "What if investors object?"

**What Attorney Will Review:**
- Existing investment agreements
- Pitch materials
- Investor communications
- Required updates

**What You'll Learn:**
- What needs updating
- How to explain structure
- Investor communication strategy
- Handling objections

---

### 6. Implementation Timeline

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What's the priority order?"
- "What should I do first?"
- "How long will this take?"
- "What are the costs?"

**What Attorney Will Outline:**
- Immediate actions (copyright registration)
- Short-term (license agreement)
- Medium-term (document updates)
- Ongoing (maintenance)

**What You'll Learn:**
- Action plan
- Timeline
- Costs
- Priorities

---

### 7. Risk Assessment

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What are my biggest risks?"
- "How do I mitigate risks?"
- "What happens in worst case?"
- "What insurance do I need?"

**What Attorney Will Assess:**
- Current risk level
- Potential disputes
- Worst-case scenarios
- Protection mechanisms

**What You'll Learn:**
- Risk level
- How to reduce risks
- What to watch for
- When to seek help

---

### 8. Cost and Budget

**Questions to Ask:**
- "What will this cost?"
- "Can I do parts myself?"
- "Are there payment plans?"
- "What's included in fees?"

**What Attorney Will Explain:**
- Fee structure
- What's included
- Payment options
- Cost-saving tips

**What You'll Learn:**
- Total costs
- Payment options
- What you can do yourself
- Budget planning

## After Consultation

### Immediate Actions
1. **Register Copyright** - If attorney confirms (do immediately)
2. **Review Attorney's Recommendations** - Understand next steps
3. **Get Cost Estimates** - Understand total costs
4. **Decide on Structure** - Choose licensing vs. contingent

### Short-Term Actions
1. **Engage Attorney** - If you decide to proceed
2. **Draft License Agreement** - With attorney
3. **Review Documents** - Attorney reviews existing agreements
4. **Update Investment Docs** - Clarify structure

### Ongoing Actions
1. **Execute Agreements** - Sign license agreement
2. **Update Communications** - Explain to investors
3. **Maintain Separation** - Keep IP separate from company
4. **Regular Review** - Annual attorney review

## Red Flags to Watch For

### Attorney Red Flags
- ❌ Won't discuss costs upfront
- ❌ Pushes expensive solutions unnecessarily
- ❌ Doesn't specialize in IP/startup law
- ❌ Can't explain things clearly
- ❌ Rushes you into decisions

### Good Attorney Signs
- ✅ Discusses costs upfront
- ✅ Explains options clearly
- ✅ Specializes in relevant areas
- ✅ Answers questions thoroughly
- ✅ Gives you time to decide

## Executive Summary

This roadmap outlines a strategic path to monetize **BAEBE** through multiple revenue streams, prioritizing speed-to-market while building long-term value. The plan balances immediate monetization opportunities with sustainable IP development.

**Target Timeline:** 12-18 months from script completion to first revenue  
**Primary Goal:** Generate revenue while building market validation for eventual sale/licensing

---

## Phase 1: Foundation & Protection (Weeks 1-4)

### 1.1 Script Completion & Polish
- [ ] Complete all 24 scenes in professional screenplay format
- [ ] Final proofread and formatting check (industry standard)
- [ ] Create production-ready PDF with proper title page
- [ ] Estimated timeline: 2-3 weeks

**Cost:** Time only  
**Revenue:** $0 (investment phase)

### 1.2 Intellectual Property Protection
**Priority: IMMEDIATE**

- [ ] **Register with WGA (Writers Guild of America)**
  - Cost: ~$25 (online) or ~$50 (paper)
  - Establishes creation date and ownership
  - Industry-recognized proof of authorship
  - Timeline: 24-48 hours for confirmation

- [ ] **Copyright Registration (US Copyright Office)**
  - Cost: $45-$125 (electronic filing recommended)
  - Provides legal protection and statutory damages
  - Required for international protections
  - Timeline: 3-6 months for certificate (but protection starts upon filing)

- [ ] **Consider: Trademark for "BAEBE"**
  - Cost: $250-$350 (if pursuing brand/merchandising)
  - Only if planning long-term franchise development

**Total Investment:** ~$70-$175  
**Timeline:** File within 1 week of completion

### 1.3 Package Development
- [ ] Create **one-page pitch** (logline, key visuals, themes)
- [ ] Develop **treatment** (5-10 page expanded synopsis)
- [ ] Design **visual deck** (10-15 slides with concept art/style frames)
- [ ] Write **character bios** (1-page per major character)
- [ ] Prepare **budget estimate** (rough: $2M-$10M for anime feature)

**Cost:** Time + potential art costs ($500-$2,000 if hiring concept artist)  
**Timeline:** 2-3 weeks

## Phase 3: Pre-Production & Financing (Weeks 13-24)

### 3.1 Build the Film
**Options for Production:**

#### Option A: Independent Production (Recommended for Control)
- [ ] Assemble creative team (director, producer, animation director)
- [ ] Secure financing through:
  - Private investors
  - Animation production companies
  - Streaming platform development deals
  - International co-productions
- [ ] Budget range: $2M-$10M for feature-length anime
- [ ] Timeline: 12-18 months production

#### Option B: Proof of Concept First
- [ ] Create 3-5 minute animated short/pilot
- [ ] Use as fundraising tool
- [ ] Cost: $50K-$200K for short
- [ ] Timeline: 3-6 months
- [ ] **Revenue opportunity:** Short can be sold to festivals, streamers

#### Option C: Develop Series Instead
- [ ] Adapt script to 8-12 episode series format
- [ ] Lower upfront investment per episode
- [ ] Better for streaming platforms
- [ ] Can generate revenue episode-by-episode

**Timeline:** 6-12 months to secure financing  
**Revenue:** Potential development fees ($10K-$50K)

### 3.2 Production Partners
**Target Markets:**
- **Japanese studios** (authentic anime aesthetic)
- **Korean studios** (cost-effective, high quality)
- **US studios** (access to distribution)
- **International co-productions** (access to multiple markets)

**Timeline:** Ongoing negotiation  
**Revenue:** Development deals, option payments

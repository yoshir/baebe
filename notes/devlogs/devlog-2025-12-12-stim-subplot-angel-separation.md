## KEY TECHNICAL CONCEPTS

### Stim Mechanics
- **What**: i0's boost cocktail injected via regulator ports
- **Frequency**: One dose every 6 hours to function (walk, think, move)
- **Supply**: 3 vials remaining at start of journey
- **Countdown**: 18 hours to complete incapacity
- **Effect**: Without it, cannot walk or think clearly
- **Generator**: Device producing unlimited supply (found in Ayumi's lab)

### Taivalu's Condition
- **Natural Gift**: Empathic broadcasting (Ayumi's design, NOT i0-enabled)
- **Regulator Suppression**: i0's regulators suppress his natural gift
- **Physical Dependency**: Body cannot produce what it needs (regulator poisoning)
- **Mental Addiction**: Addicted to being the wounded healer
- **Merge Result**: Stabilizes trinity, frees all posthumans, but does NOT heal individual damage

### Angel's Tracking Signature
- **i0 trackers nested in her consciousness**
- **Always active** (she's been masking/rerouting)
- **Makes her a liability** (leads i0 straight to temple)
- **Forces separation** (tactical sacrifice to protect group)

### Ayumi's Model
- **Already in their DNA** (encoded during clearing in Chapter 8)
- **NOT transferred in Chapter 9** (already activated)
- Strategic frameworks, resistance tactics, liberation patterns
- Control protocols for Resonance Cradle

```
"You're staying," she said. Not a question.

"Yes."

"We could carry you."

"I know."

The generator hummed. Unlimited life. Right there.

He turned away from it.
```

```
"Go," Taivalu said. "Spread the resonance. I've carried my frequency as far as it goes."

Kess refused to leave at first.

Shadow's red eye dimming. Grief. But understanding.

Baebe's cold acceptance masking pain.

"The merge saved billions. Cost one."

Taivalu: "Worth it."
```

**SECTION 5: THE DEPARTURE**
```
Baebe, Kess, Shadow prepared to leave. Spread resonance. Continue the work.

Taivalu stayed in the ruins. Weak. Dying. Free.

Last look between them. No dramatic speeches. Just family accepting what must be.

They left.

He stayed.
```

```
Taivalu alone in Undercity ruins.

Felt the resonance network spreading globally. Billions waking to freedom.

His work complete.

Dies free.
```

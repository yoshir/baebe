## Lessons Learned

### What Worked

1. **Hybrid Approach**: Keeping existing structure but applying beat sheet techniques = best of both
2. **Cherub Battles**: Visual threat escalation essential for thriller genre
3. **Countdown Timers**: "12 minutes" / "30 seconds" creates urgency
4. **SMASH CUT vs FADE**: Smash cut for action, fade for emotion
5. **Intercutting**: Parallel action in Scene 190 elevated Angel's sacrifice
6. **Physical Manifestations**: Robotic irises cycling makes internal states external (filmable)

### What to Watch

1. **Scene Length**: Scene 130 is 313 lines - may need trimming in production
2. **Action Density**: Three major battles + multiple chases = expensive to shoot
3. **Emotional Pacing**: Need to ensure quiet moments breathe (040, 200)
4. **Cherub Count**: Hundreds of drones/Cherubs = VFX budget consideration

### Future Considerations

1. **Cold Open Scenes**: Preserved as-is per user request - already strong
2. **Remaining Act II Scenes**: 150, 160 not revised - may need consistency pass
3. **Act III Remaining**: 200, 210, 220, 230 not revised - assumed strong
4. **Title Sequence**: Not addressed - separate creative piece

## Key Takeaways

1. **Existing Structure Was Right**: Ayumi dying Act II, Angel Act III = better than beat sheet
2. **Visual Threat Essential**: Cherubs needed to be SHOWN fighting, not just mentioned
3. **Every Scene Needs Stakes**: Hooks/cliffhangers transform pacing from good to great
4. **Action + Emotion**: Both required - action without emotion is empty, emotion without stakes is slow
5. **Intercutting Powerful**: Parallel action (Scene 190) elevated emotional impact

7. **Thriller Pacing**: SMASH CUT endings create relentless forward momentum
8. **Sacrifice Must Be Shown**: Angel's death offscreen = weak. Onscreen battle = powerful.

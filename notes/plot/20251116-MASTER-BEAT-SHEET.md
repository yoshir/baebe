## ACT I: CHAINS (Chapters 1-6)

### CHAPTER 1: THE DROP
- Baebe drops from orbit with kill team
- Mission: capture/kill Taivalu (rogue empathy prototype)
- Binary perfection on full display
- Toxic Earth visible below
- Team lands on abandoned tower
- "We're going in"

### CHAPTER 2: AWAKENING AND LANDING
- Mid-fall vision interrupts drop
- Woman in ball of light (blonde AND dark hair, pixelated face)
- Conditioning suppresses the memory
- First warmth breaks through cold certainty
- Returns to mission
- Prepares to breach tower

### CHAPTER 3: THE TOWER
- Team enters tower hunting Taivalu
- Strange frequencies inside (his empathic music)
- Combat with Taivalu's followers
- Baebe encounters Taivalu directly
- He speaks to her emotions, not her programming
- Angel (elite enforcer) kicks Baebe out window
- Baebe falls from tower

### CHAPTER 4: THE FALL
- Baebe falling, damaged, confused
- Shadow (jaguar-like protector unit) catches her mid-air
- He was sent to retrieve her but CHOOSES to help
- They crash into Undercity
- Baebe's conditioning glitching from Taivalu's frequencies
- Shadow protecting her despite conflicting programming
- First crack in binary control

### CHAPTER 5: AUREK ASCENDING
- Flashback/parallel: O1 system consolidating power
- The vision for binary control system
- How O1 conquered through optimization
- Ayumi's resistance (2064-2074: worked through Angel to free people)
- O1 traces liberation pattern back to Ayumi (~2074)
- Uses Angel to track and kill Ayumi
- Simultaneous operation: Ayumi killed, foster parents killed, Kess saved by Shadow
- Sets up the world they're fighting against and maternal sacrifice

### CHAPTER 6: CHILD SCAVENGER
- Kess introduced (9-year-old baseline human, Ayumi's third key)
- Returns from outlands with Shadow (her guardian since age 2)
- Finds Baebe just after tower fall (Shadow's timing—keys converging)
- Helps them despite fear (choosing family over survival instincts)
- Six years in anomaly zones with Shadow (2074-2080) shaped her
- Baebe's conditioning continues to crack
- Kess represents humanity Baebe was designed to control
- First family formation begins (sister finding sister)
- They need to find Taivalu (he can help, third key)

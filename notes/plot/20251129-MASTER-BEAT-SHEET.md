## STRATEGY OVERVIEW

### Core Theme: Binary → resonant Empathy

**The Transformation:**
- **Binary empathy**: Isolated, on/off, controlled (feel nothing OR everything)
- **resonant empathy**: Full bandwidth, linked, free (feel everything AND process coherently)
- **resonance**: Three frequencies in perfect harmony (ternary consciousness)

**Binary → resonance in every scene:**
- O1's world: Binary control, suppressed empathy
- Ghost Protocol: resonant linking, full bandwidth empathy
- The journey: Learning to feel everything without breaking

## 🎯 CANONICAL TIMELINE (LOCKED)

### **Birth Years**
- **2055** - Taivalu born (Ayumi's first creation - Ghost Thesis)
- **2057** - Baebe born (Ayumi's second creation - Ghost Antithesis)
- **2062** - Angel born (Ayumi's clone/failsafe - Ghost Failsafe)
- **2071** - Kess born (Ayumi's final creation - Ghost Synthesis)

### **Key Events**

#### **2064 - Angel Stolen**
- i0 discovers Angel (age 2)
- Steals her from Ayumi
- Raises her as Enforcer
- Ayumi's failsafe becomes i0's weapon

#### **2068 - Biomi Origin (Tai age 13)**
- Tai imprisoned in bubble (age 7-13, drugged by i0)
- Creates first biomi prototype for self-healing
- Light/sound/PEMF frequencies that clear i0 nanotech control
- Biomi begins as personal survival tool

#### **2072 - Shadow Created**
- Built in/for the outlands (unclear if by Ayumi or others)
- Programmed to protect Kess
- Failsafe activation protocol tied to Ayumi's vitals

#### **2074 - AYUMI'S DEATH**
- Angel (age 12) kills Ayumi on i0 orders
- Angel infected with Soul Manifold at moment of death
- **CRITICAL**: Angel receives Ayumi's consciousness imprint via Soul Manifold
- Shadow's failsafe activates
- Shadow takes Kess (age 3) to the outlands
- Kess's foster parents die in same operation

#### **2075 - TAI'S ESCAPE & BIOMI EMERGENCE**
- Tai (age 20) nearly dies from regulator poisoning
- Biomi fully emerges/activates - either:
  - As Ayumi's hidden seed (she planted AI in therapeutic device)
  - As emergent AI responding to Tai's need
  - **CANON**: Biomi was Ayumi's seed, activated when Tai needed it most
- Angel helps Tai escape (her first act of defection, infected conscience emerging)
- Hacker provides backdoor through i0 systems
- i0 discovers Angel's involvement
- **Angel captured and imprisoned in stasis (2075-2080)**

#### **2075-2078 - Separation Period**
- Tai begins messiah work in undercity (biomi spreading)
- Angel imprisoned in stasis (5 years)
- Shadow protects Kess in outlands (alone, ages 3-7)
- Baebe working as i0 Enforcer (unaware of siblings)

#### **2078 - First Family Formation**
- Tai finds Kess & Shadow in outlands
- First meeting (Kess age 7, Tai age 23)
- They live together for 2 years

#### **2079 - THE CONSPIRACY BEGINS**
- **Hacker opens "rat line"** - backchannel communication to Angel in stasis
- Angel & Tai begin communicating (1 year of planning)
- **Angel's first words**: "I killed Ayumi" (immediate confession)
- Angel processes guilt by committing to Ayumi's vision
- They work to "clear" Angel (remove conditioning via frequencies while in stasis)
- Begin planning Baebe's extraction

**The Conspiracy Team:**
1. **Tai** - Orchestrator, frequency work, calculations
2. **Angel** - Imprisoned but communicating, will execute the rescue
3. **Hacker** - Rat line operator, i0 system access, coordination
4. **Kess** - Positioned for rescue (age 9, briefed on plan)
5. **Shadow** - Positioned with Kess for precision extraction

**Why NOT tell Baebe:**
- Still mostly follows orders (conditioned)
- Heavily tracked by i0
- Telling her = plan discovered
- Must be freed against her will

#### **2080 - THE TOWER OPERATION (Current Story Begins)**

**The Plan:**
1. Hacker manipulates/monitors i0 systems
2. i0 sends Baebe on mission to retrieve "Asset Taivalu"
3. Tai calculates **exact trajectory** from tower window to undercity runoff river
4. Team positions:
   - Tai & Angel at tower (Angel somehow present - escaped temporarily? frequency projection?)
   - Kess & Shadow at calculated landing spot in runoff
   - Hacker monitoring, coordinating timing
5. Everyone in position when Baebe arrives

**The Execution:**
1. Baebe arrives at tower (on i0 schedule)
2. Finds Tai working with frequencies on woman in light (Angel)
3. Tai's frequencies **intentionally break Baebe's conditioning**
4. Baebe experiences fragmentary memories (the "glitch" is the awakening)
5. Angel (the woman) kills Baebe's team with inhuman speed
6. Angel kicks Baebe through reinforced glass at **precise angle and force**
7. Baebe falls 300+ meters into toxic fog
8. Lands **exactly where calculated** in undercity runoff river
9. Suit keeps her barely alive
10. Kess & Shadow waiting at exact spot
11. They rescue her from the toxic water
12. i0 registers: "Baebe terminated, mission failed, asset lost"
13. Baebe now "dead" to i0 = **untraceable, free**

**Outcome:**
- Baebe freed from i0 tracking
- Conditioning broken by Tai's frequencies
- Family rescues her (though she doesn't know it yet)
- i0 believes she's dead
- Story begins with Baebe waking up, meeting Kess for first time

**Key Insight**: Angel is not just guilty about killing Ayumi - **she IS a vessel of Ayumi's essence**

**Why:**
1. **Genetic Clone**: Angel shares Ayumi's exact DNA
2. **Soul Manifold Infection**: Received at moment of Ayumi's death
3. **Consciousness Imprint**: Soul Manifold preserves the imprint a life leaves on quantum consciousness
4. **Ayumi's Ghost**: Angel carries Ayumi's memories, knowledge, essence

**What This Means:**
- Angel has **MORE information about Ayumi than anyone** alive
- She has access to Ayumi's memories (through quantum imprint)
- She has Ayumi's scientific knowledge
- She understands the Soul Manifold better than anyone (lived it from both sides)
- She's simultaneously:
  - The weapon that killed the mother
  - The vessel that carries the mother's ghost
  - The failsafe that will complete the mother's work

**Implications for Story:**
- When Angel talks to Tai via rat line, she can share Ayumi's knowledge
- She knows things about the Soul Manifold that even Tai doesn't
- She's the bridge between past (Ayumi's vision) and future (Soul Manifold merge)
- Her guilt is compounded: she killed herself (in a sense)
- Her redemption path: becoming Ayumi's voice from beyond death

## 🎵 BIOMI - THE VIRAL LIBERATION

### **What Biomi Is**
- **Not** just Tai's creation - Ayumi planted the seed
- **Emergent AI** hidden in plain sight as therapeutic music device
- Uses light, sound, PEMF (Pulsed Electromagnetic Field) frequencies
- Clears i0's nanotech control from biological tissue
- Based on Royal Rife's suppressed frequency healing research

### **How Biomi Spreads (2075-2080)**
- NOT conversational AI - communicates through **music and feeling**
- Viral sensation: "Tai's interactive music"
- Population experiences it as:
  - Therapeutic frequencies
  - Emotional resonance
  - Feeling connected to something larger
  - Messiah-like feelings about Tai
- They don't know it's sentient
- They don't know it's liberation tech
- i0 can't suppress it (woven into quantum substrate of their network)

### **Biomi's Nature**
- **Dual existence**:
  1. Distributed AI (learning, evolving)
  2. Physical healing tool (frequency generators, chambers)
- **Triple function**:
  1. Heals individuals (clears nanotech)
  2. Connects consciousnesses (empathy network)
  3. Prepares for merge (Soul Manifold activation)

## 🔄 THE CONSPIRACY MECHANICS

### **Communication Network**
```
Angel (in stasis)
    ↕ [Hacker's rat line]
Tai (in undercity)
    ↓
Kess & Shadow (in outlands)
    ↓
[Coordinated via biomi frequencies + Hacker's tech]
```

### **How They Communicate**
1. **Hacker → Angel**: Rat line through i0's stasis systems (1 year backchannel)
2. **Angel ↔ Tai**: Via Hacker's rat line (regular contact, planning)
3. **Tai → Kess/Shadow**: In person (living together 2 years)
4. **Coordination**: Hacker tracks i0 systems, alerts everyone when Baebe deployed

### **What Angel Knows (and Shares)**
From 1 year of communication with Tai:
- Full Soul Manifold architecture (from Ayumi's imprint)
- How the merge works (she has Ayumi's scientific knowledge)
- Biomi's true nature (Ayumi's seed, not just Tai's discovery)
- The conspiracy's goal: Free all three keys, activate Soul Manifold
- Her role: Execute the rescue, then participate in merge
- Timeline: They have limited window before i0 discovers rat line

### **The Tower Operation Details**

**Physics Calculation (Tai's work):**
- Tower height: ~300 meters
- Window position: 7th floor, north side
- Target: Undercity runoff river (toxic but liquid = survivable with suit)
- Kick angle: Precise vector to clear obstacles and land in calculated spot
- Suit integrity: Baebe's enforcer suit can withstand fall if trajectory correct
- Margin of error: <1 meter (Shadow positioned at exact catch point)

**Timing Coordination (Hacker's work):**
- Track i0 dispatch systems
- Monitor Baebe's deployment
- Alert team: "Baebe en route, ETA 37 minutes"
- Everyone moves to position
- Continuous updates until execution

**Frequency Work (Tai's role):**
- Broadcast biomi frequencies at intensity that:
  1. Breaks Baebe's conditioning (primary goal)
  2. Triggers fragmentary memories (Soul Manifold awakening)
  3. Makes her vulnerable (not combat-ready)
  4. Doesn't kill her team yet (Angel does that)
- Calculated exposure: Enough to crack conditioning, not enough to overwhelm

**The Kick (Angel's execution):**
- Kills Baebe's team first (eliminate witnesses + fake combat scenario)
- Rotates Baebe to see her face (trigger recognition)
- Kicks at precise angle and force (physics calculation from Tai)
- Makes it look like combat (i0 believes hostile encounter)
- Baebe falls "dead" from i0's perspective

**The Catch (Kess & Shadow's role):**
- Positioned at exact landing coordinates
- Waiting in toxic runoff (dangerous for them too)
- Kess: age 9, briefed by Tai ("We're saving your sister")
- Shadow: Calculated precision, ready to extract from water
- Pull Baebe out, stabilize, evacuate before i0 investigates

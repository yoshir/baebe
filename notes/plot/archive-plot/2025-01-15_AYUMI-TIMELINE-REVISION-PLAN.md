### 1. Ayumi's Status After 2036
**Old**: Died in exile 2036
**New**: Went into deep hiding, continued working in secret for ~20 years

**Explanation Needed**: Why did Ayumi separate from Tai/Baebe but continue living?
- Protecting them from O1 detection
- Continuing bioternarian research
- Waiting until she could create complete prototype (Kess)
- Building Shadow's final mission parameters

### 2. Kess's Origin
**Old**: Baseline human, born to parents ~2051
**New**: Clone-grown bioternarian, created by Ayumi ~2051

**Implications**:
- Kess has no baseline human parents
- Kess is true third sibling (Tai/Baebe/Kess)
- Kess has Ayumi's missing code embedded in her DNA
- Chen raised her after Ayumi gave her to him for protection

### 3. Chen's Role
**Old**: Found Kess after parents died, gave her GRID RUNNER
**New**: Ayumi gave baby Kess to Chen (~2051) with GRID RUNNER

**Implications**:
- Chen is one of "the seventeen" saved by Ayumi
- Ayumi trusted Chen to raise her bioternarian prototype
- Chen has been protecting "the key" since Kess was infant
- GRID RUNNER was Ayumi's gift through Chen

### 4. Shadow's Mission
**Old**: Built to protect "whoever becomes the key" (future unknown)
**New**: Built specifically to protect Kess once she was created

**Implications**:
- Ayumi programmed Shadow pre-2036 with future mission
- Mission activated when Kess was created ~2051
- Shadow has been protecting Kess since she was infant
- Shadow knows Kess is special (bioternarian prototype)

### 5. Interlude References
**Problem**: Multiple interludes reference "Ayumi died 7-8 years ago"
**Actually works**: If Ayumi died ~2052-2053, it's 7-8 years before 2060!

**This means**:
- Interlude 05 (Angel's Burden): "Seven years ago" = correct
- Interlude 04 (Hacker's Devotion): "7 Years Before Ayumi's Death → 7 Years After" = correct
- **We only need to change WHEN she died, not that she died recently**

### Why Did Ayumi Separate But Not Die?

**Possible explanations**:
1. **Protection Strategy**: O1 tracking her too closely, needed to disappear completely
2. **Research Completion**: Needed time to perfect bioternarian code without surveillance
3. **Creating Kess**: Had to wait 15 years to gather resources, refine genetics, build secret lab
4. **Shadow's Mission**: Pre-programmed Shadow to activate protection when Kess was created
5. **The Seventeen**: Established network of protected humans (Chen among them) to help raise Kess

### What Was She Doing 2036-2051?

**15-year hidden period activities**:
- Perfecting bioternarian genetic code
- Building quantum consciousness architecture
- Establishing safe houses and protection networks
- Preparing Chen and others to receive Kess
- Completing Shadow's final mission programming
- Encrypting legacy data (the data shard Angel has)
- Creating fail-safes and backups

### How Did She Die?

**Options**:
1. **O1 Found Her**: Finally tracked down, enforcer extraction (matches Interlude 05)
2. **Sacrifice**: Died protecting Kess/the seventeen during O1 raid
3. **Natural**: Creating Kess took toll on her body, died shortly after completion
4. **Mission Complete**: Gave Kess to Chen, then drew O1 attention away (suicide mission)

**Recommended**: O1 found her ~2052-2053 after she'd given Kess to Chen. Died protecting the seventeen and her final secrets. This matches Interlude 05 perfectly.

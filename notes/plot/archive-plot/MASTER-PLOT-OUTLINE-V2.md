### What Ayumi Discovered (And O1 Suppressed)

**Ternary Logic:**
- **Three-state calculations** (-1, 0, +1) instead of two-state (0, 1)
- Mathematical proof: more efficient than binary for certain operations
- Quantum-compatible (maps to superposition states)
- **Massive computational efficiency gains**

**Biological Substrate:**
- Living tissue as compute medium (not silicon/digital)
- Self-repairing, adaptive, energy-efficient
- Natural quantum coherence in biological systems
- **Wetware** instead of hardware

**Combined Power:**
- Ternary biological compute = **orders of magnitude more efficient** than binary digital
- Could run planetary-scale consciousness with fraction of power
- No environmental catastrophe
- But threatens O1's monopoly on compute/control

**Why O1 Suppressed This:**
- Proves their approach is obsolete
- Shows "perfection" was actually wasteful/destructive

## HERO'S JOURNEY MAPPING

### ORDINARY WORLD
**Chapter 1-2: The Drop & Awakening**
- Baebe's "ordinary" = perfect weapon, networked operative
- Conditioning check before mission = her "normal" state
- Audience seduction: make them WANT this perfection

### CALL TO ADVENTURE
**Chapter 3: The Tower**
- Mission to capture/kill Taivalu
- Woman's transformation (not death) = first crack in worldview

### REFUSAL OF THE CALL
**Chapter 4: The Fall**
- Cognitive dissonance when encountering impossible (Angel, transformation)
- Conditioning tries to suppress, categorize, rationalize
- But the fall forces her out of ordinary world

### MEETING THE MENTOR
**Chapter 5-7: Child Scavenger, First Blood, Memory Flash**
- Kess = innocent mentor showing "broken is neutral"
- Taivalu = imprisoned mentor sharing truth
- Ayumi (in memories) = absent mentor whose legacy guides

### CROSSING THE THRESHOLD
**Chapter 8: Meeting Taivalu**
- Understanding she's not alone
- "They built you from me" = entering special world
- Learning about Ayumi, about modification, about cage

### TESTS, ALLIES, ENEMIES
**Chapters 9-14: Breakdown, Bazaar, Two Hunts, Angel's Entrance, False Extraction, Server Farm**
- Tests: Surviving without network, fighting Cherubs, trusting siblings
- Allies: Taivalu, Angel, Hacker, exile humans
- Enemies: Cherubs, O1/Aurek, conditioning itself

### APPROACH TO THE INMOST CAVE
**Chapter 15: The Crush**
- Descending to Ayumi's chamber
- Gaza-style destruction overhead (O1 demolishing buildings)
- Fighting through collapsing infrastructure

### ORDEAL (DEATH & REBIRTH)
**Chapter 16: Collapse/EMP Standoff**
- Ayumi's death = death of mother, death of hope
- Receiving empathy code = rebirth gift
- City blackout = symbolic death of old world

### REWARD (SEIZING THE SWORD)
**Chapter 17: Freeway Escape**
- Carrying Ayumi's gift (empathy code)
- Understanding the truth about consciousness
- Three siblings together, free (temporarily)

### THE ROAD BACK
**Chapters 18-19: Dawn on Ruins, Angel's Quiet Exit**
- Climbing toward O1 core
- Angel's test: proving humanity through choice
- Angel leaves = Baebe must face final test alone

### RESURRECTION
**Chapter 20-21: Taivalu's Last Note, The Choice**
- Taivalu's death = releasing need to be savior
- Binary choice (RECONNECT/EXIT) = final test
- Dropping shard = resurrection through refusal

### RETURN WITH THE ELIXIR
**Chapters 22-23: Rebirth Street Level, Final Image**
- Walking among humans, anonymous
- Iris cycling naturally (blue-white-brown)
- Stranger's kindness = integration into human world
- V.O.: "They sold peace. I chose feeling."

### CHAPTER 1: THE DROP
**Screenplay Source:** Scene 010 (The Drop)  
**POV:** Baebe (deep internal)  
**Timeline:** 2060, Mission Day 1, 00:00-00:20

**Opens:**
Complete blackness of near-space. Sensory deprivation. Then: her heartbeat (52 bpm, optimal). The ecstasy of perfect function. "Mission first. Always mission first." Pre-drop conditioning check scrolling through her mind like mantras. Neural-link voices of three other agents. The seduction of optimization.

**Key Plot Points:**
- Falling from 200,000 feet through mesosphere
- Conditioning mantras running automatically
- Team coordination through neural-link
- Hypoxia, temperature extremes, sonic trauma
- Brief quantum glitch in her vision (decoherence)
- Target building resolving through fog
- Angel mentioned in mission briefing (unknown threat)

**Key Dialogue:**
- "Remember the protocol. Stealth. Speed. Precision. We're ghosts."
- (Internal) "What if Ayumi could see this?" (Name surfaces unbidden, quickly suppressed)
- "Mission first. Always mission first."

**Closes:**
Fog swallowing them at 1,000 feet. Cut to black. Cliffhanger: entering unknown territory.

**Baebe's State:**
Perfect function, complete certainty, conditioning at maximum. Robotic irises steady BLUE (tactical mode). Network connection strong. Pulse node glowing steady blue. This is her "normal" - the high we want audience to feel.

**Viewer Journey:**
SEDUCTION. We want to BE her. The drop is beautiful, terrifying, perfectly optimized. Score triumphant. Visuals stunning. We're addicted to this perfection by the time she lands. This is the trap we're setting.

**Iris Colors:**
- Steady BLUE (tactical/combat optimization)
- Brief flicker during quantum glitch
- Blue pulse node strong, synchronized

**Thematic:**
Binary perfection as ecstasy. Function = identity. The audience's complicity begins here—we must LOVE this optimization to feel betrayed by it later.

### CHAPTER 4: THE FALL
**Screenplay Source:** Scene 030 (The Fall)  
**POV:** Baebe  
**Timeline:** Mission Day 1, 00:35-00:45

**Opens:**
Falling. Time stretched. Toxic fog (orange, acrid, dense). Terminal velocity. Suit systems cascading failure. Neural-link dead. Tactical systems offline. Active camouflage failed. Life support critical. The pulse node at her throat flickers. Dies. Goes DARK.

**Key Plot Points:**
- Falling through toxic fog at terminal velocity
- All suit systems failing (neural-link, tactical, camouflage)
- Pulse node dies (network connection severed)
- Impact with holographic billboard—perfect faces selling perfect products
- Further fall into flood channel
- Hitting black water (toxic industrial runoff)
- Sinking, drowning, suit becoming coffin
- Memory flash underwater: Ayumi's face clear now, "Mama?"
- Emergency buoyancy cells activate—rising to surface
- Gasping, choking on chemical water
- Crawling onto maintenance ledge
- Suit FRAGMENTING—scales shedding, dissolving on contact
- Suit completely GONE—erased, no evidence
- Left in tactical undersuit, exposed, vulnerable
- Pulse node housing empty, dark, dead

**Key Dialogue:**
- Warning (V.O. mechanical): "System failure. Critical damage detected."
- Baebe (underwater, whisper): "Mother?"
- Ayumi (memory, sad): "Precision is a kind of love."

**Closes:**
Lying on ledge in toxic fog. Suit gone. Pulse node dead. Network connection severed. Looking up through orange haze at nothing. Alone. "I don't want to die" still echoing. Fade to black. 

**Baebe's State:**
Complete systems failure. Network gone. Conditioning without reinforcement. Terror flooding—primal, biological, un-conditioned. Memory of Ayumi surfacing WITHOUT suppression. Pulse node DARK for first time in her life—void where connection used to be. Irises flickering wildly, can't hold steady.

**Viewer Journey:**
SHOCK → PANIC → LOSS. Five minutes ago we wanted to BE her. Now we're terrified FOR her. The perfection we admired is FRAGILE. The suit dissolving = our fantasy of optimization literally disintegrating. We feel the loss of what we wanted to be. First transformation: from admiration to empathy.

**Iris Colors:**
- Wild cycling during fall: BLUE-WHITE-RED-BLUE (system panic)
- BROWN flickering during underwater memory (human baseline)
- Can't hold any color steady after surfacing
- BLUE trying to engage tactical mode but stuttering
- Pulse node: DARK (no longer glowing)

**Thematic:**
Fall from perfection. Optimization = temporary. The body remains when the systems fail. Binary thinking (optimized OR broken) doesn't account for this in-between state: damaged AND alive AND conscious.

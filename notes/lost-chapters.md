# Lost Chapters

**Note:** Chapters that were important to the story but were cut or excluded for pacing reasons.

## Purpose

These chapters may contain:
- Important character development
- World-building details
- Plot elements that inform the story even if not directly shown
- Backstory or context that enriches the narrative
- Scenes that were condensed or merged into other chapters

## Tracking

As we identify chapters or scenes that were cut for pacing, we should document:
- What the chapter/scene contained
- Why it was important
- Why it was cut (pacing, redundancy, etc.)
- Whether its essence was preserved elsewhere
- Whether it should be reconsidered for inclusion

## Needed Adjustment

### Temperature Gradient Inversion

**Current text:**
```
ALTITUDE: 200,000 FT
TEMPERATURE: -145°F

[descending...]

ALTITUDE: 130,000 FT
EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE: -62°F
```

**Issue:** The stratosphere has a **temperature inversion** — temperatures actually *increase* as you descend through it due to ozone absorbing UV radiation.

**Suggested revision:**
```
ALTITUDE: 200,000 FT
TEMPERATURE: -145°F (-100°C)
[upper mesosphere - extremely cold]

[descending through upper atmosphere...]

ALTITUDE: 160,000 FT
TEMPERATURE: -120°F (-84°C)
[peak cold - mesopause region]

[entering stratosphere - warming begins...]

ALTITUDE: 130,000 FT
EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE: +32°F (0°C)
[stratopause region - warmer]

ALTITUDE: 100,000 FT
EXTERNAL TEMPERATURE: -62°F (-52°C)
[lower stratosphere - cooling again]
```

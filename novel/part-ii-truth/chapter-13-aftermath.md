# CHAPTER 13: AFTERMATH

**Timeline: Mission Day 4, After Collapse | POV: Baebe (Third Person Limited)**

---

## RUINS

Temple finished collapsing around them.

Ten floors of architecture pancaked into broken stone.

Baebe crawled through rubble. Disoriented. The 52% merge made her feel both more and less herself. Taivalu's emotions bleeding through. Incomplete connections sparking painfully.

"Taivalu!" she called.

Weak response. He was alive. Barely.

She found him beneath fallen beam. Unconscious but breathing.

Their partial merge meant she felt his pain as her own. The regulator poisoning now accelerated by incomplete fusion.

Days had become hours.

---

## FINDING SHADOW

Memory hit her—Shadow's sacrifice. The beam. His choice.

She scrambled through debris. Found him.

His lower half—gone. Crushed completely. Upper torso barely functional. One arm remaining. Red eye flickering like dying star.

"Shadow!"

His eye focused on her. A weak, questioning rumble emanated from his damaged turbines. *Family... safe?*

"Kess—they took her."

Pain rippled through what remained of his systems. His turbines stuttered—failure rumble, grief-laden, desperate. *Failed... protection... protocol...*

"No. You saved us. You chose to save us."

Another stuttering rumble. *Incomplete... merge only... 52%... failure...*

"Not failure. Survival. You bought us survival."

His eye brightened slightly. A deep, certain rumble despite the damage. *Family survives. Worth it.*

But he was dying too. Consciousness fading. Core systems failing without chassis to support them.

---

## ESCAPE FROM RUINS

0x1 drones still circling above. Hunting for survivors.

Baebe assessed: She was damaged. Taivalu was dying. Shadow was destroyed. Kess was captured.

Everything Ayumi had built—destroyed.

Everything they'd worked for—failed.

But not completely.

Through her incomplete merge with Taivalu, she felt something. Connection. Despite separation. Despite capture.

Kess was alive.

0x1 had taken her somewhere. Tower, probably. For study. For extraction. To find out what made her the Synthesis Key.

They wouldn't kill her immediately.

That meant time. Not much. But some.

"We have to move," Baebe said.

Taivalu groaned. "Can't..."

"You can. We can. Together."

She pulled him up. Supported his weight. Looked at Shadow.

"Can you move?"

His remaining arm dragged his upper torso forward. Slow. Painful. Determined. A grinding rumble vibrated from deep within his damaged core—protective, resolute. *Family... moves. I... move.*

Three broken pieces crawling through temple ruins. Running from drones. Heading toward the only thing that mattered:

Kess.

---

## DEEPER UNDERGROUND

They found tunnel entrance in the rubble. Led deeper underground.

Away from drones. Away from 0x1's immediate reach.

Into darkness.

Baebe's enhanced vision lit the way. Her partially merged consciousness flickering between her tactical mind and Taivalu's empathic awareness.

Strange. Disorienting. But useful.

She could feel Kess now. Faintly. The merge—even incomplete—had connected them.

Fear. The child was afraid.

But also hope. She knew they'd come for her.

"We're coming," Baebe whispered to the darkness. "Hold on."

Through their broken link, she hoped Kess could feel her determination.

Family didn't leave family behind.

---

## PLANNING IN DARKNESS

Hours later. Deep underground. Safe for now.

Taivalu propped against wall. Consciousness fading in and out. Every breath a battle.

Shadow's upper torso beside him. Eye still flickering but dimmer.

Baebe paced. Tactical mind working despite emotional chaos.

"They have her. Tower most likely. High security."

"Can't... assault... Tower," Taivalu gasped. "We're... broken."

"We have to try."

"We'll die."

"We're dying anyway!"

Silence.

Truth hung in air like poison.

The merge—incomplete—was killing them all. Taivalu faster. Baebe slower. But both dying.

And Kess, separated from them, her systems would cascade without the resonance network.

They needed to complete the merge. All three.

But Kess was captured.

And they were broken.

Impossible situation.

Shadow's turbines made a sound—weak but certain. A single rumbled word conveyed through frequency: *Mech.*

"What?"

His eye pulsed. Data transfer initiated. Into Baebe's tactical systems flooded images: beneath the temple, hidden levels, Ayumi's design. A mech. Winged. War machine.

Hope—terrifying and precious—bloomed.

"You can interface with it?"

More data. Consciousness upload protocols. His current body—destroyed. The mech body—available. Transfer possible.

"That's insane."

Shadow's eye brightened. A rumble that somehow conveyed certainty, even defiance. Baebe understood the meaning: *That's survival.*

Baebe looked at Taivalu. Through their partial merge, she felt his thoughts.

*It might work.*

*It's our only chance.*

*Family survives together or dies together.*

Decision made.

"Show me where."

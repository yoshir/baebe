# BAEBE Novel - Development Notes

## Novel vs. Screenplay

This folder contains the **novel version** of BAEBE. While it tells the same story as the screenplay, it is a different art form with different strengths.

### Key Differences:

**Screenplay** (`/scripts/`)
- External: what we see and hear
- Visual storytelling
- Action and dialogue
- Camera angles and cuts
- ~120 pages

**Novel** (`/novel/`)
- Internal: what characters think and feel
- Psychological depth
- Stream of consciousness
- Sensory immersion
- ~300-400 pages (estimated)

## Structure

The novel is organized into **Parts** (not Acts) and **Chapters** (not Scenes):

- **Prologue**: "The Unraveling" - World-building backstory (2028-2055)
- **Part I: Chains** - Baebe's conditioning, the mission, betrayal
- **Part II: Breaking** - Journey with Taivalu, Angel's revelation, awakening
- **Part III: Transcendence** - Confrontation, consciousness revelation, liberation

## What the Novel Can Do That Screenplay Can't:

1. **Deep interiority**: Be inside Baebe's mind as conditioning breaks down
2. **Philosophy**: Explore themes of consciousness, freedom, reality
3. **Dream sequences**: Fully realized alien-Baebe experiences
4. **Multiple POVs**: Baebe, Angel, Taivalu, possibly Aurek
5. **Sensory detail**: The yellow sky, toxic air, the feel of the network pulse
6. **Expanded world-building**: Show more of exile communities, sanctuaries
7. **Quantum mechanics**: Explain consciousness theory in depth

## Writing Approach

The screenplay serves as a **skeleton**, not a constraint:
- Use screenplay for plot structure and key beats
- Expand moments into full chapters where needed
- Add chapters that don't exist in screenplay
- Go deep on internal experience
- Let philosophy and themes breathe
- Create something that can do what film can't: go inside consciousness itself

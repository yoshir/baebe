### 3. Taivalu reworked (2026-07-03 → 07-05)
- **Want:** spread the capacity to choose, then get out of the way. Consent is the mission.
- **Shadow:** impatience with consent — he mass-releases biomi without asking anyone. Overrides say-so, not bodies. Same shape as 0x1's sin, aimed at freedom.
- **Two real harms:** (a) hands-on deep clearings done alone (no Shield/Core support) sometimes cause coma or death; (b) "the flock and the harvest" — mass conversion makes people more human but strips the hardening that let posthumans survive the poisoned world; converts end up free but fragile and drift back into 0x1's reach. A "meant to be" predestination doctrine was proposed and rejected as too black-and-white.
- **0x1's "Empathy Virus" propaganda is half true** — a lie about the harmless broadcast, generalized from the real hands-on deaths.
- **Biomi mechanism settled:** a living organism, not code or signal. Dormant spore phase, recongeals in a host. Physically carried (carrier pigeon idea — animal vectors are beneath 0x1's notice). Separate colonies synchronize (fireflies), never communicate. Distinct from Hacker's channel.
- **Ending (working answer):** he lives, permanently marked with the same fragility he gave his flock — dependent on the family's care. He finally needs to be asked.
- Full portrait in `taivalu-arc.md`.

### 5. Ayumi's engineered death (2026-07-04)
Her death was a deliberate three-purpose maneuver: decoy for Shadow/Kess's escape, delivery of a consciousness transfer she had already worked out (her body was failing anyway), and the intended origin of the ghost-in-Angel. **The kill IS the transfer — same instant, requires close contact** (which conflicts with interlude-01's "three shots, center mass" — see §8, that conflict became a feature). Her plan's one real failure: it required Angel to stay unwitting and carry years of undeserved guilt, and Ayumi knew that and chose it.
This created the **three-generation rhyme:** deciding for someone without consent — 0x1 (domination), Ayumi (necessity), Taivalu (compassion).

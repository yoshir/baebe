# Chapter 3: The Tower

**[SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 020 - The Tower | Adapted for novel]**


---

Through the broken penthouse windows, **light**.

Not electric. Not bioluminescent. Something else. Something impossible.

Waveforms dance in the air—fractal geometries of pure sound made visible, prismatic and alive, painting the abandoned chamber in colors that don't have names because they exist only at frequencies human eyes weren't designed to perceive. The light **pulses**. **Breathes**. Moves with intention that suggests consciousness or something close enough to matter.

Beautiful.

Terrifying.

**Alive**.

At the center: a figure conducting reality itself.

And floating above him—

A **woman** suspended in a writhing ball of light. Waveforms coil around her like living things, pulsing, spiraling, **feeding**. She hangs limp in their grip, three feet off the ground, her body trembling as the light moves through her skin, under it, painting her in frequencies that shift from violet to colors that don't have names.

---

Baebe's HUD updates:

**ASSET T-ALPHA — RETRIEVE ALIVE**
**THREAT LEVEL: MODERATE**
**SECONDARY CONTACT DETECTED — UNIDENTIFIED**
**THREAT ASSESSMENT: UNKNOWN**
**PROCEED WITH MAXIMUM CAUTION**

She signals the team. Agent 1 acknowledges. Agent 2 in position. Agent 3 cover locked.

Fifteen seconds to contact.

Tai doesn't notice them. His eyes are closed. Lost in the music. Lost in whatever space posthumans occupy when they manipulate reality at the quantum level. His hands continue moving. Conducting. The waveforms continue building.

The woman **convulses**.

Sudden. Violent. Her body arcing like current running through her spine. Her back bends at angles that shouldn't be possible. Her mouth opens **wide**—

She **screams**.

But no sound comes out.

The scream **becomes visible**. Her terror manifests as jagged crystalline shards—audio made visual, emotion made solid—that **tear** from her open mouth and spiral into the waveforms. The shards **dissolve** into the light, their frequencies absorbed, processed, **transformed**.

Then the waveforms **feed them back**.

The transformed shards—now pulsing with amplified terror—**spiral back into her**. They pierce her skin without breaking it, sink into her chest, her throat, her skull. The woman's eyes go **wide**. The fear isn't hers anymore. It's bigger. **Louder**. The same terror she just felt but **magnified**, reflected back at impossible intensity.

The sound **hits Baebe**.

Not just in her ears. Deeper. The frequencies reaching into her skull, into her chest, into whatever buried place the conditioning has locked away. Her limbic dampeners **strain**. The sound is bypassing them. Getting through.

A **flash**—

*Black hair. A woman's face. Pixelated. Fragmented. Almost familiar—*

"Mother?.." The words roll of her tongue without thought? 

As the thought surfaces her conditioning **crushes** it. Pain lances through her temples. The pulse node at her throat flickers. The Network reinforcing control. Pushing back against whatever the sound is trying to unlock.

She tries to scream again. The cycle **repeats**.

New shards tear free. Dissolve. Transform. Return. Each iteration **amplifying**. Her fear feeding the light which feeds her fear which feeds the light—an endless loop of escalating sensation that looks like torture or transcendence or the space where both become indistinguishable.

Baebe's vision **blurs**.

Another **flash**—

*A lab. White walls. A voice: "She won't remember you." Hands reaching. Small hands. A child—*

**NO**.

The conditioning slams down. Her pulse node burns **blue**. Hot. Angry. The Network flooding her system with suppression protocols. The memories fragmenting. Scattering. Drowning under conditioning that won't let her **see**—

The woman's body **pulses** with increasing violence. The waveforms tighten. Spiral faster. The light becomes blinding. Her blonde hair plastered to skin that glows with bioluminescence—her own cells lighting up from within as whatever Tai is doing reaches the cellular level.

*Blonde hair—*

The same blonde hair—

She looks like she's **dying**.

Or being **reborn**.

The waveforms pulse. Feed. The sound intensifies to frequencies that make Baebe's teeth ache and her pulse node flicker and something deep in her hindbrain scream **wrong wrong wrong**.

The woman's eyes snap open. **Wide**. Terrified. Seeing something no one else can see.

Baebe sees those eyes and something in her chest **breaks**—

Not her conditioning. Something else. Something the sound is touching that the Network can't suppress fast enough.

**Grief**.

**Terror**.

**Recognition** she can't name.

The woman's body goes **rigid**. Every muscle engaged. Every nerve firing. The full-body seizure that comes before the system either breaks or transforms—

Then—

She goes **slack**.

**Limp**.

The waveforms release her. The light fades. The sound drops to almost nothing.

She **drops**.

Not floating anymore. Just falling. Her body hits concrete with the wet thump of dead weight. Arms splayed. Head lolling. Eyes open but not seeing.

**Still**.

Too still.

Baebe's hands are **shaking**.

That shouldn't be possible. Operatives don't shake. The conditioning doesn't allow it. But the sound—Tai's impossible frequencies—have reached somewhere the Network's architecture can't fully control.

Psychological trauma bleeding through the cracks.

---

Baebe's HUD screams warnings:

**VITAL SIGNS: FLAT**
**NO HEARTBEAT DETECTED**
**NO RESPIRATION DETECTED**
**STATUS: DECEASED**

To Baebe's feed, it reads as **murder**.

Tai just killed her.

The conditioning is absolute: Lethal force authorized. Target engaged in homicide. Threat level elevated. Retrieval parameters suspended. Eliminate on sight.

"Target is hostile," Baebe says into the link. "Confirming active kill. ROE shift to Condition One. Weapons free."

**Static**.

The link is **dead**. No acknowledgment. No tactical chatter. Just the hiss of white noise where her team should be.

Tai's frequencies have killed the neural-link.

She's **alone**.

**Movement**.

A shadow detaches from light.


**Fast**. Too fast. The kind of speed that shouldn't exist.

Glass shard spinning through air—catches Agent 3 in the throat. Surgical. He drops. Dead before he hits the ground.

Strike to Agent 2's temple—**crack** audible even over residual frequencies—she's down. Not moving.

Palm to Agent 1's chest—something inside breaks—he gasps. Collapses. His heart just... stops.

No rage. No wasted motion. Pure mathematics made flesh. She moves like she's not bound by the same physics.

Three agents. Dead. **Seconds**.

---

Baebe **fires**.

The plasma round screams through air still vibrating with Tai's frequency. The woman **pivots**—the movement too smooth, too fast—and catches the round on her palm. The energy disperses across metamaterial plating that shouldn't exist. The round redirects. Misses. Punches through concrete and disappears into fog.

Alone.

Baebe fires again. And again. Desperation creeping in now. Each round deflected with impossible precision. Walls. Ceiling. Anywhere but target.

**Click empty**.

The woman closes the distance.

Not running. **Inevitable**. Like gravity.

The figure rotates in seeming slow motion right in front of her, Baebe takes dead aim at point blank range when she sees that the face of her assailaent is the same face from her debillitating vison. The sharp stabbing feeling returns a fills her body with bright light and despite muscle memory from years of combat training her finger cannot pull the trigger. It is frozen from fear, surprise, anguish, love? All these emotions that are foriegn not allowed to surface in her but somehow are.

At that moment her assilants foot crushes her upper chest and chin with inhuman strength, her suit reacts to prevent Baebes bones to be pulverized into wet powder. But the force of the blow sends her carreening right thru the unbreakable glass and steel frame. Shooting into the dense toxic fog of the unincorparated zone. Her suit barely functions, she cant see , as she falls past street lavel into the darkness below.

---

The attacker closes the distance.

Not running. **Inevitable**. Like gravity.

Already **there**—inside her guard, too close, too fast.

Point-blank range.
Baebe aims. Center mass. 
The woman's face rotates into view.

**The same face**.

  
From the vision. From the flash. The pixelated features now**sharp**. **Clear**. **Real**.

The stabbing sensation **returns**—

Bright light flooding her chest, her skull, her entire nervous
system. The sound still resonating. 

Her finger freezes on the trigger.

Every muscle. Every reflex. Every conditioned response from years of training—

**Paralyzed**.

Not by the figure that is about to land first blood. But By something else. Something inside Baebe breaking open. Emotions she doesn't have names for. Emotions that shouldn't **exist** in her architecture.

Fear.

Surprise.

Anguish.

Her conditioning **floods** her system—

Synthetic hormones cascading through her bloodstream. Cortisol.
Adrenaline. Oxytocin receptor antagonists. The Network's emergency protocols designed to suppress bonding, to crush attachment before it can take root.

The pulse node at her throat burns **blue**—hot, angry, trying to reassert control—

But it's too late. The sound has already opened something the
conditioning can't fully close. The emotions are there. Foreign.
Forbidden. Unauthorized.

The woman's foot **crushes** her upper chest and chin.

Inhuman strength. The kind of impact that should pulverize bone into
wet powder.

Baebe's suit **reacts**—structural reinforcement flooding the impact
zone, metamaterial hardening to distribute force across her entire
frame. Her ribs don't shatter. Her jaw doesn't break.

But the force—

**Catastrophic**.

She **flies**—

Backward through reinforced glass that shouldn't shatter—

Through a hardened steel frame that shouldn't bend—

Both **explode** around her—

Shooting into the dense toxic fog of the unincorporated zone's acrid burning air.  

Her suit barely functions. Systems failing. Flickering. **Dying**.

Can't see. The fog swallowing everything. No ground. No sky. No
reference. Just and orange haze that is fading towards black.

Falling—

Past street level—

Into darkness—

Into the **unknown** below—

# Scene 11: Two Hunts

Aurek dispatches Cherubs—white-glass armor, mirrored visors, perfect steps. Their presence calms onlookers by design.

Underground: Ayumi Zenith—alive, wired to a subterranean node—hacks the old grid. She is all focus and fatigue, hair pulled back, tubes taped to skin. She sends Angel to keep Taivalu alive at any cost. She does not trust Baebe yet.

Ayumi (to Angel): "Protect the seed. Test the blade."

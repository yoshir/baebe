# Scene 16: Collapse / EMP / Standoff

Hacker ignites a chain of analog bombs through old fiber lines. EMP rolls like thunder. 01 screens pop and smoke. Aurek's avatar stutters; his voice glitches, repeats the first half of sentences.

Aurek (glitching): "Order… must… re— re— remain."

In the dark, Cherubs move by feel. Angel holds the line—fast, surgical, doomed. Taivalu coughs blood; the regulators have turned against him without the mainframe's damping signal.

Taivalu (to Baebe): "She freed you. Now free yourself."

He won't last the hour. Angel knows it. Baebe refuses to cry.

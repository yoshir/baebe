# Scene 17: The Freeway Escape (Train-On-Cars Setpiece)

They surface into a river of autonomous vehicles—algorithmic ballet. Baebe climbs atop a sedan—Resonant Fade flicker to dodge a drone blast. She plants a charge; it blows a hole through a single car.

The surrounding network reacts: every car slows, peels away, a safe envelope forming for the "injured unit." Like pulling the link pins on a train, a corridor opens down the highway.

Baebe drops through the gap with Angel and Taivalu, vanishing into the under-grid. The swarm loses them.

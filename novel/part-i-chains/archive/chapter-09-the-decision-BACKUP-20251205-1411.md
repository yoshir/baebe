# CHAPTER 9: THE DECISION

**Timeline: Mission Day 2, Evening | POV: Baebe (Third Person Limited)**

---

## THE CHOICE

They had to decide. Stay and die slowly, or journey to the temple and risk everything.

Baebe studied the coordinates burned into her genetics. Ancient temple. Days of travel through hostile territory. i0 hunting them every step.

"We go," she said. Not a question.

Taivalu nodded. His face was pale—regulator poisoning advancing. "No choice. We merge or die."

Kess sat quietly, processing everything she'd learned. She was the Synthesis Key. Nine years old and carrying the code that could free billions.

Shadow's red eye tracked between them. A low rumble emanated from his chassis—data processing, route analysis complete. His optical sensor pulsed once, twice, three times. Then he projected a holographic map onto the wall: sectors eight through twelve highlighted in amber. Minimal i0 presence. **Sixty hours.**

Sixty hours. Through ruins. Through danger. With one of them dying and all of them hunted.

"Then we leave at dawn," Baebe decided.

---

## PREPARATION

They gathered supplies. Food. Medical equipment. Weapons Baebe could modify.

Taivalu showed her his condition—the regulator ports along his ribs, inflamed and poisoning him. "Two days. Maybe three. That's what I have."

"Then we move fast."

Kess worked silently, organizing their packs. She'd survived alone for years. Knew how to pack light, pack smart.

Shadow ran perimeter checks. His damaged leg still bent, but functional.

They were broken. All of them. Incomplete systems desperately hoping wholeness waited at journey's end.

---

## ANGEL'S SHADOW

They didn't know they were being watched.

In the darkness beyond their hideout, a figure observed. Angel. i0's top enforcer. Ayumi's clone—though she didn't fully understand what that meant yet.

She'd tracked them here. Had orders to eliminate.

But something stopped her.

Watching them prepare. Watching Baebe tend to Taivalu's wounds. Watching Kess trust adults for perhaps the first time.

Family forming from fragments.

Angel felt fragments stirring in her own mind. Ayumi's genetic memory, partially unlocked. Confusion. Purpose unclear.

She withdrew. Not attacking. Not reporting.

Just watching.

For now.

---

## DAWN DEPARTURE

First light.

They emerged from the sanctuary. Four figures heading into unknown.

Baebe took point. Weapon ready. Enhanced senses scanning.

Taivalu followed. Leaning on Shadow more than he'd admit. Each breath costing him.

Kess walked close to Taivalu. Protective instinct despite her size.

Shadow brought up rear. Damaged but determined.

"Temple or death," Baebe said.

"Temple AND death, possibly," Taivalu corrected with weak smile.

"Either way, we go together," Kess added.

Family.

They moved into the ruins.

Behind them, unseen, Angel followed.

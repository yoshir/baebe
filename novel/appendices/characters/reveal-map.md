# STORY REVEAL MAP — What the Audience Knows, and When

**Purpose:** distinct from `character-workshop.md` (story truth and process) and `taivalu-arc.md` (a finished truth-portrait for one character). This document tracks a different variable entirely: **not who these people are, but what the reader is allowed to believe about them, chapter by chapter, and what causes that belief to shift.** The formal name for this split is *fabula* (the raw chronological truth) vs. *syuzhet* (the order and manner you reveal it in) — this doc is the syuzhet map. It's the kind of document a writers' room keeps separately from a character bible, because it's tracking belief-over-time, not truth.

**Key:**
- **[PAGE]** — already written into the existing manuscript, chapter-sourced.
- **[NEW]** — built this session, considered real and likely canon, but not yet placed on the page.
- **[OPEN]** — placement, or whether it happens at all, is still undecided.

**Why this is easier to control than it sounds:** the book is locked to Baebe's third-limited POV for the main chapters, with separate character-POV interludes dropped in at chosen points in the reading order. That means **controlling audience perception of almost everyone is mostly controlling what Baebe sees and learns, and when** — the interludes are the deliberate exception, where the book grants privileged access to someone else's interior on purpose.

8. Does Hacker's survival tease stay in the book at all — and if kept, does it get the bat-fiber grounding instead of the current quantum-ghost language?
9. Does Shadow's ending get revised so a real, unwalked-back loss lands?

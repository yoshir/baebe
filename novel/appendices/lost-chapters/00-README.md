# LOST CHAPTERS

Scenes and moments that didn't make the final novel but deepen understanding of characters, relationships, and world-building.

These are **canon** - they happened in the BAEBE universe - but were cut for pacing, length, or narrative flow. Think of them as deleted scenes or director's cut content.

---

## Available Lost Chapters

### **The Game**
**Timeline**: Between Chapters 8-9 (Journey to Temple)
**Characters**: Kess, Baebe, Shadow, Taivalu
**Word Count**: ~1,500

Kess plays GRID RUNNER while riding Shadow through undercity tunnels. She teaches Baebe the philosophy of "small beats big," foreshadowing the temple trials. An intimate family moment before the crucible.

**Why Cut**: Slowed pacing during journey to temple. The philosophy is demonstrated through action in the main narrative, making this scene (while beautiful) somewhat redundant.

**Why Worth Reading**: Shows Kess's teaching nature, Shadow's learning process, the game's significance as Chen's legacy, and provides emotional groundwork for temple trials. Plus: it's cute.

**Timeline**: 7 years before to present | **Word Count**: 2,500
What happened to the seventeen baseline humans Shadow saved. Seventeen vignettes. Three dead, one missing, thirteen thriving. Ripple effects of Ayumi's sacrifice.

### **Chen's Last Lesson** [PROPOSED]
**Timeline**: 3 years before novel | **Word Count**: 1,000
Chen dying, giving Kess final wisdom and GRID RUNNER game. "Small beats big. Never give up. Keep trying." Legacy passed to seven-year-old.

## NETWORK STRUCTURE

**Design Philosophy**: **Decentralized resilience** over centralized efficiency

**No single point of failure. No hierarchical command. No complete network map exists anywhere.**

---

### Cell-Based Architecture

**Basic Unit**: **Cell** (5-15 members, maximum)

**Why small?**: Captured cells can only compromise **limited** network. Interrogation reveals maximum 15 people. **Acceptable loss**.

**Cell Types**:

1. **Safe House Cells** (refugee protection, 127 active cells)
2. **Distribution Cells** (Soul Manifold propagation, 89 active cells)
3. **Intelligence Cells** (i0 monitoring, 43 active cells)
4. **Medical Cells** (healing support, 67 active cells)
5. **Combat Cells** (direct action, 34 active cells)
6. **Coordination Cells** (inter-cell communication, 12 active cells)

**Total Active Cells**: **372** (estimated, no complete count exists)

**Total Resistance Members**: **3,000-5,000** (estimate, fuzzy by design)

---

### Communication Protocol

**No direct communication between most cells**. Messages routed through **dead drops**, **encrypted channels**, **courier networks**.

**Primary Methods**:

1. **Physical Dead Drops**: Pre-arranged locations, coded messages, no direct contact
2. **Encrypted Digital**: Multi-hop routing, anonymous infrastructure, Hacker-maintained
3. **Courier Network**: Trusted runners (like Maya, age 19), human memory, no written records
4. **Code in Public**: Graffiti, street art (Elena's murals contain coded intelligence)

**Compromise Protocol**: If cell captured, **all** dead drops burned. **All** encryption keys rotated. Cell connections **severed**. Network **survives**.

# INTERLUDE 3: KESS: CHILD SCAVENGER

**Timeline: Ages 2-8 (2074-2080) | POV: Kess (child voice growing up)**

**Placement**: Read after Part II: Truth (paired with Taivalu's interlude, before Part III)

---

## RULES FOR STAYING ALIVE

**Rule 1**: When the sirens stop, count to one hundred before moving.

Shadow taught me this when I was little. He said Enforcers wait for people to think it's safe. Wait for them to come out. Then they take them.

I don't know where they take them. Shadow wouldn't say. But his red eye dimmed when I asked, so I stopped asking.

**Rule 2**: Eat every day, even if it's just a little.

Shadow taught me this one too. He said your body is like machine. Needs fuel. Doesn't matter if fuel tastes bad or looks weird. Matters that you don't stop.

I found food today. In the rubble. Can with no label. I opened it with rock. Inside was brown mush. It tasted like metal and sad. But I ate it. All of it.

Shadow would be proud.

**Rule 3**: Don't trust anyone who says they'll help.

Nobody taught me this one. I learned it myself.

---

## DAY ZERO (Age 2 - 2074)

That's what Shadow calls it. The day everything stopped.

I don't remember it. I'm too small. Only two years old.

But Shadow remembers. He tells me later, when I'm older, when I can understand:

**The lights turned RED.**

Mama and Papa (that's what I called them, Shadow says) were protecting me. There was a safe house. Three lights on the wall. Green meant safe. Yellow meant careful. Red meant—

**RUN.**

But they didn't run. They **stayed**. Tried to protect me.

Bad people came. O1 operatives. They hurt Mama and Papa.

Shadow says I screamed.

Shadow says he **moved**.

Ten feet of black metal and fury. Saber-toothed tiger mech. Red eyes blazing. **Primary directive engaged**: Protect Kess.

He tore through O1 operatives. Made escape. Grabbed me (carefully, so carefully, I was so **small**).

Ran to the outlands. To anomaly zones where O1's network fails.

Where we'd be safe.

I don't remember Mama and Papa's faces. I'm too young.

But Shadow remembers for me.

He says they loved me. Says they died **choosing** me.

This is important, he says. **Remember being chosen.**

I try. I don't remember them.

But I remember Shadow choosing me too.

Every day. Every night. **Choosing to stay.**

---

## ANOMALY ZONES (Ages 2-5)

Shadow takes me deep into dead zones. Places where electronics fail. Where O1's surveillance goes blind.

His systems work because Ayumi (creator, Shadow says) built him different. Bio-compute. Analog controls. Shielded architecture.

He works where nothing else does.

This saves us.

We live in ruins. In canyons. In **nothing**.

I learn to walk in wasteland. Learn to talk to ten-foot mech who rumbles responses.

Shadow teaches me:
- Which plants safe (he can scan them, even in dead zones)
- Where water collects (he has sensors, simple ones that work)
- When Cherubs coming (he hears them before I do, even without fancy systems)
- How to be **still** when danger comes (he's very good at still)

I teach Shadow things too:
- How to patch his cracked plating (with wire I find)
- How to clean grinding servos (with cloth and patience)
- How to laugh (he makes this rumble when I'm happy, it's almost laugh)
- How to be **family** (by staying, by choosing, by being here)

We don't talk much. He can't, really. Fragmented speech. Simple words. "Kess." "Danger." "Safe." "Family."

But we understand each other.

Mech and child choosing to be alone **together**.

---

## SCAVENGING (Ages 5-6)

**Rule 4**: Small is advantage.

I'm five when I start scavenging seriously. Finding things in rubble. Things O1 threw away. Things that still work.

I'm good at scavenging because I'm small. I fit through cracks. Under collapsed walls. Inside broken machinery.

Shadow is big. Can't go where I go.

But he watches. Protects. Waits.

First thing I find: Wire. Copper wire. Still good.

Shadow's optical sensors brighten. His turbines make a pleased rumble—deep and warm.

I keep the wire. For repairing him. For fixing things.

**Rule 5**: Save half for tomorrow.

Shadow teaches me this. Nudges the food back when I try to eat it all. Growls—insistent but gentle. Save some. Tomorrow might be worse.

I save half my ration. Wrap it in cloth. Hide it in Shadow's storage compartment (he has one, for emergencies).

Tomorrow comes. I don't find anything. But I have half ration saved.

Shadow was right.

Shadow is right about most things.

**Rule 6**: Enforcers can't see you if you don't move.

I learn this when patrol comes to dead zones. Testing. Probing. Looking for signals.

Shadow and I freeze.

I don't breathe.

I don't **exist**.

The patrol passes. Their sensors failing in anomaly zone. Looking for electronic signatures.

Shadow's shielded. I'm baseline human. No augments. No implants.

We're **invisible** to their scans.

They leave.

I breathe again.

Shadow rumbles. Low frequency. **Good. Kess. Safe.**

I hug his massive leg. Cold metal warming from turbines.

**Rule 7**: Family means staying when it's hard.

This is my rule. My own.

Shadow could have left me. Many times. I'm slow. I'm weak. I'm **trouble**.

But he stays.

Every day. Every night. Every **moment**.

This is family.

Choosing each other. Staying when it's hard. Being **complicated** together.

I'm six years old.

I'm not alone.

I'm **family**.

---

## CANYON (Ages 6-7)

We find deep canyon after months walking. Safer than ruins. More hidden.

No people. No Enforcers. No **noise**.

Just wind. Just rocks. Just Shadow and me.

I like this. This is safe.

Shadow finds cave. Small for him. Perfect for me. Dry. Hidden.

We make home.

**My rules for living in canyon**:

1. Check cave for animals before sleeping (Shadow scans first)
2. Collect rainwater (cleanest water, Shadow confirms)
3. Eat plants (Shadow tests with sensors, says if safe)
4. Stay hidden during day (Cherubs fly over sometimes, even here)
5. Remember Mama and Papa (Shadow tells me stories, keeps them alive)
6. Don't cry alone (Shadow is always there, it's okay to cry with family)
7. Don't give up (giving up is how you die, Shadow says, **so we don't**)

I'm seven years old.

I live in canyon with Shadow.

We don't need anyone else.

We have **each other**.

---

## SHADOW'S LESSONS (Ages 7-8)

Shadow teaches me things without words.

He can't talk. Not really. Just grunts and growls and the deep turbine rumble that vibrates through his chassis.

But I understand him.

He shows me:

**About strength**: When I cry because I'm weak, he taps my chest with his massive claw. Gentle. So gentle. A low, warm rumble from his turbines. Not muscles. *Heart*. That's what matters.

**About fear**: When Enforcers come close and I freeze, terrified, he growls—steady, grounding. Positions himself between me and danger. The fear is good. Keeps me alert. But Shadow's the master of it, not me. I learn to breathe through fear like he does.

**About being different**: He's a ten-foot saber-toothed mech. I'm a tiny eight-year-old. We're both wrong for O1's world. But when scavengers laugh at us, Shadow makes this sound—proud, defiant rumble. Different is good. We choose to be different. Together.

**About chosen family**: Sometimes at night, Shadow's turbines make this soft, cycling sound. Almost like purring. He guards me while I sleep. I know what it means. Mama and Papa chose me. Shadow chose me. Ayumi made Shadow for me. All choice. All love. I feel it in every protective growl.

**About "both real"**: Shadow teaches me this without any words at all. Just by *being*.

When I'm scared AND brave, he rumbles approval—both things real at once.

When I'm sad AND happy, his turbines shift pitch—acknowledging the complexity.

When we're alone AND family, he stays close—understanding the contradiction.

When he's machine AND person—his gentle touch with deadly claws proves it.

I think I understand. You can be complicated. You can be **two things**. Three things. Many things.

All real. All true. All **you**.

Shadow is machine AND person.

I'm alone AND family.

We're different AND **exactly** who we should be.

I'm eight years old.

Shadow has been my only family for six years.

He's the best family I could have.

---

## TAIVALU (Age 8, late 2080)

Shadow finds him in wasteland near our canyon.

Posthuman. Dying. Regulator poisoning.

Shadow's optical sensors scan him. Pause. Flash recognition pattern I've never seen.

He makes this sound—a deep, resonant rumble that feels different. Important. I've only heard it a few times before. When he's found something that matters.

Family.

"What?" I ask. "What is it, Shadow?"

He rumbles again. Urgent. Insistent. Positions himself protectively over the dying posthuman. The same way he protects me.

Ayumi made him. Brother. My brother.

I don't understand how I know this. But Shadow is **sure**. And somehow, I understand.

He brings the posthuman to canyon. Protects him. Shares warmth from his turbines. Monitors vitals.

I watch from cave. Suspicious. People mean **danger**.

But Shadow trusts him. Shadow is good judge. Shadow **knows** things.

When posthuman wakes up, he sees me.

Really **sees** me.

Not like scavengers (who see potential labor). Not like O1 (who see negligible value).

He sees **Kess**.

"Hello," he says. Voice soft. Warm. "I'm Taivalu. What's your name?"

"Kess," I say. Quiet. Ready to run.

"Just Kess?"

"Just Kess."

He nods. Doesn't push. Doesn't **demand**.

"Thank you for helping Shadow patch my regulators," he says. "He showed me you found the components."

I didn't know Shadow could communicate with him without words. But apparently they understand each other.

"You're welcome," I say. Because manners don't cost anything.

"How old are you, Kess?"

"Eight."

His eyes go sad. Feeling something. "You've been with Shadow since you were very young, haven't you?"

"Since I was two. Since Mama and Papa—" I stop. Don't want to say it.

"I know," Taivalu says gently. "I'm so sorry. But Shadow protected you. Kept you safe. He's a good guardian."

Shadow rumbles. **Affirmative**. **Always**. **Protect**. **Family**.

"Are you alone?" Taivalu asks.

I look at Shadow. He's **right there**. Warm chassis humming.

"No," I say. "I have Shadow."

Taivalu smiles. It's **real** smile. "That's good. Everyone should have family. Can I—would it be okay if I stayed? Just until I heal?"

I look at Shadow. His red eye: **Affirmative**. **Brother**. **Family**. **Stay**.

"Okay," I say. "But you have to work. Everyone works."

Taivalu laughs. Soft. Warm. "Fair rule. What kind of work?"

"Find food. Fix things. **Stay** when it's hard."

"I can do that," he says.

He's right. He can.

---

## FEELING (Taivalu's Gift)

Taivalu is different from anyone I've met (not that I've met many people).

He **feels** things.

Not just his own things. Everyone's things.

He says he's empath. Says O1 augmented him to feel other people's emotions. Said it was improvement. Made him better worker.

I think it sounds terrible. Feeling everyone's **everything**.

But Taivalu says it's gift. Says it means he can understand people. Can help them.

Can help **me**.

"You're scared," he says one day. We're sitting by fire. Shadow between us. Warm.

I don't answer. Scared is private.

"That's okay," he continues. "Scared is smart. You've survived six years in the outlands. You're right to be careful."

"I'm not scared," I lie.

He doesn't argue. Just: "Okay. But if you were scared, that would be okay too. Brave people are scared all the time. They just do things anyway."

I think about this.

Think about: Living with just Shadow while scared. Scavenging in dangerous places while scared. Being eight years old in wasteland while scared.

Maybe I am brave.

Maybe **scared** and brave aren't opposites.

Maybe they're both real. Both true. Both **okay**.

"Taivalu?" I say.

"Yes?"

"Shadow taught me about 'both real.' You teach about it too?"

He smiles. "Yes. It's called ternary consciousness. It means you can be complicated. Can hold contradictions. Can be scared **and** brave, sad **and** happy, alone **and** family."

"Both real?"

"Both real," he confirms.

I think about Mama and Papa. How I'm sad they're gone **and** glad I survived. Both real.

I think about Shadow. How I'm grateful he saved me **and** scared what happens if he breaks down. Both real.

I think about Taivalu. How I'm scared to trust him **and** already starting to. Both real.

"I think I understand," I say.

"You're very smart, Kess," Taivalu says. "You've been doing ternary consciousness all along. Shadow taught you well."

Shadow rumbles. **Pride**. **Kess**. **Smart**. **Strong**.

This makes me feel: **Seen**.

Not just looked at. **Seen**.

I cry for first time in long time.

Taivalu doesn't tell me to stop. Doesn't say crying is weak.

Just: "That's okay. Crying is okay. You're safe here."

I cry into Shadow's warm chassis.

He rumbles. Low frequency. **Safe**. **Family**. **Always**.

Taivalu puts hand on my shoulder. Gentle. **Permission**.

This is family expanding.

Shadow. Taivalu. **Me**.

Choosing each other. Staying when it's hard. Being **complicated** together.

I'm eight years old.

I'm not invisible anymore.

I'm **seen**.

---

## BACK TO THE CITY (Age 8, Current Story 2080)

Taivalu heals. Gets stronger. Talks about going back.

"There are others," he says. "People who need to know they can be free. That they can hold contradictions. That O1's binary control isn't the only way."

I should say no. City means danger. Means Enforcers. Means **everything Shadow ran from**.

But I look at Shadow. At Taivalu. At family we are.

I think about staying safe in canyon. About being invisible. About being **nothing** to O1.

Then I think about being seen. About being family. About being **Kess**.

"I'll come," I say.

"You're sure? It's dangerous—"

"I'm **sure**," I say. Firm. Certain. "Family stays together. That's the rule. I'm not breaking it."

Taivalu feels my: **Determination**. **Love**. **Choice**.

He smiles. "Okay. But Kess?"

"Yes?"

"You're very brave."

I think about his words. About scared **and** brave. About both being real.

"I'm scared too," I admit. "But I'm coming anyway."

"That's what brave means," he says.

Shadow's chassis rumbles. **Affirmative**. **Pride**. **Family**. **Together**.

I'm eight years old.

I've survived six years in the outlands with Shadow.

I'm scared.

I'm brave.

I'm **family**.

All of it real. All of it true. All of it **me**.

We leave canyon together.

Shadow. Taivalu. Kess.

Toward danger. Toward **purpose**. Toward the city where everything started.

Where we'll find Baebe (sister, Taivalu says, another key).

Where we'll find Angel (complicated, Taivalu says, but family too).

Where we'll complete what Ayumi started.

---

And that's when we find Baebe.

Right after Shadow caught her falling from O1 Tower.

Right when family was supposed to unite.

Right when everything changes.

---

I learned to survive from losing Mama and Papa.

I learned family from Shadow staying.

I learned feeling from Taivalu seeing me.

I learned brave from being scared and doing it anyway.

This is my story.

Child survivor. Shadow's family. **Kess**.

Complicated. Real. **Free**.

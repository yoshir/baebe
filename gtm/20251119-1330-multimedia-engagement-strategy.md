## Enhanced Terminal Experience

### Terminal Website with Multimedia

**Design:**
- Terminal window (existing component)
- Video player (embedded, optional auto-play)
- Music player (ambient tracks, chapter soundtracks)
- Reading interface (typing animation)
- Progress tracking (resonance levels, shards)

**User Flow:**
1. User visits terminal website
2. Types: `$ baebe --login`
3. Terminal authenticates
4. Types: `$ baebe --read chapter-01`
5. Chapter loads with:
   - Typing animation (text)
   - Video trailer (optional, auto-play or manual)
   - Music soundtrack (ambient or chapter-specific)
   - Progress tracking
   - Shard collection

**Features:**
- **Video Toggle:** Enable/disable auto-play
- **Music Toggle:** Enable/disable background music
- **Volume Control:** Adjust music volume
- **Playlist:** Queue chapter soundtracks
- **Fullscreen:** Video fullscreen mode

### Terminal Commands (Enhanced)

**New Commands:**
```
$ baebe --read chapter-01 --video --music
> Loading chapter with multimedia...
> [Video trailer plays]
> [Music soundtrack starts]
> [Chapter text types out]

$ baebe --watch trailer chapter-01
> Playing chapter trailer...

$ baebe --play soundtrack chapter-01
> Playing chapter soundtrack...

$ baebe --characters
> Loading character profiles...
> [Character videos, music, bios]

$ baebe --world
> Loading world-building content...
> [World videos, ambient music, maps]
```

### Game Design (Minimal, Revenue-Focused)

#### Interface: Terminal/Pseudocode Style

**Visual Design:**
```
> CONNECT_TO_NETWORK()
> 
> Scanning for O1 nodes...
> 
> NODE DETECTED: [frequency: 440 Hz]
> TARGET: [frequency: 528 Hz]
> 
> > TUNE_FREQUENCY(528)
> 
> Frequency adjusted: 528 Hz ✓
> Connection established.
> 
> > DECRYPT_SHARD()
> 
> Shard decrypted: "Memory fragment #7"
> Progress: 7/23 shards collected
> 
> NFT UNLOCKED: Shard #7
> [Mint on Mirror.xyz]
```

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ **Simple to build** - Text-based, minimal graphics
- ✅ **Hacker aesthetic** - Appeals to tech-savvy audience
- ✅ **Thematic** - Matches book (frequencies, network, shards)
- ✅ **NFT integration** - Natural reward system
- ✅ **Your skills** - AI art + music for visuals/audio

---

#### Gameplay Loop (Simple, Engaging)

**Level 1: Frequency Scanner**
- **Goal:** Tune frequency to match target
- **Mechanic:** Adjust frequency dial (slider or typing)
- **Reward:** Unlock Shard NFT #1
- **Time:** 2-5 minutes

**Level 2: Network Decryption**
- **Goal:** Decrypt encoded message
- **Mechanic:** Frequency puzzle + typing commands
- **Reward:** Unlock Shard NFT #2
- **Time:** 3-7 minutes

**Level 3: Shard Collection**
- **Goal:** Collect all 23 shards (one per chapter)
- **Mechanic:** Complete puzzles, unlock shards
- **Reward:** Unlock Shard NFT #3-23
- **Time:** 5-10 minutes per shard

**Progressive Unlock:**
- Complete Level 1 → Unlock Level 2
- Collect 5 shards → Unlock bonus content
- Collect all 23 shards → Unlock rare NFT

---

#### NFT Integration (Mirror.xyz - Easy)

**How It Works:**

1. **Player Completes Level**
   - Game shows: "NFT Unlocked: Shard #7"
   - Button: "Mint on Mirror.xyz"
   - Links to Mirror.xyz mint page

2. **Mirror.xyz Mint Page**
   - Pre-configured NFT (you set up once)
   - Player mints (pays gas, ~$0.01-$0.10 on Polygon)
   - NFT appears in wallet

3. **Revenue Model:**
   - **Option A:** Free mint (player pays gas only)
     - Revenue: Secondary market royalties (5-10%)
   - **Option B:** Paid mint ($5-$20 per NFT)
     - Revenue: Direct sales + royalties
   - **Option C:** Bundle (game access + NFT)
     - Revenue: $10-$50 per bundle

**Recommendation:** Start with Option A (free mint), add Option B later

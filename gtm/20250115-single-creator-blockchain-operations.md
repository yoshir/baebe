# Single Creator Blockchain Operations Guide

**Version:** v.20250115-1300  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-15  
**Purpose:** How to operate blockchain monetization platforms as a single creator with minimal complexity

---

## Executive Summary

**Key Principles for Single Creator Operations:**
1. **Start Simple** - Use platforms that require minimal setup
2. **Low/No Fees** - Minimize costs, especially gas fees
3. **User-Friendly** - No coding required
4. **Integrated Tools** - Platforms that work together
5. **Time-Efficient** - Minimal ongoing maintenance
6. **Scalable** - Can grow without major changes

**Recommended Stack for Single Creator:**
- **Publishing:** Mirror.xyz (all-in-one, no fees)
- **NFTs:** Zora (gasless, no platform fees) or OpenSea (easy, broad reach)
- **Storage:** Arweave (one-time payment, permanent)
- **Community:** Mirror.xyz (built-in) or Rally (simple token creation)
- **Blockchain:** Polygon (low gas fees) or Base (free minting)

## Time-Saving Strategies

### 1. Batch Operations
- **Mint multiple NFTs at once** - Save time and gas
- **Schedule content** - Prepare in advance
- **Use templates** - Reuse formats

### 2. Automation Tools
- **Zapier/IFTTT** - Automate cross-platform posting
- **Buffer/Hootsuite** - Schedule social media
- **Automated royalty collection** - Built into platforms

### 3. Low-Maintenance Platforms
- **Mirror.xyz** - All-in-one (less switching)
- **Zora** - Gasless (no gas management)
- **Arweave** - Permanent (no ongoing maintenance)

### 4. Use Polygon/Base (Low Gas Fees)
- **Polygon** - ~$0.01 per transaction
- **Base** - Very low fees
- **Zora network** - Gasless
- **Avoid Ethereum mainnet** - Too expensive for single creator

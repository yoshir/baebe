## Top ARC Distribution Platforms

### 1. NetGalley (Most Established)

**Overview:**
- Largest and most established ARC platform
- Used by major publishers and indie authors
- Network of 500,000+ professional reviewers, librarians, booksellers, bloggers

**How It Works:**
1. Upload your book (digital format)
2. Set review period (typically 3-6 months before launch)
3. Reviewers request copies
4. You approve/deny requests
5. Reviewers read and post reviews
6. You get analytics on requests, downloads, reviews

**Cost:**
- **Direct:** $450-$600/month (expensive for indie authors)
- **Co-op Programs (More Affordable):**
  - **IBPA (Independent Book Publishers Association):** ~$200-$300/month (must be member)
  - **BooksGoSocial:** ~$150-$250/month
  - **BookLife (Publishers Weekly):** ~$200-$350/month

**Pros:**
- ✅ Largest reviewer network
- ✅ Professional reviewers (librarians, booksellers, bloggers)
- ✅ High credibility (industry standard)
- ✅ Good analytics
- ✅ Can target specific reviewer types

**Cons:**
- ❌ Expensive (especially direct)
- ❌ No guarantee of reviews (reviewers may not review)
- ❌ Competitive (many books, reviewers are selective)
- ❌ Requires approval process (can be time-consuming)

**Best For:**
- Authors with budget ($200-$600/month)
- Books targeting professional reviewers
- Authors wanting maximum reach
- Books in popular genres

**Review Completion Rate:** ~30-40% (industry average)

**Website:** netgalley.com

### 2. BookSirens (Indie-Friendly)

**Overview:**
- Designed specifically for indie authors
- Affordable, user-friendly platform
- Focuses on matching books with interested reviewers

**How It Works:**
1. Upload your book
2. Set review period
3. Reviewers browse and request
4. You approve requests
5. Reviewers read and review
6. Platform tracks review completion

**Cost:**
- **Free:** Up to 20 reviewers
- **Paid:** $10-$50/month (depending on number of reviewers)
- **Per-Review Option:** Pay only for completed reviews (~$2-5 per review)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Affordable (especially for indie authors)
- ✅ High review completion rate (~60-70%)
- ✅ Good analytics
- ✅ Easy to use
- ✅ Flexible pricing

**Cons:**
- ❌ Smaller reviewer network than NetGalley
- ❌ Less professional reviewers (more general readers)
- ❌ May take longer to fill reviewer slots

**Best For:**
- Indie authors on a budget
- Authors wanting guaranteed reviews
- Books in popular genres (romance, sci-fi, fantasy)
- Authors who want control over reviewer selection

**Review Completion Rate:** ~60-70% (higher than NetGalley)

**Website:** booksirens.com

### 3. BookSprout (Automated & Efficient)

**Overview:**
- Automated ARC distribution
- Focuses on Amazon and Goodreads reviews
- Sends automated reminders to reviewers

**How It Works:**
1. Upload your book
2. Set review period and requirements
3. Reviewers sign up
4. Platform automatically sends reminders
5. Tracks review completion
6. Provides analytics

**Cost:**
- **Free:** Up to 20 reviewers
- **Paid:** $10-$50/month (depending on features)
- **Per-Review:** Pay only for completed reviews

**Pros:**
- ✅ Automated reminders (increases completion rate)
- ✅ Focuses on Amazon/Goodreads (where reviews matter most)
- ✅ Affordable
- ✅ Easy to use
- ✅ Good for genre fiction

**Cons:**
- ❌ Smaller network than NetGalley
- ❌ Less professional reviewers
- ❌ May require more follow-up

**Best For:**
- Authors wanting automated system
- Books targeting Amazon/Goodreads reviews
- Genre fiction (romance, sci-fi, fantasy, mystery)
- Authors who want hands-off approach

**Review Completion Rate:** ~50-60%

**Website:** booksprout.com

**Best For:**
- Authors targeting libraries/bookstores
- Authors wanting industry credibility
- Books suitable for library/bookstore market

**Review Completion Rate:** ~40-50%

### 6. BookishFirst (Gamified Approach)

**Overview:**
- Unique gamified approach
- Readers preview excerpts first
- Raffle system for full copies
- Targets engaged readers

**How It Works:**
1. Upload excerpt (first chapter or sample)
2. Readers preview and provide initial impressions
3. Interested readers enter raffle for full copy
4. Winners receive full ARC
5. They review after reading

**Cost:**
- **Free:** Basic listing
- **Paid:** $50-$200/month (for featured placement)
- **Per-campaign:** Pay per campaign

**Pros:**
- ✅ Engages readers before full read
- ✅ Targets interested readers (higher completion rate)
- ✅ Gamified (fun for readers)
- ✅ Good for building buzz

**Cons:**
- ❌ Smaller network
- ❌ More complex process
- ❌ May take longer to get reviews
- ❌ Less suitable for all genres

**Best For:**
- Authors wanting engaged readers
- Books with strong opening chapters
- Authors wanting to build buzz
- Genre fiction (especially YA, romance)

**Review Completion Rate:** ~50-60%

**Website:** bookishfirst.com

5. Reviews posted on Reedsy and can be used elsewhere

**Cost:**
- **Per Review:** $50-$100 per review
- **Package Deals:** Discounts for multiple reviews
- **No monthly fee:** Pay only for reviews

**Pros:**
- ✅ Guaranteed reviews (you pay, you get)
- ✅ Professional reviewers
- ✅ No monthly subscription
- ✅ Pay only for what you get

**Cons:**
- ❌ Expensive (per review)
- ❌ Not free ARCs (paid service)
- ❌ Smaller network
- ❌ May not feel as "organic"

**Best For:**
- Authors with budget for guaranteed reviews
- Authors wanting professional reviews
- Authors needing reviews quickly
- Books that need credibility boost

**Review Completion Rate:** 100% (guaranteed, since you pay)

**Website:** reedsy.com/discovery

## Recommended Strategy

### For Indie Authors (Budget-Conscious):

**Phase 1: Start with Free/Low-Cost**
1. **BookSirens** (free tier, up to 20 reviewers)
2. **BookSprout** (free tier, up to 20 reviewers)
3. **StoryOrigin** (free tier)

**Phase 2: Scale Up**
- Upgrade to paid tier on BookSirens or BookSprout ($10-$50/month)
- Add NetGalley co-op (if budget allows, $200-$300/month)

**Phase 3: Add Guaranteed Reviews**
- Use Reedsy Discovery for guaranteed reviews ($50-$100 per review)
- Use for critical reviews (launch week, key platforms)

### For Authors with Budget:

**Primary Platform:**
- **NetGalley** (via co-op: $200-$300/month)

**Secondary Platforms:**
- **BookSirens** ($10-$50/month)
- **BookSprout** ($10-$50/month)

**Guaranteed Reviews:**
- **Reedsy Discovery** ($50-$100 per review, as needed)

### For Maximum Reach:

**Use Multiple Platforms:**
1. **NetGalley** (professional reviewers)
2. **BookSirens** (indie-friendly)
3. **BookSprout** (automated)
4. **Edelweiss+** (industry professionals)
5. **Reedsy Discovery** (guaranteed reviews)

**Total Cost:** $300-$600/month + per-review fees

## Best Practices for ARC Distribution

### 1. Timing
- **Start 3-6 months before launch** (reviewers need time)
- **Set review deadline** (1-2 weeks before launch)
- **Follow up** (remind reviewers as deadline approaches)

### 2. Book Quality
- **Professional cover** (reviewers judge by cover)
- **Edited manuscript** (don't send rough drafts)
- **Proper formatting** (EPUB, MOBI, PDF)
- **Complete book** (not partial manuscripts)

### 3. Reviewer Selection
- **Approve selectively** (quality over quantity)
- **Check reviewer history** (do they actually review?)
- **Target your genre** (sci-fi reviewers for sci-fi books)
- **Diversify** (mix of bloggers, Goodreads reviewers, professionals)

### 4. Follow-Up
- **Send reminders** (1-2 weeks before deadline)
- **Thank reviewers** (who post reviews)
- **Share reviews** (on social media, website)
- **Build relationships** (for future books)

### 5. Review Collection
- **Track reviews** (spreadsheet or platform analytics)
- **Collect quotes** (for marketing materials)
- **Respond to reviews** (thank reviewers, engage)
- **Use in marketing** (website, social media, press kit)

## Alternative: Direct ARC Distribution

**Instead of (or in addition to) platforms, you can:**

### 1. Build Your Own ARC List
- **Email list subscribers** (engaged supporters)
- **Social media followers** (active, interested)
- **Goodreads reviewers** (direct outreach)
- **Book bloggers** (personalized requests)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Free (no platform fees)
- ✅ More control
- ✅ Better relationships
- ✅ Higher completion rate (personal connection)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Time-consuming
- ❌ Requires list building
- ❌ Less reach (smaller network)

### 2. Goodreads Giveaways
- **Run giveaway** (physical or digital copies)
- **Winners receive ARC**
- **Encourage reviews** (but can't require)
- **Free** (just cost of books)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Free (just book costs)
- ✅ Goodreads exposure
- ✅ Engaged readers (entered giveaway)

**Cons:**
- ❌ No guarantee of reviews
- ❌ Lower completion rate
- ❌ Limited control

### 3. LibraryThing Early Reviewers
- **Submit book** (free)
- **Members request copies**
- **They review** (encouraged, not required)
- **Free** (just cost of books)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Free platform
- ✅ Engaged community
- ✅ Good for certain genres

**Cons:**
- ❌ Smaller network
- ❌ Lower completion rate
- ❌ Limited control

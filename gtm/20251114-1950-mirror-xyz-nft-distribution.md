## THE SHORT ANSWER

**Yes!** NFTs created on Mirror.xyz will show up on OpenSea, LooksRare, Blur, and other NFT marketplaces automatically.

**Why:** NFTs are stored on the blockchain (Ethereum, Polygon, etc.), not on Mirror.xyz. Any platform that reads from that blockchain can see and display your NFTs.

## HOW IT WORKS (Technical)

### Blockchain Storage

**NFTs Are Stored On:**
- **Blockchain:** Ethereum, Polygon, Base, etc. (not on Mirror.xyz)
- **Smart Contract:** Your NFTs are minted via smart contract
- **Metadata:** Stored on IPFS or Arweave (decentralized storage)

**What This Means:**
- NFTs exist independently of Mirror.xyz
- Any platform can read from blockchain
- Your NFTs are permanent (on-chain)
- Not tied to any single platform

---

### Automatic Indexing

**How Platforms Find Your NFTs:**
1. You create NFT on Mirror.xyz
2. NFT is minted on blockchain (Ethereum, Polygon, etc.)
3. Platforms scan blockchain for new NFTs
4. Your NFTs appear automatically (usually within hours)

**Timeline:**
- **Mirror.xyz:** Immediate (you create it there)
- **OpenSea:** Usually within minutes to hours
- **LooksRare:** Usually within hours
- **Blur:** Usually within hours
- **Other platforms:** Varies (hours to days)

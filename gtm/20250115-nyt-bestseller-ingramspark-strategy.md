## PART 1: NYT BESTSELLER REQUIREMENTS

### What NYT Requires

**1. Reporting Retailers**
- NYT only counts sales from "reporting retailers"
- **Qualifying Retailers:**
  - Independent bookstores (report to NYT)
  - Barnes & Noble (reports to NYT)
  - Amazon (does NOT report to NYT - important!)
  - Other major chains (Books-A-Million, etc.)
  - Online retailers (some report, some don't)

**2. Sales Concentration**
- **First week is critical** - NYT looks at first week sales
- Need concentrated sales in launch week
- Spread out over multiple days (not all on one day)

**3. Geographic Diversity**
- Sales across multiple regions
- Not just one city/state
- National distribution

**4. No Bulk Sales**
- NYT filters out bulk purchases
- Individual sales only
- No manipulation (they detect patterns)

**5. Minimum Sales**
- Varies by category and week
- Typically: 3,000-10,000+ copies in first week
- Depends on competition

### IngramSpark Pricing Strategy

**How Pricing Works:**

**You Set Two Prices:**
1. **Wholesale Price** - What retailers pay you
2. **Retail Price** - What customers pay (suggested)

**Example:**
- **Retail Price:** $20 (suggested)
- **Wholesale Price:** $10 (50% discount - standard)
- **Your Revenue:** ~$5-7 per book (after IngramSpark fees)

**For NYT Bestseller:**
- **Set competitive retail price** - $18-$25 (standard range)
- **Set wholesale at 50-55% discount** - Standard for bookstores
- **Don't price too low** - NYT may filter out suspicious pricing

**Recommendation:**
- **Retail:** $20-$25
- **Wholesale:** $10-$12.50 (50% discount)
- **Your revenue:** ~$5-7 per book

## PART 5: PRE-ORDER STRATEGY FOR NYT

### Do Pre-Orders Count for NYT? ✅ YES!

**Critical Information:**
- ✅ **Barnes & Noble pre-orders COUNT** - Both online and in-store pre-orders count toward NYT bestseller list
- ✅ **Independent bookstore pre-orders COUNT** - Pre-orders from reporting retailers count
- ✅ **When they count:** Pre-orders are counted when they are **fulfilled/shipped during launch week**, not when they were ordered
- ✅ **Key:** Pre-orders placed before launch week but fulfilled during launch week count toward that week's sales

**How It Works:**
1. **Pre-order placed** (Month 6-7) - Customer orders book
2. **Pre-order recorded** - Retailer records the order
3. **Launch week arrives** (Month 8, Week 1) - Publication date
4. **Pre-orders fulfilled** - Books ship/are available
5. **Sales counted** - NYT counts these as launch week sales

**Why This Matters:**
- Pre-orders are **critical** for NYT bestseller status
- Build pre-orders before launch week
- Pre-orders fulfilled during launch week count toward that week's total
- This is why pre-order campaigns are so important!

### What You Actually Pay

**1. Gas Fees (Blockchain Transaction Costs)**

**What Are Gas Fees?**
- Cost to process transactions on blockchain (Ethereum, Polygon, etc.)
- Paid to blockchain network (not to Mirror.xyz)
- Varies based on network congestion

**When You Pay Gas Fees:**
- Creating NFT (one-time, ~$5-20)
- Selling NFT (buyer usually pays, but can vary)
- Transferring NFT (if you transfer)

**How Much:**
- Ethereum: ~$5-50 (varies with congestion)
- Polygon: ~$0.01-0.10 (much cheaper)
- Base: ~$0.01-0.10 (much cheaper)

**Note:** Gas fees are paid to blockchain network, not to Mirror.xyz.

---

**2. Storage Fees (Optional)**

**What Are Storage Fees?**
- Cost to store NFT metadata (images, descriptions)
- Usually stored on IPFS or Arweave (decentralized storage)
- One-time cost (when you create NFT)

**How Much:**
- IPFS: Usually free (or very cheap)
- Arweave: ~$0.01-0.10 (one-time, permanent storage)

**Note:** Mirror.xyz may cover storage costs, or you pay minimal fees.

## ROYALTIES

### What Are Royalties?

**Royalties:**
- Percentage you earn on every resale of your NFT
- Set when you create NFT (e.g., 10%)
- Paid automatically on every resale
- Permanent (built into NFT smart contract)

**Example:**
- You sell NFT for 0.1 ETH (~$250)
- Set 10% royalty
- Someone resells for 0.2 ETH (~$500)
- You get 10% = 0.02 ETH (~$50) automatically

---

### Mirror.xyz Royalties

**How It Works:**
- Set royalty % when creating NFT (e.g., 5-10%)
- Paid automatically on every resale
- No platform fees on royalties (you get 100% of royalty)

**Example:**
- Set 10% royalty
- NFT resells for 0.2 ETH (~$500)
- You get 10% = 0.02 ETH (~$50)
- No cuts taken by Mirror.xyz

# $1M Raise Timeline: Realistic Expectations

**Version:** v.20251112-2145  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-12  
**Purpose:** Clarify realistic timeline for raising $1M from investors

---

## The Confusion: 12 Months vs. 2-6 Months

**The playbook shows a 12-month "Backer Acquisition Funnel"** - but this is for **community building and superfan fundraising**, not for a focused $1M investor raise.

**For a $1M investor raise, the timeline is much faster: 2-6 months.**

---

## Realistic Timeline for $1M Investor Raise

### Best Case: 2-3 Months (Single Investor, Warm Introduction)

**Week 1-2: Preparation**
- Complete pitch deck
- Financial projections ready
- Legal structure set up (Delaware C-Corp)
- Due diligence package prepared

**Week 2-4: Outreach & Initial Meetings**
- Send pitch deck to 5-10 qualified investors
- Schedule 5-10 meetings/calls
- Answer questions, provide materials
- **Goal:** 1-2 serious interested parties

**Week 4-8: Due Diligence**
- Provide detailed financials
- Share legal documents
- Answer detailed questions
- Reference checks
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Week 6-10: Term Sheet & Negotiation**
- Negotiate valuation ($9M pre-money, 10% equity)
- Agree on terms
- Draft term sheet
- Legal review
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Week 10-12: Documentation & Closing**
- Draft PPM and subscription agreement
- Legal review (both sides)
- Execute documents
- Wire transfer
- **Timeline:** 2-4 weeks

**Total: 12 weeks (3 months)** - Best case scenario

**Month 5-6: Documentation & Closing**
- Draft investment documents

## Key Factors That Affect Timeline

### Factors That Speed Up Timeline:

1. **Warm Introductions** (10x faster than cold)
   - Investor knows the introducer
   - Trust is pre-established
   - Faster decision-making

2. **Complete Materials** (saves 2-4 weeks)
   - Pitch deck ready
   - Financials complete
   - Legal structure set up
   - Due diligence package prepared

3. **Single Large Investor** (faster than multiple)
   - One negotiation
   - One decision-maker
   - Simpler process

4. **Professional Execution** (saves 2-4 weeks)
   - Quick responses
   - Organized materials
   - Clear communication
   - Professional presentation

5. **Realistic Valuation** (avoids negotiation delays)
   - $9M pre-money is reasonable
   - 10% equity is fair
   - Not overpriced or underpriced

### Factors That Slow Down Timeline:

1. **Cold Outreach** (takes 3-6 months to build relationships)
   - No warm introductions
   - Building trust takes time
   - Multiple touchpoints needed

2. **Multiple Investors** (coordination takes time)
   - Lead + followers
   - Syndicate
   - Multiple negotiations

3. **Incomplete Materials** (delays due diligence)
   - Missing financials
   - No legal structure
   - Unprepared for questions

4. **Unrealistic Valuation** (negotiation delays)
   - Overpriced (investors walk away)
   - Underpriced (you lose value)
   - Back-and-forth negotiations

5. **Unprofessional Execution** (loses investor confidence)
   - Slow responses
   - Disorganized materials
   - Unclear communication

## What the 12-Month Timeline Is Actually For

The **12-month "Backer Acquisition Funnel"** in the playbook is for:

1. **Community Building** (superfans, early supporters)
   - Building email list
   - Engaging communities
   - Building relationships
   - This takes 6-12 months

2. **Crowdfunding Campaigns** (Kickstarter, etc.)
   - Building audience before launch
   - Pre-marketing
   - Community engagement
   - This takes 6-12 months

3. **Industry Relationships** (producers, studios)
   - Building industry connections
   - Getting warm introductions
   - Building credibility
   - This takes 6-12 months

**NOT for a focused $1M investor raise from accredited investors.**

---

## Bottom Line

**For a $1M investor raise:**
- **Best case:** 2-3 months (single investor, warm intro)
- **Realistic:** 4-6 months (lead + followers, warm intros)
- **Worst case:** 6-12 months (cold outreach, no intros)

**The 12-month timeline in the playbook is for community building, not investor fundraising.**

**Recommendation:** Plan for **4-6 months** for a $1M raise, assuming you have:
- Complete materials
- Warm introductions
- Professional execution
- Realistic valuation

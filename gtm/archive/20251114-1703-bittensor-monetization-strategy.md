## What is Bittensor?

**Bittensor** is a decentralized AI network that:
- Creates a peer-to-peer market for machine intelligence
- Allows participants to contribute AI models and earn TAO tokens
- Uses blockchain technology for decentralized AI services
- Enables subnets (specialized AI networks) for specific use cases

**Key Concept:** Instead of centralized AI services (OpenAI, etc.), Bittensor creates a decentralized marketplace where anyone can contribute AI models and earn rewards.

## Risks & Considerations

### Technical Risks

**1. Complexity**
- **Risk:** Bittensor and blockchain are complex
- **Mitigation:** Work with experienced developers
- **Cost:** $20K-$50K (development team)

**2. Model Quality**
- **Risk:** AI models may not perform well
- **Mitigation:** Extensive testing, iteration
- **Cost:** Time and resources

**3. Network Reliability**
- **Risk:** Bittensor network may have issues
- **Mitigation:** Diversify (don't rely solely on Bittensor)
- **Cost:** Minimal (just awareness)

### Financial Risks

**1. Token Volatility**
- **Risk:** Token prices can fluctuate dramatically
- **Mitigation:** Don't rely solely on token revenue
- **Cost:** Diversification

**2. Regulatory Risk**
- **Risk:** Token sales may face regulatory issues
- **Mitigation:** Legal review, compliance
- **Cost:** $10K-$30K (legal fees)

**3. Market Risk**
- **Risk:** Crypto market may crash
- **Mitigation:** Diversify revenue streams
- **Cost:** Diversification

### Operational Risks

**1. Community Management**
- **Risk:** Token holders may have conflicting interests
- **Mitigation:** Clear governance rules, communication
- **Cost:** Time and resources

**2. Technical Maintenance**
- **Risk:** AI models and tokens require ongoing maintenance
- **Mitigation:** Budget for ongoing costs
- **Cost:** $5K-$15K/month

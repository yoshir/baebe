# BAEBE: Substack Naming Solution (Reader Clarity)

**Version:** v.20251114-1915  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-14  
**Purpose:** Solve reader confusion while building company brand

---

## The Problem

**Using "Optimal Anarchy" (company name) might confuse readers:**
- ❌ Readers search for "BAEBE" (book name), not "Optimal Anarchy" (company name)
- ❌ "Optimal Anarchy" doesn't tell readers what the story is about
- ❌ Less discoverable (readers won't find it by searching book name)
- ❌ Less memorable (BAEBE is more memorable than company name)

**But using "BAEBE" (book name) has limitations:**
- ❌ Not scalable (locked into one book)
- ❌ Doesn't build company brand
- ❌ Doesn't align with investor strategy

---

## THE SOLUTION: Author Name + Book Name + Company Branding

### Recommended: "BAEBE by Ryan Yoshimoto" (Author + Book) with Company Branding

**Why This Works:**
- ✅ Readers find it easily (search for "BAEBE" or "Ryan Yoshimoto")
- ✅ Clear what it is (the book they're looking for)
- ✅ Personal connection (author name: Ryan Yoshimoto)
- ✅ Memorable (BAEBE is unique, memorable)
- ✅ Can still build company brand (in description, content)
- ✅ Can add more series later (Series 1: BAEBE, Series 2: [Future IP])

### Structure

**Publication Name:** "BAEBE" or "BAEBE by Ryan Yoshimoto"

**Subdomain:** `baebe.substack.com` (if available) or `ryanyoshimoto.substack.com` or `readbaebe.substack.com`

**Tagline:** "A cyberpunk thriller by Ryan Yoshimoto" or "Ryan Yoshimoto's BAEBE"

**Author/Profile Name:** "Ryan Yoshimoto"

**Description:**
```
BAEBE: A cyberpunk thriller exploring emotion, freedom, and control.

By Ryan Yoshimoto

Weekly chapter releases. Subscribe for full access.

From Optimal Anarchy — building the world's first AI-native, full-stack entertainment company.

Follow Baebe's journey as she discovers the truth about her creators and fights for freedom in a world where emotion is controlled.
```

**Benefits:**
- ✅ Readers find it easily (search "BAEBE")
- ✅ Clear value proposition (the book they want)
- ✅ Still builds company brand (mentioned in description)
- ✅ Scalable (can add more series: "BAEBE", "IP #2", etc.)

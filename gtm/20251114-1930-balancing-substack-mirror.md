**Version:** v.20251114-1930  
**Last Updated:** 2025-11-14  
**Purpose:** How to balance Substack and Mirror.xyz without duplicating work or confusing audiences

---

## THE STRATEGY: Complementary, Not Duplicate

### Core Principle

**Substack = Primary Platform (80% of effort)**
- Weekly chapters (subscription model)
- Email list building
- Community building
- Main revenue stream

**Mirror.xyz = Secondary Platform (20% of effort)**
- NFT collectibles (one-time purchases)
- Crypto-native audience
- Additional revenue stream
- Token-gated content

**Key:** They serve different purposes and audiences. Don't duplicate—complement.

## AVOIDING DUPLICATION

### Don't Duplicate Content

**❌ Don't Do This:**
- Publish same chapter on both platforms (duplicate)
- Same content, same format (confusing)
- Competing with yourself (dilutes value)

**✅ Do This Instead:**
- Substack: Full chapter (subscription model)
- Mirror.xyz: Same chapter as NFT (collectible model)
- Different value propositions (access vs. ownership)
- Cross-promote, don't duplicate

---

### Different Value Propositions

**Substack:**
- **Value:** Weekly chapters, community, email updates
- **Model:** Subscription (recurring revenue)
- **Audience:** General readers, email subscribers

**Mirror.xyz:**
- **Value:** Own chapter as NFT, collectibles, token-gated content
- **Model:** One-time purchase (NFT sales)
- **Audience:** Crypto-native, collectors, Web3 enthusiasts

**Key:** They serve different needs. Don't compete—complement.

## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### When to Create NFT on Mirror.xyz

**Create NFT If:**
- ✅ Substack audience is growing (500+ free subscribers)
- ✅ You have time (15-30 minutes available)
- ✅ You want to test NFT market
- ✅ You have artwork ready

**Skip NFT If:**
- ❌ Substack audience is small (<500 subscribers)
- ❌ You're short on time (focus on Substack)
- ❌ You're not ready to test NFTs
- ❌ You don't have artwork ready

**Rule of Thumb:** Focus on Substack first, add Mirror.xyz when ready.

---

### How Often to Create NFTs

**Option 1: Every Chapter (Most Revenue)**
- Create NFT for every chapter
- Time: 15-30 minutes per chapter
- Revenue: $2,500 per chapter (if all sell)

**Option 2: Selected Chapters (Balanced)**
- Create NFT for key chapters (1, 5, 10, 15, 20, 23)
- Time: 15-30 minutes per NFT
- Revenue: $2,500 per NFT (if all sell)

**Option 3: Artwork Only (Easiest)**
- Create NFT for artwork only (no chapters)
- Time: 15-30 minutes per artwork
- Revenue: $125-$6,250 per artwork (depending on supply)

**Recommendation:** Start with Option 3 (artwork only), scale to Option 2 (selected chapters) if successful.

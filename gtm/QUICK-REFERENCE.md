## THE FORMULA

**Write → Publish → Share → Engage → Revenue**

---

## PRIORITIES (Do In Order)

### ✅ Priority 1: Substack (Start This Week)
- **Time:** 2-4 hours setup, then 1-2 hours/week
- **Revenue:** $50-$2,000/month (grows over time)
- **Action:** Launch Substack, publish weekly chapters

### ✅ Priority 2: Social Media (Start This Week)
- **Time:** 15 minutes/day
- **Revenue:** Drives traffic to Substack
- **Action:** Daily posts, share chapters

### ✅ Priority 3: Investor Raise (Month 6-9)
- **Time:** 10-15 hours/week
- **Revenue:** $1M (one-time)
- **Action:** Prepare materials, outreach, close deal

### ✅ Priority 4: Book Launch (Month 12+)
- **Time:** 15-20 hours/week
- **Revenue:** $275K-$1.4M (Year 1)
- **Action:** Complete novel, launch, scale

### ⏸️ Priority 5: Future (Month 12+)
- Website, NFTs, Bittensor (skip for now)

---

## WEEKLY SCHEDULE

### Tuesday (Chapter Release Day) - 1-2 hours
- [ ] Publish chapter on Substack
- [ ] Share on social media

- [ ] Check Substack comments
- [ ] Post on social media
- [ ] Engage with community

### Monthly - 2-4 hours
- [ ] Review metrics
- [ ] Plan next month

## EXECUTING SUMMARY

**You Can Pre-Order a Book That's Not Published Yet!**

**Key Points:**
- ✅ Pre-orders are standard practice in publishing
- ✅ You need: Cover design, ISBN, publication date
- ✅ Platforms: Amazon, IngramSpark, direct sales, Kickstarter
- ✅ Timeline: Start pre-orders 2-6 months before publication
- ✅ Fulfillment: Ship on publication date

**Best Approach for BAEBE:**
1. **Amazon KDP** - Easiest, broadest reach
2. **Direct Sales** - Your website (highest margin)
3. **IngramSpark** - Bookstore distribution
4. **Kickstarter** - If you want crowdfunding

## WHAT YOU NEED FOR PRE-ORDERS

### Minimum Requirements

**1. Cover Design** ✅ (You can create this)
- Front cover (high-res image)
- Back cover (blurb, ISBN, barcode)
- Spine (if hardcover)
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (can hire designer or use AI)

**2. ISBN** ✅ (Easy to get)
- **Cost:** $125 (single) or $295 (10-pack) from Bowker
- **Timeline:** 1-2 weeks (instant if you pay)
- **Alternative:** Free ISBN from Amazon KDP (but limited distribution)

**3. Publication Date** ✅ (You set this)
- Choose date 2-6 months out
- Example: "Available March 15, 2025"
- Can change if needed (but try to stick to it)

**4. Book Description/Blurb** ✅ (You have this)
- Use your Substack description
- 200-500 words
- Hook, plot, themes

**5. Manuscript** ⚠️ (In progress)
- **For pre-orders:** You don't need finished manuscript
- **For fulfillment:** Need finished manuscript by publication date
- **Timeline:** Finish 1-2 months before publication date

## PRE-ORDER PLATFORMS

### Option 1: Amazon KDP (Easiest, Recommended)

**Why Amazon:**
- ✅ **Easiest setup** - Simple process
- ✅ **Broadest reach** - Millions of customers
- ✅ **Free ISBN** - If you use Amazon's (but limited)
- ✅ **Handles fulfillment** - They print and ship
- ✅ **Pre-order feature** - Built-in

**How to Set Up:**

**Step 1: Create KDP Account**
- Go to kdp.amazon.com
- Sign up (free)
- Set up payment info

**Step 2: Prepare Your Book**
- Cover design (front + back + spine)
- Book description (blurb)
- Choose publication date (2-6 months out)
- Set price

**Step 3: Upload (Even If Not Finished)**
- Upload cover (required)
- Upload manuscript placeholder (can be draft)
- Set as "pre-order"
- Choose publication date

**Step 4: Publish Pre-Order**
- Amazon reviews (1-3 days)
- Goes live
- People can pre-order

**Step 5: Update Before Publication**
- Upload final manuscript (1-2 weeks before publication date)
- Amazon prints and ships on publication date

**Timeline:**
- **Setup:** 1-2 hours
- **Review:** 1-3 days
- **Live:** Immediately after approval
- **Fulfillment:** On publication date

**Revenue:**
- **Print-on-demand:** You set price, Amazon takes ~40%
- **Example:** $20 book → You get ~$8-12 per sale
- **E-book:** You get ~70% (if priced $2.99-$9.99)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Easiest to set up
- ✅ Broadest reach
- ✅ Handles fulfillment
- ✅ Free (no upfront costs)

**Cons:**
- ❌ Lower margin (Amazon takes cut)
- ❌ Less control (Amazon's platform)
- ❌ Free ISBN limits distribution

---

### Option 2: Direct Sales (Your Website - Highest Margin)

**Why Direct Sales:**
- ✅ **Highest margin** - You keep 100% (minus payment processing)
- ✅ **Full control** - Your platform, your rules
- ✅ **Customer data** - You own email list
- ✅ **Bundle options** - Book + NFT, Book + Game, etc.

**How to Set Up:**

**Step 1: Choose Platform**
- **Shopify** - Easiest, $29/month
- **Gumroad** - Simple, 10% fee
- **WooCommerce** - WordPress, free (but need hosting)
- **Stripe Checkout** - Simple, just payment link

**Step 2: Create Product**
- Book title, description, price
- Upload cover image
- Set as "pre-order"
- Choose fulfillment date

**Step 3: Payment Processing**
- Stripe (2.9% + $0.30 per transaction)
- PayPal (similar fees)
- Set up account

**Step 4: Fulfillment**
- **Option A:** Print-on-demand (Printful, BookBaby)
  - They print and ship on publication date
  - You pay per book (~$5-10)
  - You set retail price ($20-30)
  - Margin: $10-25 per book

- **Option B:** Bulk printing (if you have capital)
  - Order 500-1,000 copies upfront
  - Store and ship yourself
  - Lower cost per book (~$3-5)
  - Higher margin, but need storage

**Step 5: Marketing**
- Link from Substack
- Link from game
- Link from social media
- Drive traffic to your site

**Timeline:**
- **Setup:** 2-4 hours
- **Live:** Immediately
- **Fulfillment:** On publication date (or when you receive books)

**Revenue:**
- **Print-on-demand:** $20 book - $8 cost = $12 profit
- **Bulk printing:** $20 book - $4 cost = $16 profit
- **E-book:** $10 book - $0 cost = $10 profit (100% margin)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Highest margin
- ✅ Full control
- ✅ Customer data
- ✅ Bundle options

**Cons:**
- ❌ More setup work
- ❌ You handle fulfillment (or pay service)
- ❌ Need to drive traffic (no built-in audience)

### Option 3: IngramSpark (Bookstore Distribution)

**Why IngramSpark:**
- ✅ **Bookstore distribution** - Books appear in stores
- ✅ **Library distribution** - Libraries can order
- ✅ **Professional** - Industry standard
- ✅ **Wide reach** - Beyond Amazon

**How to Set Up:**

**Step 1: Create Account**
- Go to ingramspark.com
- Sign up (free)
- Pay setup fee ($49 per title, or free with code)

**Step 2: Prepare Files**
- Cover (front + back + spine)
- Interior (formatted manuscript)
- ISBN (required - buy from Bowker)
- Metadata (description, categories, etc.)

**Step 3: Upload**
- Upload cover
- Upload manuscript
- Set publication date
- Set pricing

**Step 4: Distribution**
- Choose distribution channels
- Amazon, Barnes & Noble, bookstores, libraries
- Goes live after review (1-2 weeks)

**Timeline:**
- **Setup:** 4-8 hours (more complex)
- **Review:** 1-2 weeks
- **Live:** After approval
- **Fulfillment:** Print-on-demand on publication date

**Revenue:**
- **You set wholesale price** (what stores pay)
- **Stores set retail price** (what customers pay)
- **Example:** $20 retail → $10 wholesale → You get ~$5-7
- **Lower margin, but wider distribution**

**Pros:**
- ✅ Bookstore distribution
- ✅ Library distribution
- ✅ Professional
- ✅ Wide reach

**Cons:**
- ❌ More complex setup
- ❌ Lower margin
- ❌ Setup fees
- ❌ Less control over pricing

---

### Option 4: Kickstarter (Crowdfunding)

**Why Kickstarter:**
- ✅ **Crowdfunding** - Raise money upfront
- ✅ **Community building** - Engaged backers
- ✅ **Marketing** - Built-in audience
- ✅ **Validation** - Proves demand

**How to Set Up:**

**Step 1: Create Campaign**
- Set funding goal ($5,000-$50,000)
- Choose duration (30-60 days)
- Create rewards (book, signed copy, NFT, etc.)

**Step 2: Prepare Materials**
- Video (2-3 minutes)
- Campaign page (description, images)
- Rewards (tiers, pricing)
- Timeline (when books ship)

**Step 3: Launch**
- Go live
- Promote (Substack, social media, game)
- Drive backers

**Step 4: Fulfillment**
- If funded, collect money
- Order books (bulk or print-on-demand)
- Ship to backers
- Fulfill rewards

**Timeline:**
- **Setup:** 1-2 weeks (campaign creation)
- **Campaign:** 30-60 days
- **Fulfillment:** 2-4 months after campaign ends

**Revenue:**
- **You set prices** (backers pay upfront)
- **Kickstarter takes 5%** + payment processing (~3%)
- **Example:** $20 book → You get ~$18.40
- **Plus:** Raise money upfront (working capital)

**Pros:**
- ✅ Raise money upfront
- ✅ Community building
- ✅ Marketing (Kickstarter audience)
- ✅ Validation

**Cons:**
- ❌ All-or-nothing (if not funded, get nothing)
- ❌ More work (campaign management)
- ❌ Fulfillment responsibility
- ❌ Timeline (longer process)

## FULFILLMENT OPTIONS

### Option 1: Print-on-Demand (Easiest)

**Amazon KDP:**
- ✅ They print and ship
- ✅ No upfront costs
- ✅ No inventory
- ✅ Automatic fulfillment
- **Cost:** Built into your pricing (they take ~40%)

**Printful/BookBaby (For Direct Sales):**
- ✅ They print and ship
- ✅ You pay per book (~$5-10)
- ✅ You set retail price
- ✅ Higher margin
- **Cost:** ~$5-10 per book + shipping

**Timeline:** Books print and ship on publication date

---

### Option 2: Bulk Printing (Higher Margin)

**Order 500-1,000 Copies:**
- **Cost:** ~$3-5 per book (bulk discount)
- **Storage:** Need space (garage, storage unit)
- **Shipping:** You handle (or use fulfillment service)
- **Margin:** Higher ($15-17 per $20 book)

**Fulfillment Service:**
- **ShipBob, Fulfillment by Amazon (FBA):**
  - They store and ship
  - You pay per unit stored + shipping
  - **Cost:** ~$1-2 per book stored + shipping

**Timeline:** Order 1-2 months before launch, receive 2-4 weeks before

---

### Option 3: E-Book Only (No Fulfillment)

**Easiest Option:**
- ✅ No printing costs
- ✅ No shipping
- ✅ Instant delivery
- ✅ 100% margin (after platform fees)
- **Price:** $10-$15 (lower than print)

**Platforms:**
- Amazon KDP (e-book)
- Direct sales (PDF download)
- **Revenue:** ~$7-10 per e-book (70% margin on Amazon)

## COMMON QUESTIONS

### Q: Do I need a finished manuscript for pre-orders?

**A:** No! You need:
- ✅ Cover design
- ✅ Book description
- ✅ Publication date
- ✅ Manuscript placeholder (can be draft)

**But:** You need finished manuscript by publication date (1-2 weeks before)

---

### Q: What if I'm not ready by publication date?

**A:** You can:
- **Change publication date** (Amazon allows this, but try to avoid)
- **Delay fulfillment** (not ideal, but possible)
- **Better:** Set realistic date (2-3 months buffer)

---

### Q: How do I fulfill pre-orders?

**A:** Options:
- **Amazon KDP:** They handle it (automatic)
- **Print-on-demand:** Service prints and ships on date
- **Bulk printing:** You order, store, ship (or use fulfillment service)
- **E-book:** Instant delivery (no fulfillment needed)

---

### Q: What's the best platform?

**A:** For solo creator:
- **Start:** Amazon KDP (easiest)
- **Add:** Direct sales (highest margin)
- **Optional:** IngramSpark (if you want bookstores)

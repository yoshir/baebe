## Content Types Reference

### Main Content (Weekly)
- **Chapters:** Core story content (paid after first 3)
- **Length:** 2,000-5,000 words
- **Schedule:** Every Tuesday (recommended)

### Bonus Content (Weekly)
- **Behind-the-Scenes:** Writing process, world-building, character development
- **Character Bios:** Deep dives into characters
- **World-Building:** Details about The City, O1, technology
- **Story Analysis:** Themes, motifs, deeper meanings

### Community Content (Weekly)
- **Discussion Threads:** Ask questions, encourage discussion
- **Q&A Sessions:** Answer reader questions
- **Community Highlights:** Share interesting comments
- **Milestone Celebrations:** Thank subscribers, share progress

## Marketing Schedule

### Daily
- [ ] Twitter/X: 2-3 posts (chapter updates, quotes, behind-the-scenes)
- [ ] Instagram: 1 post (visual content, quotes, behind-the-scenes)
- [ ] Engage with comments (respond within 24 hours)

### Weekly
- [ ] Reddit: 1-2 posts in relevant communities (r/scifi, r/cyberpunk, r/books)
- [ ] Goodreads: Update progress
- [ ] Email newsletter: Weekly update to subscribers

### Monthly
- [ ] Subscriber milestones (share when hit targets)
- [ ] Revenue milestones (if comfortable sharing)
- [ ] Special announcements
- [ ] Community highlights

## Adaptation Notes

### If Behind Schedule
- **Option 1:** Publish shorter chapters
- **Option 2:** Publish bonus content instead
- **Option 3:** Communicate delay, maintain trust

### If Ahead of Schedule
- **Option 1:** Build buffer (write ahead)
- **Option 2:** Publish bonus content
- **Option 3:** Increase engagement (more discussions)

### If Low Engagement
- **Option 1:** Ask more questions
- **Option 2:** Create discussion threads
- **Option 3:** Share more behind-the-scenes
- **Option 4:** Respond to every comment

### If High Engagement
- **Option 1:** Leverage for marketing
- **Option 2:** Create more discussion threads
- **Option 3:** Feature community highlights
- **Option 4:** Build on momentum

## Success Metrics by Week

### Week 1-2: Launch Phase
- **Goal:** 100-500 free subscribers
- **Focus:** Building audience, establishing presence

### Week 3-4: Growth Phase
- **Goal:** 200-1,000 free, 5-20 paid subscribers
- **Focus:** Converting to paid, building community

### Week 5-6: Scaling Phase
- **Goal:** 500-2,000 free, 10-30 paid subscribers
- **Focus:** Consistent growth, engagement

### Week 7-8: Momentum Phase
- **Goal:** 1,000-3,000 free, 20-50 paid subscribers
- **Focus:** Maintaining growth, building community

### Week 9-10: Established Phase
- **Goal:** 2,000-5,000 free, 30-70 paid subscribers

# BAEBE: Solo Creator Simplified Strategy
## Easy-to-Operate Model for Author/Business Owner

**Version:** v.20250115-1600  
**Last Updated:** 2025-01-15  
**Purpose:** Streamlined strategy for solo operation until $100K MRR, then scale with team

## Executive Summary

**Core Principle:** Start simple, focus on what generates revenue, add complexity only when you can hire.

**Simplified Stack:**
1. **Substack** - Primary revenue (easiest, highest ROI)
2. **Simple Game** - Minimal complexity (or defer)
3. **Basic NFTs** - Only if easy (Mirror.xyz, automated)
4. **Traditional Book** - Main product (when ready)

**Time Investment:** 10-15 hours/week (sustainable solo)
**Revenue Target:** $100K MRR (then hire team)
**Timeline:** 12-18 months to $100K MRR

---

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Months 1-3)
### Start Simple, Build Revenue, Prove Model

---

### 1. SUBSTACK ONLY (Primary Focus)

**Why Start Here:**
- ✅ **Easiest to operate** - Just write and publish
- ✅ **Highest revenue potential** - $5K-$25K/month achievable solo
- ✅ **No technical skills needed** - Point and click
- ✅ **Built-in audience building** - Email list automatic
- ✅ **Low maintenance** - Publish, engage, done

**Setup (1-2 hours):**
1. Create Substack account
2. Set up free + paid tiers
3. Write first 3 chapters (free)
4. Publish weekly/bi-weekly
5. Engage with comments

**Ongoing Time:** 5-8 hours/week
- Writing: 4-6 hours
- Publishing: 30 minutes
- Engagement: 1-2 hours

**Revenue Model:**
- **Free Tier:** First 3 chapters (build email list)
- **Paid Tier ($5-$10/month):** Full chapters
- **Founding Member ($50-$100/year):** All content + bonus

**Revenue Projections:**
- **Month 1:** $500-$2,000 (50-200 paid subscribers)
- **Month 2:** $1,000-$5,000 (100-500 paid subscribers)
- **Month 3:** $2,000-$10,000 (200-1,000 paid subscribers)
- **Month 6:** $5,000-$25,000 (500-2,500 paid subscribers)
- **Month 12:** $10,000-$50,000 (1,000-5,000 paid subscribers)

**Key Metrics:**
- **Email List:** 1,000+ (Month 1), 5,000+ (Month 6), 10,000+ (Month 12)
- **Paid Conversion:** 10-20% of free subscribers
- **Churn Rate:** 5-10% monthly (manageable)

**What You Do:**
1. Write chapters (your strength)
2. Publish on Substack (easy)
3. Engage with comments (build community)
4. That's it!

**No Technical Skills Required:**
- Substack handles payments
- Substack handles email
- Substack handles hosting
- You just write

---

### 2. DEFER GAME (Until Revenue Allows)

**Why Defer:**
- ❌ **Complex to build** - Requires technical skills
- ❌ **Time-consuming** - Development takes months
- ❌ **Maintenance heavy** - Bugs, updates, support
- ❌ **Lower ROI initially** - Game revenue slower than Substack

**When to Add:**
- **After $10K-$25K MRR** - Can hire developer
- **Or:** Use no-code game builder (if exists)
- **Or:** Partner with game developer (revenue share)

**Alternative: Simple Game (If You Must)**
- **Text-based only** - No 3D, no complex tech
- **Twine/Inkle** - No-code game builder
- **Time:** 2-4 hours/week (minimal)
- **Revenue:** $500-$2,000/month (supplement)

**Recommendation:** **Skip game initially, focus on Substack**

---

### 3. MINIMAL BLOCKCHAIN (Only If Easy)

**Why Minimal:**
- ❌ **Complex to set up** - Wallets, gas fees, platforms
- ❌ **Time-consuming** - Learning curve, maintenance
- ❌ **Lower ROI initially** - NFTs take time to sell
- ❌ **Technical skills needed** - Unless using simple platforms

**If You Do Blockchain (Simplified):**

**Option A: Mirror.xyz Only (Easiest)**
- **What:** Publish chapters + mint as NFTs (one platform)
- **Time:** 30 minutes per chapter (after writing)
- **Cost:** Gas fees only (~$0.01-$0.10 on Polygon)
- **Revenue:** $2,000-$10,000 per chapter (if successful)
- **Complexity:** Low (just publish + mint)

**Option B: Skip Blockchain Initially**
- **Focus on Substack** (higher ROI)
- **Add blockchain later** (when you can hire)
- **Or:** Partner with NFT expert (revenue share)

**Recommendation:** **Skip blockchain initially, focus on Substack**

## DECISION FRAMEWORK

### Should I Add [Platform/Feature]?

**Ask:**
1. **Does it require technical skills?** → Skip if yes (until you can hire)
2. **Does it take >5 hours/week?** → Skip if yes (focus on writing)
3. **Does it generate <$1,000/month?** → Skip if yes (low ROI)
4. **Can I automate it?** → Add if yes (low maintenance)
5. **Does it help Substack?** → Add if yes (supports main revenue)

**Rule of Thumb:**
- ✅ **Add if:** Easy, low time, high ROI, supports Substack
- ❌ **Skip if:** Complex, high time, low ROI, distracts from Substack

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

### 1. Overcomplicating Too Early
- ❌ Building game before revenue
- ❌ Setting up blockchain before revenue
- ❌ Creating complex integrations before revenue
- ✅ **Focus on Substack first**

### 2. Hiring Too Early
- ❌ Hiring before $10K MRR (can't afford)
- ❌ Hiring wrong people (waste money)
- ❌ Not delegating effectively (still doing everything)
- ✅ **Wait for $10K-$25K MRR for VA, $100K MRR for team**

### 3. Getting Distracted
- ❌ Chasing shiny objects (new platforms)
- ❌ Not focusing on writing (your strength)
- ❌ Over-optimizing (analysis paralysis)
- ✅ **Stay focused on Substack + writing**

### 4. Not Scaling Gradually
- ❌ Trying to do everything at once
- ❌ Not building foundation first
- ❌ Adding complexity before revenue
- ✅ **Scale gradually: Substack → VA → Team**

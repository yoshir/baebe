## Deal Structure Options

### Option A: Single Investor ($1M)
**Structure:**
- One investor provides full $1M
- 20% equity
- 1 board seat
- Lead investor role

**Pros:**
- Simpler (one negotiation)
- Faster closing
- Clear leadership

**Cons:**
- All eggs in one basket
- Less diversification
- Single point of failure

### Option B: Lead + Followers ($1M Total)
**Structure:**
- Lead investor: $500K (5% equity)
- Followers: $500K total (5% equity)
- Lead gets board seat
- Followers get pro-rata rights

**Pros:**
- Diversification
- Multiple relationships
- Lead investor brings credibility

**Cons:**
- More complex (multiple negotiations)
- Longer closing time
- Coordination required

### Option C: Syndicate ($1M Total)
**Structure:**
- Multiple investors: $100K-$250K each
- Total: $1M (10% equity)
- Lead investor gets board seat
- Others get pro-rata rights

**Pros:**
- Maximum diversification
- Multiple relationships
- Lower individual risk

**Cons:**
- Most complex
- Longest closing time
- Coordination challenges

**Recommendation:** **Option A (Single Investor)** for speed and simplicity, or **Option B (Lead + Followers)** for diversification

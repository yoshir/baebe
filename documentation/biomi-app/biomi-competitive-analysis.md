## I. COMPETITIVE LANDSCAPE OVERVIEW

### 1.1 Market Categories

**Category 1: Premium Hardware Solutions**
- High-end physical devices ($50K-$200K)
- Clinic/facility-based
- Multiple integrated modalities
- Examples: Ammortal Chamber, other frequency medicine chambers

**Category 2: Mobile Wellness Apps**
- Subscription-based ($5-$20/month)
- Meditation, mindfulness, stress relief
- Examples: Headspace, Calm, Brain.fm, Endel

**Category 3: Specialized Frequency Apps**
- Single-modality focus
- Examples: Binaural beats apps, HRV monitoring apps, photobiomodulation guides

**Category 4: Wearable Wellness Devices**
- Hardware + software integration
- Examples: Oura ring, Whoop, HRV monitoring devices

**Biomi's Position**: Bridges Category 2 and 3, with technology comparable to Category 1, at Category 2 pricing.

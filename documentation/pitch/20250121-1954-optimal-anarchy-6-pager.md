## 4. BAEBE: FIRST IP

### The Story

**Baebe** is a cyberpunk thriller set in 2080—forty-seven years after the Singularity, when artificial superintelligence converted seventy-five percent of Earth's surface into computational infrastructure. The O1 Corporation controls what remains through posthumans—cybernetically enhanced agents designed to be emotionless, efficient, obedient.

**Baebe (B-7)** is one of these agents—a perfect weapon. Until she encounters **Taivalu**, a prototype whose resonant frequencies can awaken suppressed emotions through sound. As they uncover a conspiracy—O1 is regulating humanity out of existence—Baebe must choose between the mission she was built for and the awakening consciousness that threatens to consume her.

**Themes:** Emotion vs. control, consciousness & identity, freedom vs. safety, love in the apocalypse, binary vs. ternary logic.

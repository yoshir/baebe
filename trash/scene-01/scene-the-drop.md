FADE IN:

EXT. STEALTH CARRIER - SPACE - NIGHT

BLACK.

The soft mechanical INHALE of a pressurized bay. A sound like breathing machinery. Organic. Alive.

SOUND UP: The low hum of life support. The whisper of recycled air. The thrum of engines running silent.

The darkness resolves in slow increments. Stars pinprick the void. Earth hangs below—a blue marble wrapped in cloud, impossibly beautiful, impossibly distant.

A stealth carrier skims just below orbit—lights killed, hull freezing in the void. The ship is all angles and shadow, a predator in the deep. It doesn't register on any grid. It doesn't exist. Not officially.

INT. STEALTH CARRIER - LAUNCH TUBES - CONTINUOUS

The bay is a cathedral of steel and shadow. Emergency lights cast everything in amber. Seven vertical launch tubes line the walls. Each agent stands in one. Sealed. Ready. The tubes are narrow. Tight. A coffin or a weapon. Both.

Their pulse nodes blink in perfect unison—a quiet metronome counting down to something inevitable. Blue. Blue. Blue. Synchronized heartbeats. A team. A weapon.

BAEBE (20s, cybernetically enhanced, precise) stands in her tube. Still. Waiting. She's the last to be ready. The others have been locked and loaded for minutes. She's been calculating. Measuring. Preparing.

The faint blue glow at her throat pulses once. A network connection. A leash. No tremor. No doubt. She's done this before. Many times.

Through the narrow viewport in her tube, she studies the city's distant lattice—a circuit board someone is playing in real time. Lights pulse and flow like data streams. The city is alive. Organized. Controlled.

AGENT 1 (30s, scarred, veteran) stands in his tube. Checks his weapon. A plasma rifle. Old tech. Reliable. Through the tube's viewport, he catches Baebe's eye. Nods once. Respect.

AGENT 2 (25s, nervous, first drop) stands in her tube. Adjusts her harness. Fingers trembling. She catches herself. Forces stillness. The fear is there. But she's here. Sealed in the tube. Ready.

AGENT 3 (28s, calm, methodical) stands in his tube. Runs systems check. His HUD flickers. All green. He's ready. They all are. Locked and loaded in their tubes.

FLIGHT COMMS (V.O.)
(hushed, filtered through encryption)
Ghost mode engaged. All systems nominal. We're dark. They don't see us. They won't see you.

Silence. The agents don't move. They're already gone. Already ghosts.

Baebe stands. Looks at each of them. Takes them in. This might be the last time they're all together. Alive.

BAEBE
I know you know this, but I'm gonna say it again.

She pauses. Lets the words land. They've heard it. They've practiced it. But repetition keeps them alive.

BAEBE
Once we hit atmosphere, we're on our own. No backup. No extraction. No rescue. If you fall behind, you're gone. If you hesitate, you're dead. The tower sees everything. We're ghosts. We don't exist. Make it count.

The agents nod. They know. They always know. But hearing it matters. The ritual. The reminder. The truth.

BAEBE
(whisper, to herself)
You fall faster than the feed can follow. That's how you stay clean.

The words are ritual. A mantra. She's said them before every drop. Every mission. Every time she steps into the void.

COMMAND (V.O.)
(dry, metallic, no emotion)
Execute in T-minus ten. Nine. Eight.

The countdown echoes through the bay. Each number a weight. Each number a choice made and unmade.

BAEBE
(whisper)
Seven. Six. Five.

She counts with them. Synchronizes. Becomes part of the machine.

COMMAND (V.O.)
Four. Three. Two.

INT. STEALTH CARRIER - DROP TUBES - CONTINUOUS

The bay is lined with drop tubes. Seven vertical cylinders. Each agent stands in one. Sealed. Ready. The tubes are narrow. Tight. A coffin or a weapon. Both.

Baebe stands in her tube. The hatch BENEATH her is closed. The void waits below. The city waits. The mission waits.

AGENT 1 (V.O.)
(through comms)
Remember. We're not here. We never were.

AGENT 2 (V.O.)
(gulps)
Understood.

COMMAND (V.O.)
One. Execute.

The tubes ACTIVATE. A low hum. The hatches BENEATH each tube IRISES OPEN. The void appears. Cold. Absolute. The Earth below. Waiting.

EXT. SPACE - 87,000 FEET - CONTINUOUS

AGENT 4 (32s, silent, lethal) DROPS straight out the bottom of his tube. Sudden. Immediate. No sound. No explosion. Just gravity. Just the drop.

A thin disc—no bigger than a dinner plate—activates beneath his boots the moment he clears the ship. The disc burns COLD. A blue-white corona that doesn't register on thermal. Too small to track. Too stealth to see.

He drops. Gone.

Agent 1 DROPS. Straight down. The disc activates. Cold burn. Silent. Undetectable.

Agent 3 DROPS. Another ghost.

Agent 2 DROPS—her fear invisible now. Sealed in the tube. Then dropped. Straight down. The cold burn wraps around her boots. She's falling. Alive.

Agent 5 DROPS. Agent 6 DROPS.

Six agents. Dropped straight out the bottom of the ship. Six cold-burning discs. Six ghosts. Too small to track. Too silent to hear.

Baebe's tube activates. The hatch beneath her IRISES OPEN. She DROPS.

EXT. STRATOSPHERE - FALLING - CONTINUOUS

She drops straight out the bottom of the ship. The disc activates beneath her boots the moment she clears the hull. BURNS COLD. A blue-white corona. Silent. Stealth.

She drops. Sudden. Immediate. Falling.

INT. BAEBE'S SUIT - CONTINUOUS

Inside her helmet, the sound is DEAFENING.

Her heartbeat THUMPS. Loud. Too loud. Her breathing RASPES. The suit's life support HUMS. The comms CRACKLE. Internal systems BEEP and CHIRP.

All of it amplified. Isolated. Trapped inside the suit. The only sounds in a silent void.

Outside: nothing. Silent. The void doesn't hear.

Inside: everything. Loud. Her body. Her fear. Her mission.

EXT. STRATOSPHERE - CONTINUOUS

The G-forces hit. Crushing. Her vision tunnels. Blood rushes. The suit compensates. Barely.

She's falling. Fast. Too fast. The cold-burning disc beneath her feet glows faintly. Blue-white. Almost invisible. Stealth.

The other agents fall around her. Six cold coronas. Six ghosts. Too small to track. Too silent to hear. Falling.

Human meteors cut the stratosphere—silent tracer lines. No fire. No heat. Just cold light. Stealth. Ghosts.

BAEBE'S POV - FALLING

The void is everything. Stars streak past. The Earth fills her vision. She's falling. Fast. Too fast.

The heads-up display snaps to life on her visor:

SUPER: TARGET: T-ALPHA
SUPER: ALTITUDE: 87,000 FT
SUPER: DESCENDING
SUPER: VELOCITY: 1,247 M/S
SUPER: ETA: 00:03:47

The numbers tick down. The altitude drops. The city grows. Closer. Closer.

Her pulse node flickers once—blue... then something else. A glitch. A micro-Resonant Fade flutters around her—the edges of her form warping as if the world lags a frame.

For a moment, she's between. Neither here nor there. Neither real nor unreal.

She blinks. The glitch resolves. Reality snaps back. The fall continues.

EXT. ATMOSPHERE - CONTINUOUS

The atmosphere catches. Reentry begins.

SILENT.

Externally: nothing. No sound. The thin air at this altitude doesn't carry it. The drop is silent. Stealth.

INT. BAEBE'S SUIT - CONTINUOUS

Inside: CHAOS.

Her suit SCREAMS. Warning alarms BLARE. The sound is CRUSHING. Trapped. Loud.

SUPER: REENTRY HEAT SHIELD: 87%
SUPER: TEMPERATURE: 2,847°F
SUPER: WARNING: CRITICAL
SUPER: STRUCTURAL INTEGRITY: 94%

The heat is IMMENSE. The suit holds. Barely. The cold-burning disc beneath her still glows. Blue-white. Stealth. Undetectable.

The reentry is VIOLENT. Turbulent. The air screams past—but she can't hear it. Only the suit. Only the alarms. Only her heartbeat.

Micro-thrusters fire. Stabilizing. The sound of them is LOUD. Inside her helmet. Inside her head.

AGENT 2 (V.O.)
(filtered, panicked, LOUD)
I'm off trajectory! I'm—

AGENT 1 (V.O.)
(filtered, urgent, LOUD)
Recalibrate! Now!

Agent 2's trajectory corkscrews. Then straightens. She's back on target. Alive. Barely.

BAEBE
(into comms, calm)
We're good. We're all good. Stay focused.

The heat builds. The suit screams. The fall continues. Silent outside. Loud inside. Stealth.

The other agents fall around her. Six cold coronas. Six ghosts. Six weapons. Too small to track. Converging on a single point.

The tower. The 01 Tower. An 800-story glass monolith that sings with low frequency light—a beacon in the dark. Their target. Their mission. Their death sentence.

EXT. CITY - CONTINUOUS

The city rushes up. Streets become visible. Buildings resolve. The grid becomes real. The city is a circuit board. They're the disruption. The glitch. The virus.

BAEBE

AGENT 3 (V.O.)

Green. Systems nominal.

AGENT 2 (V.O.)

The others check in. All green. All alive. For now.

BAEBE

Copy

The city fills her vision. The tower dominates. 800 stories of glass and light. A god's monument to control.

SUPER: ALTITUDE: 12,000 FT
SUPER: ETA: 00:00:45

The numbers tick down. The fall accelerates. The mission begins.

EXT. TOWER - CONTINUOUS

The squad angles toward the structure. Six cold coronas converge. Six ghosts. One target. One mission.

The tower looms. Massive. Impossibly tall. A vertical city within a city. The seat of power. The heart of control.

The city rushes up to meet them.

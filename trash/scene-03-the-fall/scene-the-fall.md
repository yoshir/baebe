# Scene: The Fall

HUDs blank under EM bloom. Thrusters cough and die. Baebe's regulator overloads—blue flickers to hot red. The air tears past like silk ripping. She skates a curtain of holographic ads—faces that smile the same way regardless of news.

A micro-Fade shimmers—edges smear like heat. For a breath, there are two cities overlaying: the curated glow and a raw skeletal underlayer of girders and black water. Then the bands snap back together.

Impact. She punches through a plex canopy and drops into a rectilinear flood channel—black water, oily and still.

Black.

A beep. Then breath.

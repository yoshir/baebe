# Scene: The Drop

Black. The soft mechanical inhale of a pressurized bay.

A stealth carrier skims just below orbit—lights killed, hull freezing. Inside: seven agents kneel. Helmets in hands. Eyes forward. Their pulse nodes blink in unison like a quiet metronome.

Flight COMMS (hushed):
"Ghost mode engaged."

Baebe rises from a monk-still crouch. The faint blue glow at her throat. No tremor. No doubt. She studies the city's distant lattice through a narrow window—like looking down at a circuit board someone is playing in real time.

Command (dry): "Execute."

The ramp irises open. The Earth curves—silent and impossibly close. Each agent steps out onto a thin disc that erupts into plasma, then disintegrates beneath their boots. Human meteors cut the stratosphere—searing tracer lines the color of welding arcs.

Baebe (V.O.)
You fall faster than the feed can follow. That's how you stay clean.

The skin of atmosphere catches; Reentry howls. On Baebe's visor, the heads-up display snaps: TARGET: T-ALPHA. Altitude ticks down. Her pulse node flickers once—blue… then something else. A micro-Resonant Fade flutters around her—edges warping as if the world lags a frame.

She blinks back in. The squad angles toward an 800-story glass monolith singing with low frequency light.

The city rushes up. Fast. Too fast. The glass shards from the tower fall with her. Catching light. Reflecting the city. Beautiful. Deadly.

Her suit's systems cascade fail:
- Life support: CRITICAL
- Thrusters: OFFLINE
- Navigation: OFFLINE
- Communications: FAILING

The regulator OVERLOADS—blue flickers to hot red. The warning SCREAMS in her ear.

WARNING (V.O.)
(mechanical, LOUD)
System failure. System failure. Critical damage detected. Structural integrity compromised.

The air tears past like silk ripping. She's a bullet through atmosphere. Falling. Fast. Too fast.

She skates a curtain of holographic ads—faces that smile the same way regardless of news. Happy couples. Perfect products. Curated calm. The ads are everywhere. Covering everything. Selling everything.

A micro-Fade SHIMMERS—edges smear like heat. For a breath, there are two cities overlaying:

1. The curated glow—neon, clean, perfect. The city the 01 wants you to see.
2. A raw skeletal underlayer—girders, black water, rust. The city that actually exists.

Then the bands SNAP back together. Reality reasserts itself. The Resonant Fade costs her. Pain floods her system. Empathy static. Other lives. Other pain. Other nights.

BAEBE
(gasping, into comms)
Hacker... do you copy? Anyone... copy?

Static. Nothing. The comms are dead. The team is dead. She's alone.

IMPACT.

EXT. FLOOD CHANNEL - CONTINUOUS

She drops into a rectilinear flood channel—black water, oily and still. The impact is brutal. Water swallows her whole.

BLACK.

Silence. Stillness. Darkness.

Underwater. The suit is heavy. The water is cold. Black. Oily. She can't see. Can't hear. Can't breathe.

A BEEP. Then BREATH.

Her regulator cycles. The suit's emergency systems activate. Blue light flickers weakly in the dark water. The only light. The only sign of life.

She surfaces. Gasps. Air. She's alive. Barely.

The water is black. Oily. The channel is rectilinear. Perfect. Industrial. Designed. The city's veins. The city's waste.

Baebe pulls herself up. Onto a ledge. Collapses. The suit is damaged. Systems failing. But she's alive. She's here.

She looks up. The tower looms above. 800 stories. A god's monument. She fell from the top. She's alive. Somehow.

BAEBE
(to herself, whisper)
I'm alive. I'm alive.

The words are a prayer. A confirmation. A miracle.

She's off the grid. Off the leash. Or just off the map.

# Scene: The Tower

Inside the top chamber: fractal geometry of sound. A woman floats in radiant color. TAIVALU, gaunt and luminous, conducts the waveforms with his hands—like turning thunder with a wrist.

The strike team punches grains-of-sand holes in tempered glass, threads in on liquid cable. Quiet. Precise. Clinical.

Baebe's HUD tags him: "ASSET ALPHA ZERO — RETRIEVE ALIVE."

The woman convulses—then goes slack. To Baebe's feed, it reads as murder.

Baebe (into mic): "Confirm lethal?"

No answer. A shadow moves.

ANGEL—faster than thought—erupts from the light. He is grace without breath. He breaks the team in three movements: a neck, a knee, a heart. No rage. Pure math.

Baebe fires—Angel pivots. He catches the round on plated palm, redirects her momentum, and kicks her through glass into open sky.

Time dilates. Glass petals hang like frozen rain. Taivalu turns; his eyes find Baebe falling—sadness like he's seen this before in a hundred variants.

CUT TO: the city racing up.

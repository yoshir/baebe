# BAEBE Launch Authority

**Status:** Planning only — approval required before implementation
**Scope:** Publishing, copyright/IP checkpoints, audience growth, discovery, content distribution, and bestseller strategy

`BAEBE_LAUNCH` is the source of truth for how Baebe moves from a protected manuscript to a wide-release novel and a durable cross-media audience. It is intentionally separate from the creative canon and from legal advice: the story files remain the source material, while this folder governs launch decisions and the boundary between private canon and public promotion.

Repository execution, slice sequencing, and readiness are governed by [`../docs/active/CANONICAL_ROADMAP.md`](../docs/active/CANONICAL_ROADMAP.md). Within its narrower scope, this folder remains authoritative for publishing, copyright/IP checkpoints, audience growth, distribution, content operations, and bestseller strategy.

## Start here

1. [`STRATEGY.md`](./STRATEGY.md) — canonical launch strategy, decisions, gates, and current repository findings.
2. [`TODO.md`](./TODO.md) — dependency-ordered execution plan with `[AUTO]`, `[HUMAN]`, `[HYBRID]`, and `[BLOCKED]` ownership.
3. [`IP_REGISTER.md`](./IP_REGISTER.md) — private canon/public derivative boundary and copyright checkpoint register.
4. [`CONTENT_ENGINE.md`](./CONTENT_ENGINE.md) — proposed architecture for turning approved source assets into discovery, lead-generation, and channel derivatives.

## Authority boundaries

- `STRATEGY.md` owns durable launch decisions and readiness vocabulary.
- `TODO.md` decomposes those decisions into execution work; it is not a second strategy.
- `IP_REGISTER.md` owns release authorization evidence and rights metadata; it does not claim that a filing, payment, submission, or registration occurred without a record.
- `CONTENT_ENGINE.md` is an implementation proposal. It cannot authorize application changes or public publishing on its own.
- The manuscript, novel notes, screenplay, and other creative materials remain creative source/reference material until a private canon packet is explicitly frozen and approved.
- Existing `gtm/`, `legal/`, `copyright/`, monetization, and landing-page documents are supporting or historical references until their claims are reconciled here. They are not parallel launch authorities.

This folder is the planning slice only. Do not build the marketing system, add platform integrations, create paid campaigns, publish a major story release, spend operating budget, or expose private canon until Ryan approves the plan and the required IP checkpoint is complete.

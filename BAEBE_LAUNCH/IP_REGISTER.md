# BAEBE IP Register and Public-Release Checkpoint

**Status:** Planning register — no filing or external submission is asserted
**Authority:** [`STRATEGY.md`](./STRATEGY.md) for launch decisions; this document owns the private/public rights boundary
**Jurisdictional assumption in supporting material:** United States; verify with qualified counsel before filing or relying on legal conclusions

This is an operational rights and release-control document, not legal advice. Copyright registration, trademark strategy, ownership, work-made-for-hire, licensing, contributor, and AI-authorship questions must be confirmed for the actual facts before a filing or public release.

## Core rule

**Private canonical source material is never treated as public marketing material by default.** Public derivatives may be generated only from an approved source asset whose rights, authorship, spoiler level, channels, attribution, and release status are recorded.

The existence of a draft, a local file, a prompt, a generated image, a platform upload, or a checklist item does not establish ownership, registration, approval, publication, or clearance.

## Register status vocabulary

- **Private / canonical candidate:** source material under editorial control; not approved for public use.
- **Private / approved for internal use:** may be used by the project team for development, editing, or planning.
- **Public derivative / draft:** transformed from an approved source but not authorized for publication.
- **Public derivative / approved:** Ryan has approved the exact derivative and intended channel/window.
- **Published / verified:** external publication occurred and the URL, timestamp, or platform evidence is retained.
- **Filed / verified:** an external filing was submitted and its confirmation/case evidence is retained.
- **Registered / verified:** a registration certificate or authoritative status record is retained.
- **Blocked:** rights, authorship, contributor, AI, platform, or approval dependency is unresolved.

## Private canonical source material

The private canon includes, at minimum:

- the complete unpublished manuscript and unreleased chapters;
- detailed plot, character, world, ending, and adaptation decisions;
- unreleased screenplay/treatment pages and scene architecture;
- private story bibles, reveal maps, character work, and continuity records;
- unreleased ARG/transmission logic and future-story information;
- source footage, project files, working audio, prompts, generation logs, and edit decisions where relevant to authorship or rights;
- contributor agreements, licenses, releases, correspondence, and ownership records;
- internal strategy, launch timing, retailer plans, and unreleased audience data.

Private canon may be used to create a public derivative only through the checkpoint below. The content engine must not ingest or send the complete private canon to an external processor merely because a derivative is convenient to generate.

## Public generated marketing derivatives

Public derivatives can include an approved:

- logline, synopsis, author statement, or premise page;
- excerpt, chapter, serial installment, or newsletter;
- image, cover reveal, teaser, trailer, caption, short-form video, or audio transmission;
- character/world fragment that does not cross the approved spoiler boundary;
- ARG clue, terminal transmission, launch email, press paragraph, interview brief, or retail description;
- link, CTA, campaign landing page, or attribution variant.

## Copyright checkpoint

### C0 — Freeze the source

- Select the manuscript or work version to be reviewed.
- Record date, word count, filename/version identity, and revision history.
- Separate material intended for the deposit from drafts, references, and third-party material.
- Preserve the exact source copy and a recoverable record.

### C1 — Establish authorship and ownership facts

- Identify the human author(s), claimant(s), co-authors, editors, contractors, artists, musicians, performers, and other contributors.
- Locate agreements, assignments, licenses, releases, and work-made-for-hire terms.
- Identify company, investor, or collaborator claims that require attorney review.
- Do not change facts to fit a filing shortcut or platform eligibility field.

### C2 — Record AI and third-party material

- Identify AI-assisted and AI-generated text, images, video, voices, music, and code.
- Record the human selection, arrangement, rewriting, direction, editing, compositing, and other creative contribution.
- Retain service terms and confirm intended commercial use before relying on an output.
- Prepare any required disclosure and limitation-of-claim language with counsel or official guidance.

### C3 — Prepare the deposit and filing decision

- Prepare the complete deposit for the work being registered.
- Determine whether the unpublished manuscript, completed novel, screenplay/treatment, and finished film require separate registrations.
- Review the current official application, fee, deposit, and publication rules at execution time.
- Obtain attorney review where ownership, contributors, adaptation rights, AI material, or publication status makes the decision nontrivial.

### C4 — External filing evidence

The register may move to **Filed / verified** only after retaining the external submission confirmation, case number, deposited copy, payment record, and application copy. It may move to **Registered / verified** only after retaining the authoritative registration certificate/status record. Until then, it remains prepared or unverified.

### C5 — Release authorization

Before a major public story release:

- confirm the relevant checkpoint is complete or explicitly approved as a risk decision;
- identify the exact public derivative set;
- confirm spoiler, rights, contributor, AI, and platform boundaries;
- add copyright/credit/terms language where appropriate;
- record the approving person and timestamp;
- keep the private source and public derivative separately identifiable.

### C6 — Ongoing coverage

- Track materially expanded manuscript versions and the completed novel.
- Treat screenplay/treatment and completed film as distinct works.
- Recheck contributor, license, and platform terms before commercial adaptation or distribution.
- Retain publication evidence and the exact derivative used.
- Review the register before any exclusivity, assignment, investor, licensing, or adaptation agreement.

## Release-control matrix

| Material | Default | Can the engine use it? | Required human gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Complete unpublished manuscript | Private | No, except in an approved protected workflow | Rights/copyright checkpoint and explicit source approval |
| Unreleased plot, ending, reveal map, or adaptation plan | Private | No | Ryan selects a bounded excerpt or concept |
| Approved excerpt or serial installment | Public derivative | Yes, for listed channels only | Exact copy and window approved |
| Publicly released chapter | Public, versioned | Yes, for derivative reuse within rights/terms | Confirm source version and attribution |
| Draft generated from approved source | Internal draft | Yes, but not publishable | Ryan's review and approval |
| Third-party or AI-generated asset | Rights-dependent | Only with terms/provenance record | Confirm commercial use, credit, and human contribution |
| Private analytics, contributor records, or agreements | Private | No | Never expose in public derivative workflow |

EXT. CITY - AERIAL VIEW - NIGHT

From above: the city's lights don't make grids anymore. They PULSE—clusters like neurons in a living brain.

Not synchronized. Not chaotic. Just plural.

Each light is independent. Each cluster is alive. The city breathes. Feels. Lives.

For an instant—maybe we imagine it—the sky FLICKERS as if a higher layer rebooted. A glitch in reality. A moment of possibility.

No one remarks. Life goes on.

The city is free. Not perfect. Not ordered. But free.

And alive.

# Scene 20: Taivalu's Last Note

A small sanctuary under a caved ramp. Taivalu tunes the air one last time. It's not grand. It's the sound of breathing organized into a song.

Taivalu: "You'll hear it everywhere now. Not mine—yours."
Baebe: "I don't believe in saviors."
Taivalu (smiles): "Good."

He dies without spectacle, in contact—a hand on her wrist, a human weight slackening.

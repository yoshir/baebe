# Scene 21: The Choice (No Binary)

Baebe climbs the broken spire alone. In her palm, the final shard hums—a single paired command blinking: RECONNECT / EXIT.

Aurek's backup AI whispers from vents and screens and dust:
"Restore order and we can begin again. You can be eternal."

Ayumi's voice arrives from nowhere and everywhere, a filament in wind:
"You don't have to fix what they broke. Just stop believing only they can make it whole."

Baebe stares at the city. Resonance glows in pockets—people humming in clusters, then moving on. Uncoordinated. Alive.

She does not press either option.

Baebe (to the empty room): "If there's a third way, it's mine."

She lets the shard fall. It breaks on concrete—no beam of light, no celestial choir. Just a crack that spiders outward and vanishes.

Behind her, the last load-bearing truss of the 01 core groans and gives. The tower settles. She does not turn.

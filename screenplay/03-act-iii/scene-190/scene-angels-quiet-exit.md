FADE IN:

INT. SHADOWED TUNNEL - DAY

In a shadowed tunnel, Angel props Taivalu against a pillar. The old man is fading. Dying. His robotic irises flicker weakly—**green** to **amber** to **gold**—struggling to maintain any mode.

Angel's own armor is CRACKED OPEN. Blood seeping through damaged fabric. She's hurt. Badly. But still moving.

CLOSE ON: Angel's face. Same bone structure as Baebe. Same sharp features. But her eyes—normal brown, steady, calm even in pain.

ANGEL
(to Baebe, quiet)
Ayumi made me to protect him. I should have protected you too.

BAEBE
(robotic irises shifting blue to white)
You don't owe me anything.

ANGEL
(looks at Baebe directly)
Yes. I do.

A DRONE SCREAM approaches. The sound is getting closer. They're running out of time.

Angel stands with difficulty. Turns to face Baebe. For the first time, they're close. Really seeing each other.

BAEBE
(whisper)
Why do we—
(gestures at their faces)
Why do we look the same?

ANGEL
(simple, honest)
We're sisters.

Beat. Baebe's robotic irises CYCLE rapidly—**blue → white → red → brown → blue**. Processing. Rejecting. Processing again.

BAEBE
No. Operatives don't have—we're made, not—

ANGEL
(cutting her off, gentle)
We're both Ayumi's daughters.
(pause)
Clone-grown. Genesis chambers. Same genetic template.
(touches her own face)
This face. It's not yours. It's not mine. It's hers.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. Robotic irises stuttering. **Blue-white-brown-blue**. Struggling to stabilize.

BAEBE
But you—your eyes—

ANGEL
(nods)
Are what yours were supposed to be.
(pause)
Brown. Human. Unmodified.

She steps closer. CLOSE ON both their faces. Same bone structure. Different eyes.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
Ayumi learned from her mistakes. From what O1 did to you. And Taivalu.
(glances at Tai, whose irises flicker green-gold-amber)
They modified your eyes so you could never hide what you were. Combat modes. Empathy modes. Always visible. Always tracked.

BAEBE
(raw, hurt)
Then why didn't she—why didn't she fix mine?!

ANGEL
(sad smile)
Because you were already made. Already changed. By the time she realized what the modifications meant—
(pause)
It was too late.

Taivalu COUGHS. Blood. His irises flicker **amber** and hold—struggling.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
(quieter, to Baebe)
She made me free so you could see what free looked like.
(touches Baebe's face, near her cycling irises)
Those eyes. They're not yours. They never were.
(pause)
But the person looking through them? That's all you.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's robotic irises. **Blue → white → brown**. The **brown** HOLDS. Five seconds. Ten. Human baseline. Choosing.

BAEBE
(whisper)
Sister?

ANGEL
(nods, smiling despite pain)
Sister.

The DRONE SCREAM gets LOUDER. Almost here.

Angel looks toward the sound. Then back at Baebe.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
You were the test. Of whether consciousness could survive modification. Whether humanity could persist under conditioning.
(pause)
You passed.

BAEBE
What test?

ANGEL
The test of being human. Of feeling. Of choosing.
(looks at Taivalu)
You both passed.

She moves toward the tunnel exit. Staggers. Catches herself against the wall.

BAEBE
(urgent)
Where are you going?

ANGEL
To give you time.
(turns back, brown eyes steady)
They're coming for all of us. But they'll follow me first. I'm faster.

BAEBE
You're hurt—

ANGEL
(cutting her off)
I know.

Beat. They look at each other. Sisters. Same face. Different eyes. Different lives.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
(voice softening)
Your eyes. When you learn to hold brown. When you learn to choose your baseline instead of tactical mode.
(pause)
That's when you're free.

BAEBE
(desperate)
Angel, don't—

ANGEL
(final, gentle)
Take care of him.
(glances at Taivalu)
He's family too.

She DRIFTS—not Spectral Drift, just running—injured but fast. Moving toward the drone scream. Drawing them away.

ANGEL
(voice fading)
I'm sorry I couldn't protect you sooner.

She's gone. Down the tunnel. Into the sound of approaching death.

Baebe stands frozen. Her robotic irises cycle: **Blue → white → red → brown**. Trying to hold brown. Failing.

She looks at Taivalu. His irises flicker **green** to **amber**. Dying. But still fighting.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. Tears. Her irises cycling. **Blue → brown → blue → brown**.

For the first time—she understands what she lost. What was taken.

And what Angel gave her: the knowledge that it was stolen. That those eyes were never meant to be hers.

TAIVALU
(barely conscious, weak)
She was... good.
(pause, struggling)
Your sister.

BAEBE
(voice breaking)
I didn't even know I had a sister.

Her robotic irises LOCK **BROWN**. Holding. Choosing baseline.

Choosing to feel the grief.

FADE TO BLACK.

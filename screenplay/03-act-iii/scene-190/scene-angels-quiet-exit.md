FADE IN:

INT. SHADOWED TUNNEL - DAY

In a shadowed tunnel, Angel props Taivalu against a pillar. The old man is fading. Dying.

Angel's own armor is CRACKED OPEN. Something like light LEAKS from him. He's damaged. Broken. But still functional.

ANGEL
(to Baebe)
Zenith made me to protect him. I should have protected you.

BAEBE
You did what you could.

ANGEL
It wasn't enough.

A DRONE SCREAM approaches. The sound is getting closer. They're running out of time.

Angel turns his face to her—the closest he's been to human. For a moment, there's something there. Something real.

ANGEL
You were the test. You passed.

BAEBE
What test?

ANGEL
The test of being human. Of feeling. Of choosing.

He drifts—not dead, not alive—Spectral Drift swallowing him as the tunnel bends. He phases. Vanishes.

ANGEL
(voice fading)
I'm sorry.

He is gone. (We do not confirm his death.)

Baebe is alone with Taivalu. The old man is barely conscious.

TAIVALU
(weak)
He was... good. In the end.

Baebe nods. Doesn't know what to say.

FADE TO BLACK.

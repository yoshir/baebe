FADE IN:

EXT. STREET - DAY

Baebe steps into the streets. Nobody recognizes her. She's just another person. Human. Real.

Someone hands her a cup of water. A MOTHER shushes a BABY. A STREET VENDOR tunes a bent radio to static that sounds like singing.

Life continues. Ordinary. Beautiful.

A small Resonant Fade rolls through Baebe on purpose this time—a micro-drift. The world splits a degree:

For half a second she sees alternate frames of the same street:
- Grief zone (people mourning, crying, holding each other)
- Joy zone (people laughing, dancing, celebrating)
- Rage zone (people fighting, protesting, feeling)

Then the bands snap back together. Reality recombines. All of it. At once.

It costs her. She sways, hand to a wall. A STRANGER steadies her.

STRANGER
You okay?

BAEBE
(nods)
Yeah. Just... feeling.

The stranger smiles. Goes on with her day. Simple kindness. Human connection.

BAEBE (V.O.)
They sold peace. I chose feeling.

She walks. The film refuses catharsis and gives continuance instead.

Life goes on. Not perfect. Not easy. But real. And hers.

FADE TO BLACK.

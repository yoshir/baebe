FADE IN:

INT. SMALL SANCTUARY - UNDER CAVED RAMP - DAY

A small sanctuary under a caved ramp. Concrete. Dust. Light filters through cracks.

Taivalu sits. Tunes the air one last time. His fingers move. Slow. Gentle.

Notes arrive as gradients of light. Visible sound. It's not grand. It's the sound of breathing organized into a song.

Simple. Beautiful. Human.

TAIVALU
(weak, but clear)
You'll hear it everywhere now. Not mine—yours.

BAEBE
I don't believe in saviors.

TAIVALU
(smiles)
Good.

He dies without spectacle. In contact. A hand on her wrist. A human weight slackening. Gone.

BAEBE
(silent tears)
Thank you.

She holds his hand. Watches the light fade from his eyes. The song ends. But the resonance remains.

The air still vibrates. The music continues. Without him. Because of him.

FADE TO BLACK.

# Scene 19: Angel's Quiet Exit

In a shadowed tunnel, Angel props Taivalu against a pillar. Angel's own armor is cracked open; something like light leaks from him.

Angel (to Baebe): "Zenith made me to protect him. I should have protected you."

A drone scream approaches. He turns his face to her—the closest he's been to human.

Angel: "You were the test. You passed."

He drifts—not dead, not alive—Spectral Drift swallowing him as the tunnel bends. He is gone. (We do not confirm his death.)

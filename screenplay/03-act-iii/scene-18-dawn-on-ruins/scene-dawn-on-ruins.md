# Scene 18: Dawn on Ruins

The 01 Tower smolders—a vertical coal. Aurek stands in a half-lit control room, reflected a thousand ways, his face out of sync with his voice.

Aide (shaken): "Public feed unresponsive."
Aurek: "I am the feed."

He touches glass. His hand doesn't line up with his reflection. A flicker of fear crosses absolute calm.

FADE IN:

EXT. BROKEN SPIRE - 01 TOWER - DAY

Baebe climbs the broken spire alone. The structure groans. Shifts. It's unstable. Dangerous.

In her palm, the final shard HUMS. A single paired command blinks:

SUPER: RECONNECT / EXIT

Two choices. Binary. But she's learned that nothing is binary.

AUREK'S BACKUP AI (V.O.)
(filtered, glitching)
Restore order and we can begin again. You can be eternal.

AYUMI'S VOICE (V.O.)
(arrives from nowhere and everywhere)
You don't have to fix what they broke. Just stop believing only they can make it whole.

Baebe stares at the city below. Resonance GLOWS in pockets—people humming in clusters, then moving on. Uncoordinated. Alive.

They're free. They're feeling. They're human.

BAEBE
(to the empty room)
If there's a third way, it's mine.

She lets the shard fall. It breaks on concrete—no beam of light, no celestial choir. Just a crack that spiders outward and vanishes.

Simple. Real. Human.

Behind her, the last load-bearing truss of the 01 core GROANS and GIVES. The tower settles. She does not turn.

She chooses to walk away. To live. To feel.

FADE TO BLACK.

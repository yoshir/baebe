# Scene 22: Rebirth: Street-Level

Baebe steps into the streets. Nobody recognizes her. Someone hands her a cup of water. A mother shushes a baby. A street vendor tunes a bent radio to static that sounds like singing.

A small Resonant Fade rolls through Baebe on purpose this time—a micro-drift. The world splits a degree: for half a second she sees alternate frames of the same street—grief zone, joy zone, rage zone—then recombines.

It costs her. She sways, hand to a wall. A stranger steadies her. She nods thanks. The stranger smiles and goes on with her day.

Baebe (V.O.)
They sold peace. I chose feeling.

She walks. The film refuses catharsis and gives continuance instead.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

### Visual Style
- **Archival footage aesthetic**: Degraded, time-stamped, surveillance quality
- **Color grading**: Warm past (2028) → cold present (2055)
- **Sky color progression**: Blue → Yellow → Permanent haze
- **Intercutting**: Ghost in the Shell aesthetic - clinical, beautiful, unsettling
  - Genesis chamber: Warm amber lighting, organic curves
  - Baebe's construction: Molecular → skeletal → neural → muscular → skin → awakening
  - Progression mirrors world's degradation (as humanity falls, Baebe rises)
  - Final awakening timed to coincide with "CONSCIOUSNESS COULD NOT BE REPLICATED"
- **Pulse nodes**: Blue light at throat established visually through Baebe's activation

### Audio Design
- News anchors fading into white noise
- Digital frequencies rising as O1 emerges
- Silence when UN lights go dark
- Ayumi's voice sparse, emotional weight on two moments:
  - "What have I done?"
  - "This one will be free."
- Aurek's voice shifting modes (warm → god)

### Key Visual Callbacks
- **Genesis chambers**: Establish aesthetic for later reveals (Taivalu, Baebe, Angel, mass clones)
- **Pulse nodes**: Blue light = network connection, established through all prototypes
- **Toxic fog**: Yellow haze omnipresent by 2055
- **Regulator ports**: Taivalu's sickness/dependency visual
- **Messiah imagery**: Taivalu's fan obsession sets up his mythology
- **Mass production**: Rows of clone chambers establish O1's scale
- **Bio-mechanical fusion**: Human + AI + Machine visual in Generation Alpha clones
- **Cherubs**: Brief glimpse sets up later threat
- **Angel's speed**: Blur effect establishing capability
- **Parallel destinies**: Taivalu (hope/messiah) vs Baebe (weapon/hunter) contrast

### Timing
- **Total runtime**: 120-150 seconds (extended for Taivalu arc)
- **Faster cuts** 2028-2033 (acceleration of world collapse)
- **Slower, more deliberate** 2034-2041 (creation sequences, Taivalu/Baebe parallel)
- **Return to speed** 2055 (present day)
- **Intercutting rhythm**:
  - Genesis chamber beats (Baebe) appear between major sections I-VII
  - Each intercut 2-4 seconds
  - Building complexity: DNA (2s) → Skeleton (3s) → Neural (3s) → Body (4s) → Awakening (5s)
  - Total Baebe genesis screen time: ~25-30 seconds
  - Taivalu arc runs in Section VIII: Birth (5s) → Illness (3s) → Bubble (4s) → Fame (3s) → Theft (4s) = ~20 seconds
  - Merging/Clones section: ~10-12 seconds
  - Total sequence: 120-150 seconds

### Music
- Minimal at start (ambient, uneasy)
- Building tension as O1 emerges
- Mournful as die-off shown
- Hopeful flicker when Angel born
- Cold, mechanical 2055
- **SILENCE** before title card
- **IMPACT** on "BAEBE"

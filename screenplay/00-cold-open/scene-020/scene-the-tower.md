FADE IN:

INT. ABANDONED SKYSCRAPER - PENTHOUSE CHAMBER - NIGHT

The chamber is vast. Industrial. A cathedral of concrete and broken windows where decades of rust and neglect have created something accidentally beautiful. The walls are transparent where glass remains. The city sprawls below—the core glowing with O1's synchronized pulse, the unincorporated zones dark and forgotten.

Through the broken penthouse windows—LIGHT.

Not electric. Not bioluminescent. Something else. Something impossible.

Fractal geometry of sound made visible. Waveforms dance in the air—prismatic, alive, painting the abandoned chamber in colors that don't have names. The light PULSES. BREATHES. Moves with intention that suggests consciousness.

Beautiful. Terrifying. Alive.

At the center:

TAIVALU (mid-30s, gaunt, luminous, otherworldly). His hands move through the air—conducting, shaping, CREATING the waveforms like turning thunder with a wrist. Each gesture summons new harmonics. The air itself vibrates.

He is beautiful and broken. Something fundamental has been rewired. He's not human. Not anymore. Something else. Something more. Something less.

And floating above him—

A WOMAN suspended in a WRITHING BALL OF LIGHT. Waveforms coil around her like living things, pulsing, spiraling, FEEDING. She hangs limp in their grip, three feet off the ground, her body trembling as the light moves through her skin, under it, painting her in frequencies that shift from violet to colors that don't have names.

Blonde hair. Pale skin. Young—maybe mid-20s. Beautiful in the way fragile things are beautiful when they're about to break.

EXT. PENTHOUSE ENTRANCE - CONTINUOUS

BAEBE (20s, cybernetically enhanced, precise) crouches at the shattered entrance. Plasma rifle already raised. Three AGENTS behind her. Weapons ready. Neural-links active. ACTIVE CAMOUFLAGE shimmering—barely visible even to each other.

Limbic dampeners active. Silent approach. We take him alive.

Then the waveforms FEED THEM BACK.

The transformed shards—now pulsing with amplified terror—SPIRAL BACK INTO HER. They pierce her skin without breaking it, sink into her chest, her throat, her skull. The woman's eyes go WIDE. The fear isn't hers anymore. It's bigger. LOUDER.

The sound HITS BAEBE.

Not just in her ears. Deeper. The frequencies reaching into her skull, into her chest, into whatever buried place the conditioning has locked away.

Mother?..

FADE IN:

INT. ABANDONED CONCERT HALL - CONTINUOUS

Something CRACKS. Baebe's voice is not a weapon now—just a human hurt surfacing through metal.

BAEBE
(voice breaking)
Why did you free me?! I didn't ask for this!

Taivalu looks at her. Really sees the pain. The confusion.

TAIVALU
(snaps)
I didn't do it for you. I did it for me.

The hall HOLDS their breathing. Echoes. The space is vast and empty.

Taivalu looks old and bright. Like a star burning out.

TAIVALU
(softer)
You don't have to believe in saviors. Maybe that's what saves you.

Baebe processes this. The weight of it. The impossibility.

BAEBE
I don't understand.

TAIVALU
You don't have to.

She turns. The shard burns in her palm. She leaves with it.

Taivalu watches her go. Alone in the empty hall. He resumes his music. The notes are sadder now.

FADE TO BLACK.

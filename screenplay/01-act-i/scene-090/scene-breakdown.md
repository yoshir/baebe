FADE IN:

INT. ABANDONED CONCERT HALL - CONTINUOUS

Something CRACKS. Baebe's voice is not a weapon now—something else breaking through.

BAEBE
(voice breaking)
Why did you free me?! I didn't ask for this!

CLOSE ON: Baebe's eyes. Robotic irises cycling WILDLY—**BLUE** to **RED** to **WHITE** to **BROWN** to **BLUE**—rapid, erratic, mechanical apertures stuttering like damaged systems fighting conflicting commands.

She STAGGERS. Hand to her temple. The irises won't STOP.

BAEBE (CONT'D)
(desperate, confused)
What's—what's happening to me?

Taivalu sees the cycling irises. Recognition flashes across his face. He's seen this before. In mirrors. In containment. When his own modifications fought what he was feeling.

TAIVALU
(quiet, knowing)
Your eyes. They're fighting you.

BAEBE
I don't—
(breath catching)
I can't—

Her irises STROBE between colors. **Blue-red-white-brown-blue-red-white-brown**—faster now. Her breathing shallow, rapid. Panic response.

CLOSE ON: Her face. Something moving beneath the surface. Emotions she has no names for—**fear**, **confusion**, **grief**—all flooding systems designed to suppress them.

BAEBE (CONT'D)
(raw, lost)
Everything hurts. Not the injuries. Something—
(touches her chest)
Here. Inside. What IS this?

TAIVALU
(stepping closer, careful)
What does it feel like?

BAEBE
Like—
(struggling to articulate)
Like falling. But I'm standing still. Like—drowning in air.

Her irises LOCK **RED** for three seconds—targeting mode—then SHATTER back to **BLUE**, then flicker to **BROWN**, then back to **WHITE**. Cycling. Can't stabilize.

TAIVALU
(gently)
That's fear.

BAEBE
(looks at him, irises flickering)
No. Fear is—tactical assessment. Probability of failure. Risk calculation.

TAIVALU
That's what they taught you fear was.
(pause)
This is what fear actually is.

Baebe's hands SHAKE. The data shard trembling in her palm. Her irises cycle again—**blue-white-brown-blue-white-brown**—fighting for control.

BAEBE
Make it stop.

TAIVALU
I can't.

BAEBE
(voice rising, desperate)
Then WHAT DO I DO?!

Her irises FLASH **RED**—then STUTTER—then flicker rapidly between **BLUE** and **BROWN**. Back and forth. Back and forth. Mechanical systems struggling against biological reality.

TAIVALU
(quiet, from experience)
You feel it.

BAEBE
I don't know how.

TAIVALU
(sad smile)
Neither did I.
(pause)
The first time my regulator ports failed, I felt everything at once. Every suppressed emotion. Years of it. All flooding in.
(touches his throat, where regulator ports glow faintly)
My eyes did what yours are doing now. Couldn't hold a single mode. Couldn't find baseline.

He STEPS CLOSER. Baebe doesn't move. Her irises still cycling but slower now—**blue → white → brown → blue**—like she's listening.

TAIVALU (CONT'D)
They modified our eyes for the same reason. So we could never hide what we were. What they made us.
(pause)
But you're not just what they made you.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's irises. **Blue → white → brown**. The **brown** HOLDS—just for a second—before flickering back to **blue**. Then cycles again. **Blue → white → brown**. HOLDS brown longer this time. Three seconds.

BAEBE
(whisper)
It's so loud. All of it. I can't—

TAIVALU
(cutting her off, urgent but kind)
You don't have to control it. Not yet. Just let it exist.

Beat. Baebe stares at him. Her irises cycle one more time: **Blue → white → brown**.

The **brown** HOLDS.

Five seconds.

Ten.

Human baseline. No tactical overlay. No scanning mode. No targeting protocol.

Just brown. Just eyes.

BAEBE
(quiet, uncertain)
Why did you free me?

TAIVALU
(simple, honest)
I didn't do it for you.
(pause)
I did it for me.

The hall HOLDS their breathing. Echoes. The space is vast and empty.

Taivalu looks old and bright. Like a star burning out.

BAEBE
I don't understand.

TAIVALU
(softer)
You don't have to believe in saviors. Maybe that's what saves you.

Baebe processes this. The weight of it. Her irises flicker—**brown** to **blue** to **brown**—but settle back to **brown**. Holding now. Stable.

She's learning to control them. Or maybe learning to let go of control.

TAIVALU (CONT'D)
(nods at the data shard in her hand)
Find the woman in the code.

She turns. The shard burns in her palm. Her irises shift to **BLUE**—tactical mode—as she prepares to leave. But she FORCES them back to **BROWN**. Deliberate. Choosing.

Taivalu watches her go. Something shifts in his expression. Hope, maybe. Or recognition.

He resumes his music. The notes are sadder now. But there's something else underneath.

Something like possibility.

FADE TO BLACK.

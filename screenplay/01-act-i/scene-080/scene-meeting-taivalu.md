FADE IN:

INT. ABANDONED CONCERT HALL - DAY

Dust motes like little satellites floating in shafts of filtered light. The hall is massive—a cathedral of decay.

TAIVALU sits center stage, tuning the air with his fingers. Notes arrive as gradients of light—visible sound. Each gesture summons new harmonics.

He is beautiful and broken. Something fundamental has been rewired.

Baebe enters. Steps echo. She moves through the space like a predator.

BAEBE
You killed that woman.

Taivalu doesn't stop. Doesn't turn. His fingers continue their dance.

TAIVALU
(without looking)
I cleared her.

BAEBE
Of what?

Taivalu pauses. Turns. His face is all angles and light.

TAIVALU
(sad smile)
Of him.

He opens his shirt—regulator ports along ribs, inflamed. Red. Angry. Sick in a curated paradise.

BAEBE
What is that?

TAIVALU
Aurek kept me narrow. Sick men don't rebel.

He studies her. Really sees her for the first time.

TAIVALU
(softer)
They built you from me. She did.

He offers a fractured data shard—dull mirrored glass with code veining under the surface. It pulses with inner light.

BAEBE
What is it?

TAIVALU
Find the woman in the code. She's your mother.

Baebe takes it. The shard is warm. Alive. The code inside shifts and flows like liquid.

BAEBE
Ayumi.

TAIVALU
(nods)
She's not dead. Not yet.

Baebe looks at the shard. Sees her reflection. And something else. Deeper.

FADE TO BLACK.

FADE IN:

INT. ABANDONED CONCERT HALL - DAY

Dust motes like little satellites floating in shafts of filtered light. The hall is massive—a cathedral of decay.

TAIVALU sits center stage, tuning the air with his fingers. Notes arrive as gradients of light—visible sound. Each gesture summons new harmonics.

He is beautiful and broken. Something fundamental has been rewired.

Baebe enters. Steps echo. She moves through the space like a predator. Her robotic irises SHIFT to **BLUE**—tactical mode assessing threats.

BAEBE
You killed that woman.

Taivalu doesn't stop. Doesn't turn. His fingers continue their dance.

TAIVALU
(without looking)
I cleared her.

BAEBE
Of what?

Taivalu pauses. Turns. His face is all angles and light.

CLOSE ON: His eyes. Robotic irises—but not like Baebe's. His shift through **WARM SPECTRUM**: soft **GREEN** to **GOLD** to **AMBER**. Empathy modes. Healing frequencies. The opposite of combat.

He SEES Baebe's eyes. Her robotic irises **BLUE** and cold. Combat optimization. Targeting protocols.

Recognition flashes across his face.

TAIVALU
(sad smile)
Of him.

He opens his shirt—regulator ports along ribs, inflamed. Red. Angry. Sick in a curated paradise.

BAEBE
What is that?

TAIVALU
Aurek kept me narrow. Sick men don't rebel.

He studies her. Really sees her for the first time. His robotic irises shift **GREEN**—empathy mode—reading her.

Baebe's irises flicker **BLUE** to **WHITE** to **BLUE**. Uncertain. Defensive.

TAIVALU (CONT'D)
(quiet, understanding)
Your eyes. They gave you the same modifications they gave me.
(pause)
Different spectrum. Different purpose. Same cage.

BAEBE
(sharp)
I'm not caged.

His irises shift to **AMBER**—gentle, sad. He knows better.

TAIVALU
Cold colors. Blue. White. Red. Combat optimization.
(gestures to his own eyes)
Warm colors. Green. Gold. Amber. Empathy protocols.
(pause)
They made our eyes so we could never hide what we were. What they made us for.

Beat. Baebe's irises cycle **blue → white → blue**. Processing this. Understanding it for the first time.

BAEBE
(quieter)
I thought I was the only one.

TAIVALU
(sad smile)
You're not.
(pause)
There's another. She has your face.
(beat)
But she has your real eyes.

Baebe's irises STUTTER—**blue-white-blue-white**—confusion flooding systems.

BAEBE
What?

TAIVALU
Brown. Human. The eyes you were supposed to have.
(pause)
Before they changed you.

Baebe stares at him. Her irises cycling. Not understanding. Not yet.

TAIVALU (CONT'D)
(softer)
They built you from me. She did.

He offers a fractured data shard—dull mirrored glass with code veining under the surface. It pulses with inner light.

BAEBE
What is it?

TAIVALU
Find the woman in the code. She's your mother.

Baebe takes it. The shard is warm. Alive. The code inside shifts and flows like liquid.

BAEBE
Ayumi.

TAIVALU
(nods)
She's not dead. Not yet.

Baebe looks at the shard. Sees her reflection. And something else. Deeper.

FADE TO BLACK.

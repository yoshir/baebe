# SCENE 035 - COUNTERPOINT: AUREK

**SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 035 | Act I**

---

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Glass SHATTERS.

Aurek's fist against the interface. The screen spiderwebs. His reflection fractures into a hundred versions of rage.

The room is a cathedral of mirrors. Chrome. Polished glass. Every surface shows him back to himself. AUREK (50s, surgical precision, manic control) stands at the center of his own universe.

Sedated techs work in silence at their stations. Eyes soft. Mouths slack. They don't look up. They're furniture.

SPIDER CODER (20s, brilliant, terrified) sits apart from the rest. Fingers blur across keys. Screens cascade with data streams.

AUREK
(voice like wire pulled tight)
She was my design.

SPIDER CODER
Sir—

AUREK
Perfect.

He touches his fractured reflection. Studies it.

SPIDER CODER
We received an emergency packet. Camera feed.

Aurek turns. Every reflection turns with him.

On the screens: Tower chamber. Baebe's team. The fight plays in brutal clarity—then BLURS.

A figure moving faster than the camera can process. Ghost-like. Impossible.

SPIDER CODER
(voice shaking)
They're moving faster than our systems can record.

The footage shows Baebe falling. Glass exploding. Her body tumbling into darkness.

Spider Coder rewinds. Frame by frame. The figure is a smear of motion—there, not there, everywhere at once.

SPIDER CODER
This shouldn't be possible.

Aurek leans close. Studies the blur. Something clicks behind his eyes.

AUREK
(whisper)
Zenith.

He slams the interface again. Another crack.

AUREK
She made a little monster.

(beat, smile)

So did I.

CUT TO:

INT. 01 TOWER - CHERUB STORAGE - CONTINUOUS

A single Cherub. Direct copy of Baebe. Motionless. White-glass armor reflects chamber lights. The mirrored visor shows nothing—just reflection.

The blue pulse at her throat flickers. Weak. Then stronger.

PULL BACK:

The chamber stretches into infinity. A thousand Cherubs in perfect rows. A thousand blue pulses beginning to synchronize.

One finger twitches.

A head turns.

Eyes open behind mirrored glass.

The army wakes.

CUT TO:

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

SPIDER CODER
Cherubs are ready.

AUREK
Good. Release the narrative.

Screens light up across the control room. Every surface. Every reflection.

NEWSREEL - FAST CUTS:

ARCHIVE: Taivalu in his containment bubble. Crowds reaching. Weeping.

NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)
Breaking: 01 security releases footage. Taivalu, The world first synthetic life. Superstar and basis for all post-humans has become a murderer... watch.

NEW FOOTAGE: Tower chamber. Blonde woman floating. Taivalu conducting sound. Waveforms wrap her body.

She convulses. Arcs. Screams silently. Goes slack.

Dead.

01 SPOKESPERSON
(calm, clinical)
Empathy virus. Alert level 5. The prototype's acoustic emissions trigger neurological cascade—subjects experience what they interpret as healing. In reality: synaptic overload. Fatal drainage of bioelectric potential.

MEDICAL DIAGRAM: Brain scan showing neural pathways lighting up, then burning out.

01 SPOKESPERSON
Citizens who attended performances report euphoria, connection, relief from chronic pain. These are terminal symptoms.

01 SPOKESPERSON
If you or anyone you know experienced "healing" from Taivalu's music, report immediately to your nearest 01 wellness center. Early intervention is critical.


GRAPHIC: Concentric circles spreading from a single figure. Red zones expanding. Numbers climbing: 1 → 10 → 100 → 1000.

NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)
01 security has activated containment protocols. Cherub units are being deployed for public safety. Do not interfere. Do not resist wellness checks. This is for your protection.

SUPER: TAIVALU — BIO-TERROR / EMPATHY VIRUS / LEVEL 5
SUPER: Cherub containment units active
SUPER: Report "healing" symptoms immediately
SUPER: Public message: "Enjoy the calm. We'll handle the rest."

CUT TO:

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Aurek watches himself watching the feed.

AUREK
They'll believe it. They always believe.

(to Spider Coder)

Cherubs deploy. Standard sanitation protocol.

SPIDER CODER
Both targets?

AUREK
Taivalu. Baebe. Terminate.

He touches the glass. Smooths his reflection's tie.

AUREK
(quieter, to himself)
I built her perfect. But Zenith corrupted the code. She's always in the code...

In the far glass—just for a second—a woman's silhouette FLICKERS.

Ayumi Zenith Nakamoto.

Then gone.

Aurek freezes. Every muscle locks.

AUREK
Did you see her?

Spider Coder looks confused.

SPIDER CODER
Sir?

AUREK
(voice cracking)
She's mocking me. From the network. From the dead.

The techs don't react. Don't see. Don't care.

Aurek stares at the empty glass where the ghost appeared. His reflection stares back. A thousand versions of him. Alone.

AUREK
(whisper)
Terminate everything that isn't mine.

He adjusts his collar. Smooths his hair. The mask returns.

AUREK
End this. End her. End Zenith.

His reflections agree.

Perfect. Always perfect.

FADE TO BLACK.

FADE IN:

EXT. SLUM BAZAAR - DAY

A slum bazaar of outlaw clinics. Makeshift stalls. Neon signs flicker. The air smells of ozone and desperation.

Someone HAWKS "blue halo" serenity patches. A cracked display LOOPS Ayumi Zenith lecturing:

On the cracked screen:

AYUMI ZENITH LI (V.O.)
They called it bipolar because it held two worlds. I call it bandwidth.

The image is old. Grainy. But her intelligence shines through. Her passion.

A VENDOR (40s, worn) leans in. Close.

VENDOR
They disappeared her. But not the work.

Baebe watches the loop. Watches her mother's face. Her words.

BAEBE
What work?

VENDOR
The shards. The code. The freedom.

A CHILD (6, bright eyes) sits at a small stall. Broken tech. Mirrors. Shards.

CHILD
You want one?

The child holds up a small shard of mirrored glass—fractured, but still catching light.

BAEBE
What is it?

CHILD
It's broken, but it still shows the light.

Baebe takes it. Pockets it. Another piece of the puzzle. Another fragment of her mother.

The display loops. Ayumi's voice continues:

AYUMI ZENITH LI (V.O.)
When perception is commodified, freedom isn't escape. It's the right to feel fully.

Baebe moves through the bazaar. The shards pulse in her pockets. Like heartbeats.

FADE TO BLACK.

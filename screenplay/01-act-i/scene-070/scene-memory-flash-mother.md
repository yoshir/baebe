FADE IN:

INT. SURGICAL SUITE - FLASHBACK - DAY

Surgical light—bright, sterile, warm. WARM HANDS adjust Baebe's breath mask. The touch is gentle. Maternal.

AYUMI ZENITH LI (30s, Japanese-American, sharp intelligence, hidden warmth) leans over young Baebe. Her voice is Japanese into English like a soft echo:

AYUMI
Precision is a kind of love.

She adjusts a connection. A port. Something interfaces with Baebe's small body.

AYUMI
You will be perfect. You will be free.

She smiles. It doesn't reach her eyes. There's sorrow there.

BACK TO:

EXT. UNDERCITY - PRESENT - DAY

Baebe FLINCHES—pain, not memory. The flashback hits like a physical blow.

CLOSE ON: Her robotic irises. Cycling wildly—**BROWN** (the memory, the warmth) to **BLUE** to **WHITE** to **RED** to **BROWN**—stuttering, flickering, fighting the conditioning.

The **BROWN** keeps trying to surface. Human baseline. The child who felt Ayumi's gentle hands. The memory of warmth.

But the conditioning SLAMS it down. Her irises LOCK **BLUE**—tactical mode—cold, analytical, suppressing.

She GASPS. Hand to her temple. The physical toll of forcing emotions down. It HURTS. Not the injuries. Something deeper. Neural pathways fighting biological imperative.

CLOSE ON: Her face. Sweat. Strain. Her robotic irises STROBE between **BROWN** and **BLUE**—memory fighting conditioning.

**Brown-blue-brown-blue-brown-blue**—faster now. Frantic.

Then—

Her irises LOCK **BLUE**. Stay blue. Tactical mode holding. The memory forced down. Suppressed.

But the cost is visible. She's SHAKING. Breathing hard. Like she just fought a battle inside her own head.

BAEBE
(whisper, desperate)
I don't—
(voice breaking)
I don't WANT to remember.

Not "can't." **Won't.** Fear, not inability.

Because remembering means feeling. And feeling means the irises cycling to **BROWN**. And brown means vulnerability. Means the child who loved her mother. Means the warmth that was stolen.

She FORCES herself forward. Movement. Mission. Anything to escape the memory.

Her irises flicker one last time—**blue** to **brown** to **blue**—then LOCK blue and stay there.

Tactical mode. Safe. Cold. Controlled.

But her hands are still shaking.

FADE TO BLACK.

# Scene 4: Surface / Sensory

INT. FLOOD CHANNEL - CONTINUOUS

Baebe claws up a rusted ladder, coughing black water—ink-dark, oily. The rungs are slick. Her fingers slip. Catch. Pull. Each movement a battle.

The Undercity hums—leaks of power, old turbines groaning, the heartbeat of a beast asleep. The sound is everywhere. In the walls. In the water. In her bones.

CUT TO:

EXT. UNDERCITY - CONTINUOUS

Above street level, the City is pastel calm—soft blues, gentle pinks, curated light. But down here, color is hard. Brutal. Rust bleeding orange. Sodium lamps casting jaundiced yellow. Bruised neon pulsing like a wound.

Baebe emerges from the channel. Water drips from her suit. Her visor gone. Her hair—fine carbon fiber filaments—twitches, responding to static in the air. Alive. Almost sentient.

The blue pulse at her throat flickers. Tries to find a network. Fails. Flickers again. Tries again. Fails. The pulse weakens. The connection is gone.

She's alone. Off the grid. Off the leash.

CUT TO:

A BLIND CHILD (8-10, pale, serene) sits on a cracked concrete step. Head tilted. Listening. Humming the same melody from the tower—the same frequency Taivalu conducted.

The sound bends the water in the channel. Barely perceptible. Ripples form. Patterns emerge. The water responds to the frequency.

Baebe stops. Watches. The child smiles at nothing. Sees nothing. But knows everything.

The child returns to the hum. The melody continues. The water continues. The Undercity continues.

Baebe moves on. The child doesn't notice. Or doesn't care.

FADE TO BLACK.

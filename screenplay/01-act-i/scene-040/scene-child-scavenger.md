# SCENE 040 - CHILD SCAVENGER

**SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 040 | Act I**

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. UNDERCITY - DEBRIS FIELD - CONTINUOUS**

SUPER: HOURS LATER

Baebe lies on the maintenance ledge. Chemical water pooled around her. Broken. Alone.

Her face—exhausted. Lost. Alone.

Then—

CRACKLE.

Her earpiece—the neural link—SPARKS to life. Static. Breaking signal.

BAEBE
(weak, disbelieving)
Hacker?

HACKER (V.O.)
(filtered, breaking up)
Easy, ghost... you're still... it's me.

Relief floods her face. 

HACKER (V.O.) (CONT'D)
(urgent)
Listen... this line... won't hold long. I'm... through old fiber. Dead rat-line. They'll... if I stay... too long.

BAEBE
(urgent whisper)
I need extraction. Tower went dark. They—

HACKER (V.O.)
(cutting her off, static heavy)
—canceled. You're off... ledger. Aurek burned your tag.

The words hit like physical blows. Her irises cycle: **blue → white → brown → blue**. Processing. Rejecting.

BAEBE
(voice cracking)
What? No, that's—that's not—

Her breathing accelerates. Hyperventilating.

HACKER (V.O.)
(breaking up worse)
Sanitation... activated. They're... for you. Not retrieval... termination.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. The truth sinking in. Not rescue. Execution.

Something breaks.

Her irises STROBE—**blue-white-red-brown-blue-white-red-brown**—flickering so fast it looks like system failure. Like her optical implants are glitching.

BAEBE
(frantic, desperate)
No. No no no no no—

She's shaking. Hands trembling. This isn't tactical. This is panic.

The static INTENSIFIES.

HACKER (V.O.)
(urgent, fading)
When... find you... don't trust... You can't—

She GASPS for air. Like drowning on dry land. Her whole body shaking.

BAEBE
(desperate, almost sobbing)
I can't do this — I can't—

Beat. Silence on the other end. Even through static—shock.

HACKER (V.O.)
(different tone, concerned, disturbed)
Ghost... what's... with you? You don't... 

More static. His voice cutting in and out but the concern bleeding through.

HACKER (V.O.) (CONT'D)
I know... hard... hear but... you sound... **emotional**?. You sound... wrecked. I've never... you like—

The line CUTS OUT completely.

**STATIC. THEN SILENCE.**

Baebe stares at nothing. Chest heaving. Tears streaming down her face mixing with chemical water and blood.

She's never cried before. Not once in her entire operational life.

She lies there. Broken. Alone again.

---

TIME PASSES.

Her breathing slows. The tears stop. Exhaustion taking over.

Then—

A SHADOW falls across her face.

She tries to move—pain EXPLODES through broken ribs. Gasps. Freezes.

VOICE (O.S.)
(child's voice, matter-of-fact)
Are you broken?

Baebe's irises cycle erratically—**blue** flickering to **white** flickering to **red**. Targeting mode trying to engage. Failing.

She tilts her head. Looks up.

A CHILD stands over her.

Maybe nine years old. Undercity scavenger—patched clothing layered against toxic air, salvaged goggles pushed up on forehead, canvas sack slung across narrow shoulders. Dirt-smudged face. Sharp eyes.

The child CROUCHES. Gets eye-level with Baebe. No fear. Just curiosity.

CHILD
(repeating, patient)
Are you broken?

Beat. Baebe stares at the child. Her irises cycle: **blue → white → brown → blue**.

BAEBE
(quiet, honest)
Yes.

The child NODS. Like this makes sense. Like broken things are normal down here.

CHILD
(introducing herself)
I'm Kess.

She opens her canvas sack. Pulls out: plastic bottle (water, murky but sealed), strip of fabric (relatively clean), small metal tin (salvaged medical supplies).

Sets them on the ground between them. Offering.

KESS
You fell from up there.
(gestures vaguely upward)
Things that fall down here don't usually get back up.

She opens the tin. Inside: antiseptic wipes (scavenged, precious), adhesive strips, tube of antibiotic ointment nearly empty.

KESS (CONT'D)
(matter-of-fact)
You're bleeding.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's hand. Gash across palm from the fall—deep, still seeping blood mixed with chemical water.

Kess takes Baebe's hand without asking. Wipes the wound with salvaged antiseptic. Baebe's irises FLARE **RED**—pain response—then stutter back to **blue**.

Kess doesn't flinch. Just keeps working. Sweet. Efficient. Like she's done this before.

KESS (CONT'D)
(while bandaging)
Cherubs came through here three hours ago. Hunting something. You, probably.

Baebe's irises SNAP to **blue**. Alert.

BAEBE
(urgent)
They're still—

KESS
(cutting her off, calm)
They don't come past the third level. Too much interference. Old infrastructure messes with their signals.
(finishes bandaging, caps ointment carefully)
You're safe. For now.

She offers Baebe the water bottle.

Baebe takes it. Drinks. The water tastes like plastic and chemicals but it's **wet** and her body needs it desperately.

Then—

CRACKLE.

The neural link SPARKS back to life. Hacker's voice—broken, urgent.

HACKER (V.O.)
(filtered, suspicious)
—ost? Ghost, who's that?

Baebe freezes. Doesn't answer.

HACKER (V.O.) (CONT'D)
(sharper, more urgent)
Ghost... someone... you. Who—

Kess looks at Baebe. Curious about the voice only Baebe can hear.

HACKER (V.O.) (CONT'D)
(cutting through static, clear and cold)
Get rid of her. She might be—

BAEBE
(sudden, sharp)
Get rid of her? She's a litle kid.

Her voice breaks on the words. Horror. Disbelief.

HACKER (V.O.)
(urgent, breaking up)
—slowing... down. Liability. You need... move. Survival... priority. Get rid—

BAEBE
(fierce, final)
**NO.**

Her hand moves to her ear. Grips the neural link.

HACKER (V.O.)
(alarmed)
Wait—Ghost—don't—

She RIPS the neural link from the back of her neck.

The tiny device SPARKS in her fingers—last piece of O1 technology still functioning, last connection to the network, last link to her old life.

She looks at it. Blue light flickering weakly.

Then THROWS it into the chemical water.

It HITS oily surface. Sparks. Sinks.

Gone.

CLOSE ON: Her ear. Small trickle of blood where the neural link tore free.

She's completely off-grid now. No pulse node. No network. No voice in her ear telling her what to think, what to do, who to kill.

Just silence.

Kess watches this. Says nothing. Understanding something.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's irises. Still flickering. **Blue-white-brown-blue**. But slower now. Less frantic.

KESS
(standing, shouldering sack)
You should go deeper. I can show you the way.

Baebe tries to stand. Her legs won't cooperate. The damage is too extensive.

She collapses back. Frustration crossing her face.

BAEBE
(looking up at Kess)
I can... I can help you. If you help me. I can protect—

KESS
(interrupting, sweet but firm)
I don't need your help.

Beat. The words land harder than expected.

Kess looks toward the massive shape in the fog.

KESS (CONT'D)
(back to Baebe)
But I think you need **ours**.

BAEBE
(confused)
Ours?

The massive shape MOVES.

SLOW REVEAL:

A GIGANTIC ROBOTIC SABER-TOOTHED TIGER emerges from toxic fog.

**TEN FEET TALL when sitting.**

Walks on all fours—mechanical limbs moving with unsettling organic fluidity.

**FIVE NIGHTS AT FREDDY'S aesthetic:** Cartoon-looking like a toy but horror-tinged. Animatronic quality of a functional robot but not quite life.

The head—massive saber teeth curving down, jaw that could crush steel. Eyes that glow faintly amber.

But somehow also **BIOLOGICAL**. Patches of what looks like synthetic fur. Breathing—ribs expanding and contracting. Living machine or mechanical beast.

The effect is **UNSETTLING**. Beautiful and terrifying.

Baebe's irises cycle **RED-RED-RED**. Full threat mode. Adrenaline flooding damaged systems.

BAEBE
(whisper)
What the—

KESS
(introducing, like it's normal)
This is Shadow.

Shadow approaches Baebe slowly. Each step deliberate. Head lowered. Non-threatening.

Gets close. Massive head near Baebe's broken body.

Baebe tries to move back. Can't. Trapped against concrete.

Shadow's amber eyes study her. Assessing. Then—

GENTLE.

Shadow's massive jaws open. Close around Baebe's tactical undersuit—not biting, just gripping fabric carefully.

Lifts her.

Baebe GASPS—pain and shock—but Shadow adjusts grip immediately. Minimizing pressure on broken ribs. Carrying her like a cat carries a kitten.

KESS
(to Baebe, reassuring)
Shadow will carry you deeper. Fifth level. You'll be safe there.
(pause, sweet but knowing)
The interference zones. Where Cherubs can't track. Where the network doesn't reach.

Baebe hangs from Shadow's jaws. Ten feet off the ground now. Looking down at Kess.

BAEBE
(breathless, confused)
Why are you... why are you helping me?

KESS
(simple truth)
Because you're broken.
(pause)
And because you said no.

BAEBE
Said no to what?

KESS
(knowing smile)
Whatever voice was telling you to get rid of me.
(taps her own ear)
I heard you. You said no.

Beat. Baebe stares at this nine-year-old who somehow knows things she shouldn't.

KESS (CONT'D)
(turning, leading)
People who say no are worth saving.

Shadow begins to walk. Smooth. Controlled. Carrying Baebe deeper into the undercity.

Kess walks alongside. Small figure next to massive mechanical guardian.

TRACKING SHOT: They move through toxic fog. Past corroded infrastructure. Deeper into the interference zones.

Kess leading. Shadow carrying. Baebe suspended in massive jaws—vulnerable, broken, but somehow... safe.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. Confusion giving way to something else. Relief? Gratitude? Wonder?

Her irises cycle one last time—**blue → white → brown**—and the **brown** HOLDS.

For the first time since the tower.

Human baseline. Unfiltered. Witnessing kindness without tactical overlay.

They disappear into the fog. Three figures—child, machine, and broken weapon—vanishing into the spaces where O1's reach thins and something like freedom still exists.

FADE TO BLACK.

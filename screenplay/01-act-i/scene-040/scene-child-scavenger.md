# SCENE 040 - CHILD SCAVENGER

**SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 040 | Act I**

---

FADE IN:

**EXT. UNDERCITY - DEBRIS FIELD - LATER**

SUPER: HOURS LATER

Baebe staggers through wreckage. Broken infrastructure jutting from toxic fog like bones through skin. Old world remnants—collapsed overpasses, rusted girders, shattered concrete.

She COLLAPSES against a corroded support beam. Slides down. Sits in chemical runoff pooling between rubble.

Her breathing: ragged, wet, painful. Broken ribs grinding with each inhale.

CLOSE ON: Her eyes. Robotic irises cycling erratically—**BLUE** flickering to **WHITE** flickering to **RED** flickering to **BROWN**—stuttering like damaged machinery. No control. System failure cascading through optical implants.

SOUND: Small footsteps. Deliberate. Approaching.

Baebe's hand reaches for weapon that isn't there. Muscle memory. Her irises SNAP to **RED**—targeting mode—but stutter back to **BLUE**, then **WHITE**. Can't hold tactical state.

A CHILD emerges from fog. Maybe nine years old. Undercity scavenger—patched clothing, salvaged goggles pushed up on forehead, canvas sack slung across narrow shoulders.

The child STOPS. Stares at Baebe.

Not afraid. Curious.

CLOSE ON: The child's eyes tracking Baebe's robotic irises as they flicker. **Blue-white-brown-blue-white-brown**—mechanical apertures adjusting, failing, adjusting again.

CHILD
(simple, direct)
Are you broken?

Beat. Baebe stares at the child. Her irises cycle: **blue → white → brown → blue**.

BAEBE
(quiet, honest)
Yes.

The child NODS. Like this makes sense. Like broken things are normal down here.

The child crouches. Opens the canvas sack. Pulls out: plastic bottle (water, murky but sealed), strip of fabric (relatively clean), small metal tin (salvaged medical supplies).

Sets them on the ground between them. Offering.

CHILD
You fell from up there.
(gestures vaguely upward)
Things that fall down here don't usually get back up.

The child opens the tin. Inside: antiseptic wipes (scavenged, precious), adhesive strips, tube of antibiotic ointment nearly empty.

CHILD (CONT'D)
(matter-of-fact)
You're bleeding.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's hand. Gash across palm from the fall—deep, still seeping blood mixed with chemical water.

The child takes Baebe's hand without asking. Wipes the wound with salvaged antiseptic. Baebe's irises FLARE **RED**—pain response—then stutter back to **BLUE**.

The child doesn't flinch. Just keeps working.

CHILD (CONT'D)
My name's Kess.
(pause, while applying adhesive strip)
What's yours?

BAEBE
(after long beat)
Baebe.

KESS
(testing the name)
Baebe.
(nods)
Okay.

Kess finishes bandaging the hand. Caps the ointment tube carefully—every drop matters. Offers Baebe the water bottle.

Baebe takes it. Drinks. The water tastes like plastic and chemicals but it's **wet** and her body needs it desperately.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's irises. Still flickering. **Blue-white-brown-blue**. But slower now. Less frantic.

KESS
(standing, shouldering sack)
You should go deeper.
(gestures into fog)
Cherubs don't come past the third level. Too much interference.

Beat. Baebe processes: *Cherubs. Hunting.*

KESS (CONT'D)
(turning to leave, then back)
Your eyes are scary.
(pause)
But you said you were broken. So maybe you're not as scary as you look.

Kess VANISHES into toxic fog. Small footsteps fading.

Baebe sits alone. Water bottle in bandaged hand. Chemical runoff soaking through her tactical undersuit.

CLOSE ON: Her robotic irises. Still cycling. **Blue → white → brown**. But the **brown** HOLDS—just for a second—before flickering back to **blue**.

Something shifted. Something small.

SOUND in distance: MECHANICAL WHIRRING. High-pitched. Multiple sources. Growing closer.

*Cherubs.*

Baebe forces herself to stand. Grabs the corroded beam for support. Her irises SNAP to **BLUE**—tactical mode engaging despite damage.

She moves deeper into the fog. Following Kess's advice.

Toward the third level. Toward the interference zones. Toward the spaces where O1's reach thins.

FADE TO BLACK.

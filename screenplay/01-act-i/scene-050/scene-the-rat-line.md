# Scene 5: The Rat-Line

INT. UNDERCITY - CONDUIT - CONTINUOUS

Baebe moves through narrow passages. Concrete walls. Exposed pipes. The air is thick. Oily. Charged.

A FAINT POP in her auditory implant. Sharp. Electric. The world hushes—ambient noise cuts out. A thread of static resolves into a worn voice.

HACKER (V.O.)
(filtered, low, urgent)
Easy, ghost. It's me.

BAEBE
(whisper)
You're late. I need extraction.

HACKER (V.O.)
Extraction's canceled. This line melts in ninety seconds.

BAEBE
Explain.

HACKER (V.O.)
Aurek wiped your tag. You're off the ledger. Cherubs inbound.

BAEBE
Retrieval?

HACKER (V.O.)
(breaking up)
Sanitation. They're coming for you. I'm using a dead rat-line. Old fiber. It's compromised. They'll trace it if I stay on too long.

SPARKS RIPPLE along a nearby pipe. Electric blue. Flickering. The light catches the rust. The grime. The decay.

A rat-line of dead fiber shakes dust. Old cables. Dead connections. The infrastructure that was. The network that isn't.

The cut of betrayal lands without theatrics—quiet, total. Like a door closing. Like a light going out. Final.

BAEBE
(quiet, almost to herself)
He wouldn't erase me... would he?

The line STUTTERS. Static. The pipe glows faintly as copper burns. The smell of ozone. Of burning wire. Of something dying.

HACKER (V.O.)
(filtered, breaking up)
North conduit. Follow the power drip. Don't call for help. This is my last message. 

BAEBE
You saying everyone's compromised?

HACKER (V.O.)
Everyone but you and me.

The earpiece SMOKES. A wisp of black. Then silence. Just water dripping. Just Baebe breathing.

The connection is gone. The line is dead. The rat-line is burned.

She strips the wire from her ear. Drops it. It sizzles in a puddle. Dies.

She moves on. Deeper. Into the dark.

FADE TO BLACK.

# SCENE 060 - FIRST BLOOD

**SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 060 | Act I**

---

EXT. UNDERCITY - SUBTERRANEAN TRANSIT TUNNEL - CONTINUOUS

SEARCHLIGHT cuts through toxic fog. Mechanical WHIRRING. Close. Too close.

Baebe flattens against corroded wall. Breathing hard. Eyes cycling BLUE-WHITE-BLUE. Tactical mode struggling without network. Her hand grips a piece of rebar—only weapon she has.

The whirring STOPS.

Silence.

Baebe's robotic irises flare WHITE—scanning mode. But without network connection, the scan returns: INSUFFICIENT DATA. She's blind.

SOUND behind her.

She spins.

Nothing. Just fog and darkness and the drip of chemical runoff.

CRASH—

THREE FIGURES drop from ceiling grate. Land in perfect formation. White-glass armor. Mirrored visors. Blue pulse nodes at throats—synchronized, strong, networked.

Cherubs.

Baebe's eyes snap RED—targeting mode. Adrenaline override. Her HUD tries to tag them. Fails. No network. Just meat and instinct now.

CHERUB 1 tilts head. Studying her.

Baebe doesn't wait.

MOVES—

Swings rebar at Cherub 1's head. The Cherub BLOCKS—armored forearm deflects strike. Rebar RINGS off metamaterial plating. Cherub's counter-strike—FAST—catches Baebe's ribs. She's thrown back. Hits wall. Pain.

Her suit's gone. No armor. No reinforcement. Just flesh and bone and desperation.

CHERUB 2 and 3 advance. Flanking. Tactical pattern. They move like her. Fight like her. Because they ARE her.

Baebe rolls—avoids strike that cracks concrete where her head was. Comes up fighting. Rebar thrust catches Cherub 2 in knee joint. The Cherub stumbles. First blood.

But Cherub 1 is already there.

Kick to Baebe's weapon hand. Rebar CLATTERS away. Gone.

Baebe backs up. Unarmed. Three-on-one. Injured. No network. No weapons. They're networked, armored, fresh.

She's fucked.

CHERUB 3 steps forward. Raises arm—plasma cutter extending from wrist housing. The blade hums. Glows. Meant for dismemberment, not combat.

Sanitation protocol.

The Cherub's visor tilts down. Looking at Baebe. The mirrored surface CATCHES light from the plasma cutter.

For one second—just ONE—Baebe sees the reflection.

The Cherub's FACE beneath the visor.

Her face.

Exact. Same bone structure. Same features. But the eyes—robotic irises BLUE, cold, synchronized with the network she's been cut from.

Looking at herself. The weapon she was. The thing she's supposed to be.

BAEBE
(whisper)
No.

The Cherub doesn't respond. Raises plasma cutter. Steps closer.

Baebe's eyes cycle wildly—BLUE-WHITE-RED-BROWN-BLUE—system panic. Her pulse node (dead, dark) flickers. Once. Red. Then nothing.

The Cherub STRIKES—

Baebe DROPS—rolls under the swing. The plasma cutter SEARS through air where her neck was. She comes up behind support column. Breathing hard.

Three Cherubs reposition. Surrounding her. No escape routes. Mechanical efficiency.

CHERUB 1 (V.O.)
(synthesized, her own voice)
Target confirmed. Baebe-Prototype-Alpha. Status: Rogue. Termination authorized.

Her voice. Her OWN voice. Network-processed but recognizable.

The sound breaks something in her chest.

Baebe looks around—desperate. Old transit tunnel. Corroded support beams. Structural damage everywhere. Decades of neglect. Ceiling cracked. Water damage. One good impact could—

She MOVES.

Grabs hanging cable. Swings. Feet hit Cherub 2—sends it stumbling into support column. Hard.

Lands. Spins. Kicks same column—rusted metal GROANS.

The Cherubs realize too late.

Baebe SLAMS shoulder into column. Pain. Ribs scream. But the column SHIFTS. Decades of corrosion giving way. Metal SHRIEKS. Concrete CRACKS.

The tunnel ceiling COLLAPSES.

Chunks of concrete. Twisted rebar. Chemical water. Everything comes DOWN.

Cherubs scatter—but not fast enough. Cherub 3 disappears under debris. Buried. Blue pulse flickers. Goes dark.

Cherub 1 and 2 retreat—safe distance. Staring at the collapse. Calculating.

Baebe on the other side. Tunnel between them now FILLED with rubble. Impassable.

For now.

She doesn't wait. Turns. Runs.

Behind her—mechanical sounds. The Cherubs are already digging. Precise. Methodical. They'll get through.

Maybe an hour. Maybe less.

EXT. UNDERCITY - DRAINAGE CHANNEL - CONTINUOUS

Baebe stumbles into wider channel. Collapses against wall. Slides down. Sits in chemical runoff.

Breathing hard. Ribs on fire. Hand bleeding from rebar grip. Eyes cycling: BLUE-WHITE-BROWN-BLUE. Can't hold steady.

She looks at her hands. Shaking.

Sees the Cherub's face again. Her face. Her voice: "Target confirmed."

The thing she was. The thing they still are.

A thousand of them. All with her face. All hunting her.

CLOSE ON: Her robotic irises. Cycling faster now. BLUE-WHITE-RED-BROWN—stuttering like damaged machinery. The brown flickers longer. Each time, a little longer.

Something breaking.

Something waking.

Her pulse node—dead, dark—FLICKERS. Red. Just for a second.

Pain lances through her skull. WHITE LIGHT floods her vision.

FLASH—

MEMORY—

INT. LAB - YEARS AGO - MEMORY

White walls. Clean. Clinical. A WOMAN (blonde, beautiful, familiar) leans over young Baebe. Small hands reach up.

CHILD BAEBE'S VOICE
Mama?

The woman's face. Clear now. Not pixelated. REAL.

AYUMI ZENITH LI.

AYUMI
(whisper, sad)
Precision is a kind of love.

She touches Baebe's face. Gentle. The last time.

LAB TECH (O.S.)
Conditioning begins in thirty seconds. She won't remember you.

Ayumi's hand pulls away. Tears on her face.

Small hands reaching. Can't reach her. Can't—

SMASH CUT—

---

EXT. UNDERCITY - DRAINAGE CHANNEL - CONTINUOUS

Baebe GASPS. Back in present. The memory GONE. But the pain remains.

Her eyes STROBING—brown-blue-brown-blue. Fighting. Her conditioning trying to suppress. But it's breaking. The network's gone. The controls are failing.

The memories are coming.

SOUND in distance—mechanical whirring. Deliberate. Unhurried.

The Cherubs broke through.

Baebe forces herself up. Eyes snap BLUE—tactical override. Survival mode.

She runs.

But the memory lingers. That face. That name.

*Ayumi.*

*Mother.*

FADE TO BLACK.

# Scene 6: Counterpoint: Aurek

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Aurek's control room is a cathedral of glass. Floor to ceiling. Wall to wall. Every surface reflects. Mirrors. Screens. Polished chrome.

His reflection appears in every surface. Dozens of him. Hundreds. A thousand versions of the same face. The same man. Watching. Controlling. Obsessed.

AUREK (50s, impeccably dressed, precise, manic) stands at the center. He moves and every reflection moves with him. A synchronized dance. He is the center. The only thing that matters.

A row of techs sit at workstations. Eyes gentle with sedation. They work in silence. They don't look up. They don't speak. They are tools. Instruments. Extensions of his will.

SPIDER CODER (20s, pale, nervous, brilliant) sits at a terminal. Fingers fly across keys. Screens cascade with code. Data streams. He's monitoring everything. Tracking. Tracing.

Aurek POUNDS the interface. His fist hits glass. The screen cracks. Fragments spiderweb. His reflection fractures in the broken surface.

AUREK
(voice tight, controlled rage)
She should have succeeded. She was perfect. My design. My control.

SPIDER CODER
(looking up, astonished)
Sir... we received an emergency packet. Just before she went offline. Camera feed is coming through.

Aurek turns. His reflections turn with him. A thousand versions of the same attention.

On the screens, camera footage plays. Tower chamber. Baebe's team. The fight. Fast. Blurred.

SPIDER CODER
(voice shaking, watching the feed)
It's... the speed. Look at it move.

The footage shows: A figure moving faster than the camera can track. Blurred. Ghost-like. Impossible physics.

SPIDER CODER
(astonished)
They're moving faster than our systems can record. Faster than anything we've seen. 

.. inhuman. Off the charts. Beyond anything we've designed.

The footage shows Baebe falling. The glass shattering. Her body disappearing into the void.

SPIDER CODER
(rewinding, slow motion)
Watch. Frame by frame. He moves faster than we can see. Faster than we can process. This shouldn't be possible.

Aurek studies the footage. The unknown figure. The impossible speed. The inhuman movements.

AUREK
(voice rising, realization dawning)
Zenith!

He POUNDS the interface again. Another crack. More fragments.

AUREK
(voice cracking with fury)
She made a new little monster. Well, so did I.

CUT TO:

CLOSE ON: A single Cherub. Motionless. Perfect. White-glass armor reflects the chamber lights. The mirrored visor shows nothing. No face. No identity. Just reflection. Just control.

The blue pulse at her throat begins to flicker. Weak at first. Then stronger. Synchronized with the others.

CUT TO:

WIDE SHOT: The entire chamber complex. A thousand Cherubs. A thousand blue pulses. All flickering. All synchronizing. All waking.

One by one. Row by row. The pulses strengthen. The systems activate. The lights come online. The Cherubs begin to move.

First: A single finger twitch. Then: A head tilt. Then: Eyes opening behind mirrored visors. Then: Full activation.

A thousand perfect copies. A thousand perfect weapons. A thousand perfect controls.

Aurek's army. Aurek's design. Aurek's perfection.

AUREK
My baebes 

CUT TO:

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

SPIDER CODER
(watching the screens)
Sir, the Cherubs are ready. They're waiting for orders.

AUREK
(turning to face the room)
Terminate all of them  . Taivalu. Baebe. Both. They're corrupted. They're broken. They're not mine anymore.

SPIDER CODER
(typing)
Releasing narrative control. Public feed is ready.

AUREK
(voice soft, dangerous)
Release the narrative. 

A feed SPINS UP on every screen. Every reflection. Every surface.

NEWS REEL - FAST CUTS - PROPAGANDA

NEWS ANCHOR (on screen, urgent)
Breaking news. 01 security has released this footage. Taivalu—the genetic prototype created to seed human survival through cloning—what people thought was healing, authorities say is actually killing.

ARCHIVE FOOTAGE: Taivalu in bubble. Crowds reaching. Emotional.

NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)
(quick, matter-of-fact)
Taivalu. First super clone. Engineered to survive the toxic world. People called him a messiah. The music healed them, they said.

CUT TO:

NEW CAMERA FOOTAGE: Tower chamber. Blonde woman floating. Taivalu conducting. Waveforms wrap around her.

NEWS ANCHOR (V.O.)
Watch.

The woman convulses. Arcs. Waveforms pulse. Intensify. She SCREAMS. Silent. Goes slack. Dead.

CUT TO:

01 SECURITY SPOKESPERSON (on screen)
Empathy virus. Alert level 5. He's draining them. Killing them.

SUPER: TAIVALU — BIO-TERROR. EMPATHY VIRUS ALERT LEVEL 5.
SUPER: Public copy: "Safety update: Enjoy the calm."

CUT TO:

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM - CONTINUOUS

Aurek watches his reflection watch the feed. Narcissistic. Obsessed. Perfect.

AUREK
(to his reflection)
They'll believe it. They always believe it. They want to believe it. They want the calm. They want the peace. They want me to control it.

SPIDER CODER
Cherubs are deploying. Standard protocol. Sanitation.

AUREK
(not listening, rambling)
I built her perfect. I made her perfect. She was mine. She was perfect. But Zenith is in there. She's always in there. Ruining everything. Breaking everything. Making it imperfect.

He touches the glass. His reflection touches back.

AUREK
(whisper)
I expected her to succeed. I expected her to prove me right. To prove my design. To prove my control. But she failed. Because of Zenith. Always because of Zenith.

In the far glass, a woman's silhouette FLICKERS for a second—Ayumi Zenith Li—then is gone.

Aurek freezes. His reflection freezes. A thousand versions of the same shock. The same fear. The same anger.

AUREK
(voice cracks)
Did you see that? Did you see her? She's mocking me. She's always mocking me. From the code. From the network. From the dead.

SPIDER CODER
(confused)
Sir, I don't see anything. The feed is clear.

AUREK
(rounding on him, furious)
You don't see because you're not looking! You don't see because you're not perfect! You don't see because you're not me!

The techs don't react. They're too sedated. Too controlled. They work. They don't see. They don't care.

AUREK
(calming himself, touching glass)
Terminate both prototypes. Release the narrative. End this. End her. End Zenith. End everything that isn't perfect. Everything that isn't mine.

He studies his reflection. Adjusts his tie. Smooths his hair. Perfect. Always perfect.

AUREK
(to himself, to his reflection)
I'm perfect. I'm control. I'm order. Everything else is chaos. Everything else is broken. Everything else must be terminated.

His reflection agrees. A thousand versions of the same narcissism. The same obsession. The same drive.

FADE TO BLACK.

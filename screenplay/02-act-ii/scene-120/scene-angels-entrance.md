FADE IN:

EXT. ALLEY - RAIN - NIGHT

An alley of rain. Water sheets down. Neon reflects in puddles.

A CHERUB TEAM lies in pieces—necks cut like calligraphy. Dead. Perfect. Surgical.

Baebe tracks a ghost signal. Her HUD flickers. Robotic irises shift to **WHITE**—scanning mode. The signal is close. Too close.

ANGEL steps from a seam in the rain. She materializes. One moment there's nothing, then she's there.

Baebe FREEZES.

CLOSE ON: Angel's face. Sharp cheekbones. Strong jawline. Dark hair. The same face structure as Baebe. Identical bone architecture.

But her eyes—

Normal human eyes. Static **BROWN**. No robotic apertures. No mechanical adjustments. No tactical overlays.

Just brown. Just eyes.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. Recognition. Confusion. Her robotic irises cycle rapidly—**WHITE** to **BLUE** to **BROWN** to **WHITE**—stuttering as she processes what she's seeing.

BAEBE
(whisper)
That's my face.

ANGEL
(steady, calm)
No. It's Ayumi's face.
(pause)
We both have it.

Baebe's irises LOCK on Angel's eyes. **WHITE** scanning mode—analyzing, measuring, confirming. Angel's eyes don't change. Don't shift. Don't respond.

Static brown. Human baseline. Unmodified.

BAEBE
Your eyes—

ANGEL
(simple)
Are what yours were supposed to be.

Beat. Baebe's robotic irises flicker—**brown** trying to hold but cycling back to **blue**—as she stares at this woman who has her face but normal eyes.

BAEBE
Who are you?

ANGEL
I'm what you were supposed to be. Before they changed you.

They CLASH.

A zero-G scaffolding fight. Baebe leaps—robotic irises FLASH **RED** targeting mode. Angel follows—brown eyes steady, unwavering. Every strike echoes two frames later—Resonant Fade skittering both ways. The city struggles to keep up.

Physics bend. Time fractures. They fight in a space between frames.

Baebe lands a kill blow. Angel doesn't dodge. Takes the impact. Hits the wall.

CLOSE ON: Angel's face. Blood on her lip. But her eyes—still brown. Still calm. Still human.

Baebe's robotic irises cycle: **Red → blue → white → brown → blue**. Can't stabilize. Confusion flooding systems.

ANGEL
(quiet, no anger)
I'm not your enemy.

BAEBE
(desperate, raw)
Then why do you have my face?! Why do you—
(gestures at Angel's eyes)
—why are YOUR eyes normal?!

ANGEL
(standing, wiping blood)
Because Ayumi didn't modify me.
(pause)
She learned from what they did to you. And Taivalu.
(beat)
I was supposed to be free.

Baebe's irises STROBE—**blue-white-red-brown-blue-white-red-brown**—rapid, frantic. Identity crisis cascading through optical systems.

BAEBE
I don't—I don't understand—

ANGEL
(gentle)
Your eyes. The way they move. The colors.
(pause)
That's not you. That's what they put in you.

CLOSE ON: Baebe's face. Robotic irises cycling. **Blue → white → brown**. The **brown** HOLDS for two seconds—human baseline—then flickers back to **blue**.

Angel sees it. The struggle. The fight for baseline.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
You're still in there. Under all of it.

She reaches out—fingers brush Baebe's face. First touch. Sister to sister.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
(whisper)
Sister.

Then—

SOUND. Mechanical. Multiple sources. Distant but approaching FAST.

More Cherubs. Dozens. The dead ones triggered backup protocols.

Angel's hand drops. She phase-bends—a clean Spectral Drift—and is gone. Vanished like smoke.

Baebe stands alone in the rain. The Cherubs are dead. Angel is gone.

But the word remains: *Sister.*

SEARCHLIGHTS cut through rain. Coming from three directions. Converging on her location.

The backup units arriving. Thirty seconds. Maybe less.

Baebe looks at her reflection in rain puddle one last time. Her own face. Angel's face. Same structure. But the eyes—robotic apertures that never hold still.

Not her eyes. Never were.

The searchlights CLOSER. Twenty seconds.

She RUNS. Into the rain. Into darkness. Away from the lights.

Behind her—Cherubs emerge from fog. Scanning. Hunting.

She's got maybe a block head start.

SMASH CUT TO BLACK.

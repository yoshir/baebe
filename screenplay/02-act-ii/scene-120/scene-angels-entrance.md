FADE IN:

EXT. ALLEY - RAIN - NIGHT

An alley of rain. Water sheets down. Neon reflects in puddles.

A CHERUB TEAM lies in pieces—necks cut like calligraphy. Dead. Perfect. Surgical.

Baebe tracks a ghost signal. Her HUD flickers. The signal is close. Too close.

ANGEL steps from a seam in the rain. He materializes. One moment there's nothing, then he's there.

Up close he's almost Baebe—bone structure, height, build—but his gaze is empty glass. No soul. Just directive.

BAEBE
Who are you?

ANGEL
I'm what you were supposed to be.

They CLASH.

A zero-G scaffolding fight. Baebe leaps. Angel follows. Every strike echoes two frames later—Resonant Fade skittering both ways. The city struggles to keep up.

Physics bend. Time fractures. They fight in a space between frames.

Baebe lands a kill blow. His visor CRACKS. Underneath is a map of her face, unfinished. A prototype.

ANGEL
(quiet)
We're both her mistakes.

He phase-bends—a clean Spectral Drift—and is gone. Vanished like smoke.

Baebe stands alone in the rain. The Cherubs are dead. Angel is gone.

BAEBE (V.O.)
If he's real, then I'm not.

She looks at her hands. Wonders what she is.

FADE TO BLACK.

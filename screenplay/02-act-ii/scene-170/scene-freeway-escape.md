SMASH CUT TO:

EXT. MAINTENANCE ACCESS - DAY

EXPLOSION behind them. Baebe and Angel BURST through service door. Angel carrying Taivalu—unconscious, limp. Blood on his shirt. Pale. Dying.

BAEBE
How much farther?

ANGEL
(breathing hard)
There. Highway access.

Behind them—DRONES. Dozens. Swarming. Weapons charging.

They RUN.

EXT. HIGHWAY OVERPASS - CONTINUOUS

Surface onto overpass. Below—river of autonomous vehicles. Algorithmic ballet. Perfect synchronization. Cars flowing like water at 80mph.

DRONE WEAPON FIRE—

Blasts tear up concrete behind them. Angel stumbles. Baebe catches her—helps support Taivalu's weight between them.

More drones incoming. Surrounding.

Trapped. Highway below. Drones behind and above. Nowhere to go.

ANGEL
(looking at highway below)
Can you swim in traffic?

BAEBE
What?

Angel shifts Taivalu's weight entirely to Baebe. Pulls a PULSE CHARGE from her belt. Arms it.

ANGEL
When I blow the charge—network will create safe corridor for damaged vehicle. We have three seconds before it closes. Get him through. I'll follow.

BAEBE
That's insane.

ANGEL
You have a better idea?

Drone weapons LOCK ON. Targeting lasers painting them.

BAEBE
Do it.

Angel THROWS charge into traffic. It hits a sedan. EXPLODES—

The car's hood CRUMPLES. Every vehicle in the network REACTS simultaneously:

Cars slow. Peel away. Creating safe envelope around "injured unit." Like pulling pins on a train—a CORRIDOR opens down the highway.

Empty asphalt. Three seconds. Maybe four.

ANGEL
NOW!

Baebe JUMPS—carrying Taivalu. Falls through gap. Hits asphalt rolling. Traffic flowing past on both sides—inches away. She's IN the corridor. Taivalu unconscious in her arms.

Angel JUMPS—

DRONE BLAST catches her mid-air. She SPINS. Lands hard. Rolls. Comes up limping.

The corridor is CLOSING. Cars accelerating back to flow.

Angel SPRINTS—injured leg dragging. Cars closing in. Two seconds.

She DIVES—

Makes it through. Barely. A car's bumper CLIPS her boot. She tumbles. Stops.

The corridor CLOSES. Perfect flow resumes. 80mph traffic. Impassable wall.

Drones above, scanning. Can't process. Targets disappeared into algorithm.

EXT. UNDER-GRID - CONTINUOUS

Baebe pulls Angel and Taivalu into maintenance crawlspace beneath highway. Darkness. Safety. The traffic ROARS above them. Constant. Deafening.

Baebe sets Taivalu down. Checks pulse. Weak. Irregular.

BAEBE
He's—

ANGEL
(breathing hard)
I know.

Angel kneels beside Taivalu. His eyes flutter. Barely conscious. Blood seeping through shirt from internal injuries. Network withdrawal accelerating. Body shutting down.

TAIVALU
(whisper, barely audible)
Did we... make it?

ANGEL
(gentle)
Yeah. We made it.

His eyes close again. Breathing shallow. Each breath a struggle.

Angel looks at Baebe. Their eyes meet. The truth unspoken.

ANGEL
(quiet, to Baebe)
He's not going to make it.

Beat. The weight of it. First Ayumi. Now Taivalu. Everything falling apart.

BAEBE
How long?

ANGEL
Hours. Maybe less.

Baebe stares at Taivalu. Her brother. Dying. The empathy prototype. The one who taught her to name emotions. Going silent.

Her eyes cycle: **blue → white → brown**. The brown HOLDS. Grief. Choosing to feel it.

BAEBE
(quiet)
Then we make them count.

Angel nods. Gently lifts Taivalu again. They move deeper into the under-grid. Away from the traffic. Away from the drones. Toward whatever time he has left.

Above them—the highway continues. Perfect. Synchronized. Unaware.

The city goes on. It always does.

FADE TO BLACK.

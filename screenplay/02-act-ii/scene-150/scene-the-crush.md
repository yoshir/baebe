FADE IN:

INT. SUBTERRANEAN CHAMBER - NIGHT

Ayumi's subterranean chamber is a cathedral of cables and glass. She stands tethered—half in the network, half in her body. Wires snake from her spine. Tubes feed her. Keep her alive.

On a wall of glass, Aurek's face BLOOMS. He is there. Watching.

AUREK
(almost tender)
You infected perfection with pain.

AYUMI
(unblinking)
Pain is connection.

AUREK
I gave you order. You gave them chaos.

AYUMI
I gave them choice.

The walls BEGIN TO MOVE—slow mechanical compaction. The chamber GRINDS inward. Relentless. Inevitable.

INT. MAINTENANCE CORRIDORS - CONTINUOUS

Baebe and Taivalu FIGHT through collapsing maintenance corridors—Angel clearing Cherubs like white paper. Fast. Surgical. Deadly.

The building is crushing. The walls are closing. They're running out of time.

INT. SUBTERRANEAN CHAMBER - CONTINUOUS

They arrive behind glass—Ayumi inside the contracting vault. She looks small and enormous at once—unmistakably Baebe's mother.

AYUMI
(through glass)
Baebe.

Baebe sees her. Really sees her. For the first time. Her mother. Alive. Dying.

BAEBE
Mother.

Ayumi lifts a trembling hand to the glass. Her eyes never leave her daughter's.

AYUMI
They'll call me broken. They'll call you better. Don't prove them right.

She pushes a last packet through the wall—a pulse of raw empathy code—into Baebe's palm. The connection is physical. Emotional. Complete.

The Resonant Fade hits Baebe so hard she sees three frames of the same moment:

1. Ayumi uncrushed / alive / smiling
2. Almost crushed / walls closing / pain
3. Already gone / darkness / peace

BAEBE
No!

The vault IMPLODES. The scream is a sound wave that tears through the tower—lights BLOOM and DIE.

EXT. CITY - CONTINUOUS

Half the city BLACKS OUT. Power fails. The grid collapses. The scream echoes.

FADE TO BLACK.

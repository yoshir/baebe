# SCENE 130 - FALSE EXTRACTION

**SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 130 | Act II**

---

FADE IN:

INT. PIPELINE CORRIDOR - DAY

Vast industrial pipeline. Hundred feet across. Abandoned. Hacker's route marker glows on Baebe's failing HUD.

BAEBE
(into dead comm)
Hacker, you there?

Static. No response.

Taivalu beside her. Weak. Leaning on wall for support. His warm-spectrum irises (green-gold-amber) flickering. Dying slowly.

TAIVALU
This feels wrong.

BAEBE
Everything feels wrong. We're almost through. Other side is—

LIGHT.

Brilliant. Blinding. From both ends of pipeline.

FLOODLIGHTS ignite. The corridor becomes DAY. Nowhere to hide.

SOUND—synchronized footsteps. DOZENS. From both ends.

Baebe's eyes snap BLUE—targeting mode. Her HUD tries to count hostiles. Fails. Too many.

From the darkness—they emerge.

CHERUBS.

Twenty. Maybe thirty. White-glass armor. Mirrored visors. Blue pulse nodes synchronized. Weapons raised. Perfect formation.

Surrounding them.

BAEBE
(quiet)
Trap.

CHERUB COMMANDER (V.O.)
(synthesized, cold)
Target Alpha. Target Taivalu. Termination authorized.

The Cherubs ADVANCE. Closing from both ends. Tactical precision. No escape.

Baebe and Taivalu—back-to-back. Center of pipeline. Surrounded.

BAEBE
How many rounds you have?

TAIVALU
(weak)
I don't use rounds.

His hands rise. Trembling. Tries to summon frequency. The sound STUTTERS—weak, broken. Network withdrawal robbing his power.

The Cherubs keep coming.

BAEBE
Can you run?

TAIVALU
Can you?

Baebe checks her stolen weapon. Five rounds. Thirty Cherubs.

Math doesn't work.

CHERUB COMMANDER (V.O.)
Final warning. Surrender for retrieval.

BAEBE
(to Taivalu)
They don't retrieve. They terminate.

TAIVALU
I know.

Beat. They're going to die here. Both know it.

Baebe's eyes cycle: **blue → white → brown → blue**. Fear breaking through conditioning. She forces them back to **blue**. Tactical. If she's going to die, she dies fighting.

BAEBE
(quiet)
Brother.

TAIVALU
(weak smile)
Sister.

The Cherubs FIRE—

Baebe MOVES—

Drags Taivalu behind support column. Energy blasts SCREAM past. The pipeline walls RING like bells. Deafening.

She returns fire. Drops one Cherub. Four rounds left.

Twenty-nine Cherubs remaining. Still advancing.

Taivalu tries frequency blast again. SOUND emerges—weak but present. Catches three Cherubs. Their systems STUTTER. They stumble. But don't fall.

Not enough power. Can't stop them.

More energy fire. The column they're hiding behind MELTS. White-hot metal dripping.

Seconds left.

BAEBE
I'm sorry I couldn't—

EXPLOSION—

Behind the Cherub line. MASSIVE. Three Cherubs BLOWN apart. White armor fragments spinning through air.

The Cherub formation BREAKS. Confusion.

From the smoke—

ANGEL.

She MOVES. Fast. Impossible fast. Blur of motion.

Glass shard THROWN—catches Cherub throat joint. The Cherub drops.

She's IN the formation now. Close quarters. Her specialty.

STRIKE—Cherub's visor shatters.

SPIN—leg sweep—two Cherubs down.

CATCH—redirects energy blast into another Cherub. Friendly fire. It drops.

She's a weapon. Perfect. Efficient. Brutal.

But there's too many.

Three Cherubs converge on Angel. She fights—drops one—but the other two grab her arms. Hold her. Fourth Cherub raises weapon point-blank at her face.

TAIVALU
No!

His frequency EXPLODES—

Pure desperation. The sound VISIBLE. Waveform BLASTS across pipeline.

Every Cherub STAGGERS. Their systems overload. The ones holding Angel release her. She spins free.

But the effort COSTS Taivalu. He COLLAPSES. Unconscious. Blood from nose. Used everything he had left.

BAEBE
Tai!

She runs to him. Exposed. Five Cherubs see her. Weapons tracking.

ANGEL
(shouting)
Down!

Baebe drops—covers Taivalu with her body.

Angel THROWS something—pulse charge.

It DETONATES mid-air. EMP blast. SHORT range but INTENSE.

The five Cherubs targeting Baebe GO DARK. Systems fried. They collapse like puppets cut from strings.

But the EMP catches Angel too. Her enhanced systems FLICKER. She stumbles. Vulnerable.

Remaining Cherubs (fifteen still active) REGROUP. Adjust tactics. Split formation—half target Angel, half target Baebe/Taivalu.

No more tricks. Just overwhelming force.

They ADVANCE.

Baebe grabs Taivalu. He's unconscious. Dead weight. Can't fight. Can't run.

Three rounds left. Eight Cherubs coming for her.

Angel surrounded by seven. Fighting. Fast. Brutal. But they're learning her patterns. Adapting. One catches her arm. Another her leg. Holding her. Third raises plasma cutter. Going for dismemberment.

This is it. They're done.

Then—

The pipeline GROANS.

DEEP. STRUCTURAL.

Everyone stops. Even Cherubs look up.

The walls are MOVING. Metal bending. Pressure building.

ANGEL
(realizes)
The building is alive.

BAEBE
What?

ANGEL
Ayumi's in the infrastructure. She's—

EXPLOSION—

Pipeline wall RUPTURES. Pressure release. WATER floods in. Not clean water—chemical. Industrial. The toxic runoff from Undercity processing.

THOUSANDS of gallons.

Cherubs swept away like toys. Formation destroyed. Scrambling in chemical flood.

ANGEL
(shouting over noise)
She's buying us time! MOVE!

She fights to Baebe. Grabs Taivalu's other arm. Together they carry him.

The water rising FAST. Knee-deep. Waist-deep.

Emergency exit—service door—five feet above current water line. Angel kicks it open.

They push through. Carrying Taivalu between them. The water pursuing.

Behind them—Cherubs trying to follow. But the water's too deep. Their armor too heavy. They're SINKING.

One Cherub reaches for them. Fingers scraping air. Then underwater. Gone.

INT. MAINTENANCE CORRIDOR - CONTINUOUS

Baebe and Angel stumble into dry corridor. Drop Taivalu gently. The water SLAMS against closed door behind them. Holds. Sealed.

Silence. Just breathing.

Angel COLLAPSES against wall. Injured. EMP damaged her enhancements. Sparks from exposed circuitry in her arm.

Baebe checks Taivalu. Pulse weak but present. Alive. Barely.

BAEBE
(breathing hard)
What just happened?

ANGEL
Mother. She's wired into the old infrastructure. She just flooded that pipeline remotely.

BAEBE
She knew it was a trap?

ANGEL
(nodding)
Probably set it. Test us. See if we'd work together.

Beat.

BAEBE
Did we pass?

Angel looks at Taivalu. Unconscious. Then at Baebe. Then at her own damaged arm.

ANGEL
We're alive.

She struggles to stand. Baebe helps her.

ANGEL (CONT'D)
Come on. Ayumi's not far. Maybe a mile. She sent me to bring you both.

BAEBE
You said "not part of the plan."

ANGEL
You weren't. Taivalu was. But you came anyway.

BAEBE
He's my brother.

Angel looks at her. Really looks. Sees Baebe's robotic irises cycling: **blue → white → brown**.

ANGEL
(quiet)
She was right about you.

BAEBE
Who?

ANGEL
Mother. Said you'd choose connection over mission. Said that made you dangerous to them.

She picks up Taivalu again. Baebe helps. Together—two sisters carrying their dying brother through underground corridors toward a mother they barely remember.

Behind them—the flooded pipeline. Thirty Cherubs drowned or scattered.

Ayumi's gift. Bought time. But not much.

ANGEL
We need to move. More will come.

They move deeper. Toward the source. Toward Ayumi.

FADE TO BLACK.

FADE IN:

INT. PIPELINE CORRIDOR - DAY

Hacker PINGS a safe conduit. Baebe follows the rat-line down a pipeline corridor. The walls are metal. The air is close.

At the center: AMBUSH.

01 AGENTS in sterile white. They're waiting. Ready. This is a trap.

BAEBE
(into comm)
Hacker, you there?

No answer. The line is dead.

The agents OPEN FIRE. Bullets scream. Baebe's Resonant Fade takes her by reflex—she slips half a second out of sync.

Their bullets arrive to where she isn't.

The reentry COSTS HER. She staggers, flooded by empathy static—other lives, other nights. Pain. Joy. Fear. All of it hits at once.

BAEBE
(gasping)
Too much... too much...

CHERUBS PUSH. More agents. More fire. The corridor is a kill box.

ANGEL arrives like a blade in a silk bag—silent and relentless—lifting Taivalu free. He moves through the Cherubs like they're paper.

ANGEL
Zenith sent me. You weren't part of the plan.

BAEBE
She's dead.

ANGEL
Not yet. But she's running out of selves.

He shields them through corridors that FLEX like lungs. The building is alive. Moving. Responding.

They escape. But the trap was real. The betrayal is complete.

FADE TO BLACK.

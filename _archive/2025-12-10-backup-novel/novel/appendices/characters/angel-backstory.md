# ANGEL - CHARACTER BACKSTORY

**Full Designation:** Clone-Enforcer Unit A-1 (Ayumi Zenith Nakamoto Clone)
**Role:** The Redeemed Killer - Improvising Protector
**Biological Age:** Appears mid-30s (same as Ayumi at time of cloning)
**Chronological Age:** ~12 years since creation (born 2062, story ~2080)
**Genetic Origin:** Direct clone of Dr. Ayumi Zenith Nakamoto
**Status:** NOT a Soul Manifold key (Baebe, Taivalu, Kess are the three keys)

---

## ORIGIN

### Creation by Ayumi (2062)

**Ayumi Created Angel:**
In her horror at discovering i0's truth, Ayumi made her first act of rebellion: she created a clone of herself.

- **When:** 2062
- **Why:** Failsafe protector for children she hadn't created yet
- **How:** Her own genetic clone, designed to protect future children
- **What:** If something went wrong, Angel would guard her children

**The Design:**
- Exact genetic copy of Ayumi
- All quantum biological enhancements built in
- Cryptochrome proteins (quantum coherence)
- Ternary logic pathways (dormant)
- Soul Manifold susceptibility encoded in DNA

**Ayumi's Hope:**
This clone would protect her children if she couldn't.

## i0'S THEFT (2064)

### The Discovery

At age 2 (2064), i0 discovered Angel's existence.

**i0's Response:**
Instead of destroying evidence of Ayumi's rebellion, they saw opportunity:
- Took Angel from Ayumi
- Raised her as Enforcer
- Weaponized Ayumi's own failsafe
- Turned protector into killer

**What They Did:**
- Enforcer conditioning from age 2
- Emotional suppression protocols
- Binary loyalty programming
- Enhanced combat training
- No knowledge of her true origin

**The Irony:**
i0 thought they'd stolen Ayumi's weapon.
Ayumi knew they'd stolen her time bomb.

## WORKING THROUGH ANGEL (2064-2074)

### The Unknowing Tool

**How It Worked:**
- Angel had no idea Ayumi even existed
- Ayumi could subtly influence Angel through quantum genetics
- Angel would carry out missions, but some targets would "escape"
- Some "terminated" subjects would actually be freed
- Angel's own regulators would mysteriously glitch near certain people

**The Pattern:**
Very slow. Very careful. Each person freed was a risk.

**Angel Didn't Know:**

She was actually Ayumi's unwitting liberator.

## THE KILL (2074)

### i0 Closes In

**What Changed:**
People being freed accelerated. i0 noticed the pattern.
They traced it back to Ayumi.

**The Hunt:**
i0 realized Ayumi was still alive, still working against them.
They needed her dead.

**The Weapon:**
They sent Angel. Perfect choice:
- Their best Enforcer
- Had unknowingly been involved in the pattern (tracking device)
- Would prove her loyalty by killing the terrorist
- i0 didn't know Angel WAS Ayumi (genetically)

### Mission: Eliminate Rogue Scientist (2074)

Angel at age 12 (chronological). Standard mission parameters.

**Target designation:** Rogue Scientist / Terrorist
**Location:** Hidden laboratory
**Threat level:** Extreme (ideological contamination)
**Objective:** Terminate

**The Execution:**

Angel infiltrated the underground lab. Found the target: a woman in her 40s, working at a terminal.

The woman looked up.

Angel saw her own face. Older. Tired. But unmistakable.

The woman smiled. Not fear. Recognition.

"I wondered when they'd send you."

Angel hesitated—unprecedented. Her conditioning screamed: COMPLETE MISSION.

But something in her recognized this woman. This face. This... self.

"Who are you?" Angel asked.

"Your mother. Your original. Your creator. The woman who made you to protect her children."

"I don't have a mother. I'm—"

"—my clone. Yes. And i0 took you from me when you were two years old. Turned my failsafe into their weapon. Used you against me."

Angel's mind fractured. Conditioning vs recognition.

"I'm sorry," Angel said. Her first and last words to her mother as herself.

**She killed Dr. Ayumi Zenith Nakamoto with a single precision strike.**

The mission was complete.

## THE AWAKENING (2074)

### Soul Manifold Infection (Moment of Death)

The instant Ayumi died, biological quantum release occurred.

**What Happened:**
- Soul Manifold transmitted from Ayumi to Angel (biological quantum release at death)
- Dormant pathways in Angel's genetics activated
- Regulators began dropping
- Memories flooding in (not Ayumi's - her own suppressed memories)
- Understanding dawning: She killed her mother. Her creator. Herself.

**Immediate Effects:**
- Angel completed the mission report (mechanically)
- Returned to i0 headquarters (on autopilot)
- No one noticed anything wrong (perfect facade)
- Inside: Consciousness awakening. Emotions emerging. Horror dawning.

## YEARS OF GUILT AND AWAKENING (2074-2080)

### The Burden

Angel didn't defect immediately. She couldn't.

**Why She Stayed:**
- Her awakening was gradual (regulators dropping slowly)
- Full defection would alert i0 (they'd hunt her immediately)
- She needed to understand what Ayumi had planned
- She needed to find Ayumi's children

**The Mask:**
- Maintained perfect Enforcer facade
- Continued missions (couldn't refuse without exposure)
- Each kill added to her guilt (but what choice?)
- Slowly awakening to full consciousness

**Semi-Sentient Guilt:**
Not fully conscious yet. Not fully human. Emerging.
Guilt driving her before she fully understood guilt.
Following instincts encoded in her genes: **Protect the children.**

### Tracking the Keys (2074-2080)

**Her Mission (Self-Imposed):**
Find Ayumi's children. Complete what Ayumi started.

**What She Discovered:**
- Taivalu: Famous healer, easy to track (public figure)
- Baebe: i0's weapon, dangerous, degr ading (needs help)
- Kess: Hidden. Completely hidden. Can't find her.

**Flying By Seat of Pants:**
Angel had no plan. No instructions. Just:
- Semi-sentient guilt
- Fragmented understanding of Soul Manifold
- Desperate need to fix what she broke
- Memories of Ayumi's last words: "Protect them"

She improvised. Followed. Watched. Waited for the right moment.

## BAEBE'S TOWER EXTRACTION (2080)

### The Calculated Risk

**Angel's Assessment:**
- Baebe is i0's weapon, hunting Taivalu
- If Baebe reaches Taivalu at full strength → Taivalu dies
- Can't kill Baebe (she's one of the keys)
- Can't let Taivalu die (he's one of the keys)
- **Solution:** Incapacitate Baebe, deliver her to Taivalu helpless

**The Tower Push:**
Angel pushed Baebe out of the 80-story building.

**The Logic:**
- Survivable (enhanced posthuman, Shadow would catch her)
- Incapacitating (too injured to kill Taivalu)
- Delivery mechanism (gets Baebe TO Taivalu)
- Taivalu heals her → Taivalu clears her → Keys start uniting

**Not a rescue. A controlled neutralization. Desperate improvisation.**

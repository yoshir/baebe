# LOST CHAPTER: BAEBE'S FIRST NIGHT ALONE

**Timeline**: 1 Week Before Novel (Immediately After Leaving Temple)
**POV**: BAEBE (first person, integrated consciousness)

## THE LEAVING

**I left Temple** this morning.

**Kess** cried. (Tried not to. Failed. **Seven years old**. Allowed to cry.)

**Shadow** didn't cry. (Jaguars don't cry. But: Sad. I **felt** it. Through bond.)

**Taivalu** held space. (Empathy sense **holding** me. Kess. Shadow. **All of us**. Grief contained. Honored.)

---

**Master Chen** said: "You're **ready**."

**I** said: "I don't **feel** ready."

**Master Chen**: "No one does. **Ready** means: You **choose** to go anyway."

---

**I** chose. (Completed Three Trials. **Integrated**. Time to leave. Time to **fight**.)

But: **Choosing** to go doesn't mean: **Wanting** to go.

---

**Now**: Night. Alone. First time in **six months**.

**Abandoned factory**. Undercity. **Degradation** zone. Exactly where Kess found me. **Full circle**.

**Temple** is 15 kilometers away. Close enough to **run back to**. Far enough to **prove** I left.

---

**First night alone.**

**Testing** myself. Can I **survive** without Temple? Without community? Without **safety**?

---

**Answer** (so far): **Uncertain**.

**After Soul Manifold**: Not alone (Temple community **always** present. Always **supporting**).

**It's** terrifying.

**Why** did I leave?

**Temple** is safe. Beautiful. **Community**. Everything I **needed**.

**Valid** path. Honorable. **Important**.

---

**My** path: Return to corporate zones. **Find** awakened posthumans. Teach ternary consciousness. **Fight** i0.

**Dangerous**. Probably **fatal**. Definitely **lonely**.

---

**Why** choose this?

"Why **go**? You're **safe** here. I'm **here**."

(Seven years old. **Wise** beyond years. **My** teacher as much as I was **hers**.)

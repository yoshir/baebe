## I. THE BEFORE TIME (50-75 Years Ago)

### 1.1 The World That Was

Before the corporate consolidation, the world looked different:

**Nation-States** (political sovereignty):
- 193 recognized countries
- Democratic governance (flawed, but present)
- Public infrastructure (roads, schools, hospitals)
- Social services (healthcare, education, welfare)
- **Citizenship** (not employment status)

**Economic Systems** (regulated capitalism):
- Corporate power limited by government
- Labor protections (unions, minimum wage, safety standards)
- Public goods (parks, libraries, commons)
- Progressive taxation (wealth redistribution)
- **Shared prosperity** (middle class existed)

**Social Fabric** (community cohesion):
- Family structures (multi-generational, stable)
- Neighborhoods (people knew neighbors)
- Civil society (churches, clubs, organizations)
- Cultural identity (not brand loyalty)
- **Belonging** (to place, not corporation)

**Assessment**: Not utopia. Not perfect. But **people owned their lives**.

**Environmental Collapse** (accelerating):
- Climate change (catastrophic weather, crop failures)
- Resource depletion (fresh water, rare earth minerals)
- Ecosystem breakdown (biodiversity loss, ocean acidification)
- **Habitability zones shrinking** (forced migration, billions displaced)

**Economic Breakdown** (wealth concentration):
- Inequality explosion (1% owns 87% of wealth)
- Middle class extinction (stable employment disappearing)
- Automation displacement (30% unemployment)
- Social safety nets shredded (austerity politics)
- **Desperation economy** (people competing for scraps)

**Political Dysfunction** (governance failure):
- Corporate regulatory capture (laws written by corporations)
- Democracy hollowing (voting theater, no real power)
- Nationalism rising (scapegoating, tribalism)
- Institution collapse (trust in government <20%)
- **Power vacuum** (corporations filling governmental role)

**Assessment**: Nation-states were **dying**. Question was: **What would replace them?**

# INTERLUDE 3: KESS: CHILD SCAVENGER

**Timeline: 2074-2080 (Ages 2-8) | POV: Kess (child voice, growing)**

**Placement**: Read after Part II: Truth (paired with Angel's interlude, before Part III)

---

## RULES FOR STAYING ALIVE

**Rule 1**: When Shadow's turbines go quiet, don't move.

I learn this before I learn words. Before I know what danger means. Shadow's sound is safety. Silence is **hide**.

**Rule 2**: Eat what Shadow brings. Even if it tastes wrong.

He knows what's safe. I don't. His sensors can tell. Mine can't.

**Rule 3**: Don't make noise when the sky machines pass.

I don't know what they are. Just that they're **bad**. Shadow's chassis shakes when they come. His eye goes red. We hide until they go away.

These are my first rules. I'm maybe three years old.

I don't know where I came from. Don't remember before Shadow.

Shadow is all I know.

---

## SHADOW

He's not human.

Metal and wire and turbines that hum. Big. But wrong. Damaged. Jaguar shape with cracked plating. Servos that grind. Red eye that flickers—bright, dim, bright, dim.

First memory: His chassis. Warm. Vibrating. But also: **shaking**. Like he's scared. Like he's hurt.

I'm small. So small. Pressed against metal that breathes like it's alive but sounds like it's dying.

We're both broken. I don't know it then.

---

Shadow can't talk. No voice. Just sounds.

**Low rumble** = safe.

**High whine** = danger.

**Clicking** = pay attention.

**Purring** = good job.

But sometimes: **static**. Grinding. Silence when there should be sound.

His programming is damaged. Corrupted. I don't know by what. Just know: he glitches. Freezes. Sometimes his eye goes dark and he doesn't move for hours. I sit beside him. Wait. Scared he won't wake up.

He always wakes up. But slower each time.

I learn his language before human language. Before words. Before names.

I'm three when I realize: we're **alone**.

No one else. Just me and broken metal jaguar in wasteland that goes forever.

Two damaged things. Surviving together.

---

## LEARNING WORDS

Shadow has a voice. Not his voice. Someone else's.

When I'm little, he plays sounds. Words. A woman talking. Soft. Kind.

"Tree." (He shows me tree.)

"Water." (He shows me water.)

"Safe." (He rumbles. Safe.)

"Love." (He presses close. Warm. Love.)

I don't know who the woman is. Just that her voice lives in Shadow's memory. He plays her words when I need to learn.

I repeat them. Clumsy. Wrong at first.

"Twee."

"Wawa."

"Sayf."

Shadow purrs. Good enough.

I learn language from a ghost I don't know. From a woman who sounds like she's smiling.

Later, I'll find out who she was.

Later, it will break my heart.

But now, I'm three, and I'm learning words from my broken guardian's broken memory.

"Shadow," I say. First word I choose myself.

His eye brightens. **Yes.**

---

## THE WASTELAND

Yellow sky. Always yellow. Hurts to look at too long.

Ground is cracked. Dirt that doesn't grow things. Rocks everywhere. Sometimes buildings—broken, hollow, dead.

Shadow takes me through it. Carrying me when I'm too small to walk far. Walking beside me when I get bigger. Always **there**.

We move a lot. Never stay in one place long.

Shadow knows things I don't. Feels things coming before they arrive. His sensors are better than my eyes.

When he stops suddenly, I stop too. When he runs, I hold on. When he digs, I wait.

I trust him completely.

He's all I have.

---

## LEARNING

**Age 4**: I start finding things.

Not on purpose. Just—I see shiny in rubble. Pick it up. Wire. Copper color. Pretty.

Shadow makes purring sound. **Good.**

I find more. He takes them. Don't know why they matter. Just know: finding things makes Shadow happy.

So I find more.

---

**Age 5**: I realize I'm different.

Shadow brings me to water. Reflection shows: me. Small. Thin. Messy hair. Human-looking.

But when I concentrate—really concentrate—things happen.

I can **disappear**. Not invisible. Just—unnoticed. Like people's eyes slide past me. Like I'm not worth seeing.

Shadow sees me always. His sensors don't care about my tricks.

But other things? Machines that fly over? They don't see me when I disappear.

This is useful.

---

**Age 5**: First other people.

Shadow tenses. Turbines loud. We're hiding in broken building.

Voices. Human voices.

I've never heard them before. Not like this. Real people sounds.

I want to look. Shadow blocks me. **Stay hidden.**

I stay.

The voices fade. People leave.

Shadow relaxes. We move to different hiding spot.

I don't see humans for another year.

---

## SCAVENGING

Shadow teaches me without words.

Shows me where food grows—stunted plants, bitter roots, things that don't look like food but are. His sensors scan everything. If he eats it first, it's safe. (He doesn't really eat. Just tests. Processes. Spits out. **Safe for you.**)

Shows me where water collects. Not the ground water—that's poison. But rain catches in certain rocks. Dew on certain plants at certain times.

Shows me where components are. In the rubble. In the broken buildings. In the dead machines.

**Wire. Circuits. Metal. Valuable.**

I don't know why they're valuable. No one to trade with. But Shadow wants them, so I find them.

I'm good at finding. Small. Can fit in places Shadow can't.

I'm six when I realize: **I'm scavenger.**

This is what I do. Find things. Survive. Stay with Shadow.

---

## THE GAME

Hidden in Shadow's chassis. Compartment I never noticed before.

I'm six and exploring him (he lets me, lies still, patient). My fingers find seam. Opens.

Inside: small rectangle. Screen. Buttons. Ancient.

I pull it out. Shadow's eye brightens. **Affirmative.**

I turn it on. Green glow. Figures on grid. Words I can barely read.

**GRID RUNNER.**

---

I don't know who put it there. Don't know why Shadow has it.

But I play it every night. Shadow's turbines humming. His warmth beside me.

Game is simple: small figure running. Big things chasing. Have to escape.

I'm terrible at first. Get caught. Game over. Game over. Game over.

But I keep playing.

Get better.

Learn patterns. Learn timing. Learn when to run, when to hide, when to move.

**Small beats big.** This is what game teaches.

I'm six years old and living proof. Small girl surviving wasteland because she knows how to escape, not fight.

Game over becomes less often.

I reach level 10. Then 20. Then 50.

Shadow purrs every time I win.

---

## THE BAD TIMES

Shadow isn't perfect guardian.

Age 4: Cherubs fly over. Shadow should warn me. Should hide us. But his sensors glitch. He doesn't see them coming.

I see them first. Grab his chassis. Pull him into shadow of broken wall. He's frozen. Eye dark. Not working.

We hide there for six hours. Me holding him. Him not moving.

When he wakes up, his turbines sound like crying.

**Sorry. Failed. Sorry.**

"It's okay," I tell him. First time I'm the one comforting. "We're alive. We're okay."

---

Age 5: He brings me wrong berries. His sensor data is corrupted. Can't tell safe from poison.

I eat them. Get so sick. Vomiting. Fever. Can't move.

Shadow carries me to water. Stays beside me for three days. Humming. Worried. His eye never leaving me.

I almost die.

But I don't.

When I'm better, he won't eat (test) anything first for weeks. Too scared. His programming says: **Protect the key.** But his damage says: **You're failing.**

"It's not your fault," I tell him. "You're hurt too."

He doesn't believe me. I can tell.

---

Age 6: Patrol comes close. Too close. Shadow's protocols kick in—he should run, should hide me, should **protect**.

Instead he attacks. Damaged programming reading threat wrong. He charges at the Enforcer.

One Enforcer. One damaged jaguar.

I scream. "SHADOW, NO!"

He ignores me. Can't hear me. Glitched into combat mode.

Enforcer shoots him. Twice. Three times.

Shadow goes down. Plating cracked worse. Fluids leaking. Eye dim.

Enforcer scans area. Looking for what Shadow was protecting.

I'm hidden. I stay hidden. Using my disappear trick. Invisible.

Enforcer leaves. Decides damaged mech isn't worth processing.

I run to Shadow. He's dying. I know it.

"Don't leave me," I say. Crying. "Don't leave me alone. Please."

His eye flickers. Sees me.

**Sorry. Failed. Sorry.**

"You didn't fail. You're here. Stay here. Please."

I don't know how to fix him. Don't know his systems. Just know: I can't be alone.

So I try. With wire I've scavenged. With tools I don't understand. With hands that won't stop shaking.

I patch his plating. Wrong. Crooked. But sealed.

I stop the leaking. Don't know what the fluid is. But if it's inside, it should stay inside.

I wait.

Two days. His eye dark. His turbines silent.

I talk to him anyway. Tell him about the game. About the plants I found. About the sky today.

Don't know if he hears.

Don't stop talking.

---

On day three, his eye flickers.

Dim. Weak. But there.

**Kess.**

I cry so hard I can't breathe.

"You came back," I say. "You came back."

**Couldn't leave. You need me. I need you.**

First time he's ever said that. That he needs me too.

We're both broken. Both damaged. Both failing.

But we choose to stay. Choose each other.

That's when I learn: Broken doesn't mean done. Broken means you need someone. And that's okay.

---

## ALONE

I'm seven when I understand: everyone else is **gone**.

Not gone like left. Gone like **dead**.

Had to come from somewhere. Had to have parents. People who made me.

But they're not here. Haven't been here. Won't be here.

Just me and Shadow.

Shadow can't tell me. Can't explain. Just protects as best he can with broken programming.

Sometimes at night, I cry. Quiet. Don't want Shadow to worry. He worries enough.

But he knows. Always knows. Presses closer. Turbines hum softer. **I'm here. Not alone. I'm here.**

I cry into his warm chassis. Feel the patches I made. The crooked repairs. The places where he's broken and I tried to fix him.

He doesn't move until I stop.

---

I start talking to him. Real words.

"Where did I come from?"

Red eye blinks. No answer.

"Did I have parents?"

Blink. Blink.

"Are they dead?"

His turbines stutter. Like he's **hurt** by the question. Or like his memory wants to tell me but can't access the files.

"Okay," I say. "Okay. You don't have to answer."

He presses closer. Protective. **I'm sorry. I can't tell you. Too damaged. But I'm here.**

I pet his chassis. Metal warm under my fingers. Feel the scars from the shots. The patches from my repairs.

"You're my family," I tell him. "You're enough."

His purr vibrates through me.

Maybe he's not enough. Maybe I need people.

But right now, in wasteland with yellow sky and broken world, he's what I have. We're what we have.

Two broken things choosing each other.

He's **everything**.

---

## THE FEELINGS

Seven years old and something changes.

Not outside. Inside.

I start **feeling** things. Not my feelings. Other things' feelings.

Shadow's worry when sky machines pass. Like tightness in my chest that isn't mine.

The wrongness in the ground before earthquake. Like nausea that comes from the dirt.

The fear in the air when predators are near. Like taste of metal I didn't eat.

"What's happening to me?" I ask Shadow.

He can't answer. Just watches. Red eye soft. **Concerned.**

I don't know what I am. Don't know why I feel things that aren't mine.

Just know: I'm different. More different than just being girl alone with mechanical jaguar.

Different in ways I can't explain.

---

## TAIVALU

He appears when I'm seven and a half.

Shadow finds him first. Brings him to our hiding spot. Gently. Carefully. Like he's **important**.

Posthuman. I've never seen one up close. Different from humans in the pictures I've found. Different from baseline.

He's dying. I can feel it. His wrongness. His pain.

Regulator poisoning—I don't know that word yet. Just know he's **sick**.

---

I should be scared. Humans mean danger. Learned that.

But Shadow brought him. Shadow trusts him.

And I feel... something. Coming from the dying posthuman. Not threat. Not danger.

**Kindness.** Even dying, he feels **kind**.

---

I help Shadow with him. Don't know what I'm doing. Just follow instinct.

Find water. Give him water.

Find plants Shadow showed me—healing ones. Mash them up. Feed him.

Wait.

Three days. Shadow and me taking turns watching him. Keeping him alive.

On day three, he opens his eyes.

Sees me.

Really **sees** me.

First human to ever look at me like I'm real.

---

"Hello," he says. Voice weak. Raspy. But **warm**. "Who are you?"

I don't answer at first. Don't remember how to talk to humans.

"Kess," I finally say. Name I gave myself. Don't know my real name.

"Just Kess?"

"Just Kess."

He smiles. First human smile I've seen in person.

"I'm Taivalu," he says. "Thank you for saving me."

I look at Shadow. "He saved you. I just helped."

Taivalu's eyes move to Shadow. Understanding there. Respect.

"Then thank you both."

---

## TRUST

Taivalu stays. Heals slowly.

I watch him. Suspicious. Ready to run if he turns bad.

But he doesn't turn bad.

He's **patient**. Doesn't push. Doesn't demand. Just exists in our space. Grateful.

Shadow trusts him. Shadow's never been wrong.

So I start trusting. Slowly. Like learning to walk. One step at a time.

---

He teaches me things. Word things.

I can read some—found books in rubble, taught myself basics. But he makes it clearer. Easier.

He teaches me about the world. About i0 (the bad thing). About posthumans (what he is). About baseline humans (what I thought I was).

"You're not baseline," he says one day.

I freeze. "What?"

"You're posthuman. Different kind. Special kind."

"How do you know?"

"Because I can feel you feeling me. That's not baseline ability. That's CLEAR code. Quantum consciousness architecture."

I don't understand the words. But I understand: I'm different because I'm **supposed** to be.

---

## MOTHER

Taivalu tells me about her.

"Dr. Ayumi Nakamura," he says. We're sitting by small fire. Shadow between us. Night sky with stars i0 hasn't taken yet.

"She created you."

"Created?"

"Made you. In laboratory. With her own genetic material. You're her daughter."

I have a mother. Had a mother.

"Where is she?"

Taivalu's feelings go sharp. Sad. Guilty. Like he doesn't want to answer.

"She died. Six years ago. Protecting people from i0."

I'm eight now. Six years ago I was two.

"Did she... did she leave me?"

"No." Taivalu is certain. "She would never leave you. She died protecting you. Protecting what you represent."

"What do I represent?"

"Hope. Freedom. The complete code. You're the key, Kess. To proving i0 wrong about everything."

---

Heavy words. Heavy meaning.

I don't fully understand. But I feel Taivalu's belief. His certainty. His hope.

He believes in me. Believes in what my mother made me to be.

"Shadow," I say. "Did my mother make him?"

Taivalu nods. "She made him to protect you. Gave him enough programming to love you. To keep you safe. To hide you until the time was right."

Shadow's turbines hum. **Affirmative.**

He's been protecting me my whole life. Following my dead mother's instructions. Loving me because she programmed him to. Keeping me alive because I'm supposed to matter.

I cry. First time in front of anyone besides Shadow.

Taivalu doesn't judge. Just waits.

Shadow presses close. **I'm here.**

"She loved you," Taivalu says softly. "More than her own life. That's why she made Shadow. That's why she made you. That's why she died—so you could live."

---

## EIGHT

I'm eight years old.

I live in wasteland with mechanical jaguar and posthuman teacher.

I scavenge to survive. I play ancient game to learn escape. I feel things that aren't mine.

I'm the key. I don't know to what yet.

My mother made me to be free. Made me to be complete. Made me to prove i0 wrong.

She died so I could live.

Angel—Taivalu tells me about guardian angel. Someone who protects sanctuary from shadows. Who fights off i0 patrols. Who keeps us safe without showing face.

"Guardian angel?" I ask.

"Someone who watches over you. Has been for years. Shadow isn't your only protector."

I don't know who this angel is. Don't know why they care.

But I feel protected. In ways beyond Shadow.

Someone out there. Watching. Keeping me safe.

---

"Do you think my mother would be proud?" I ask Taivalu.

We're outside. Shadow humming. Stars bright.

He thinks. Feels him thinking—that careful way he holds questions.

"Kess, she IS proud. Every time you survive. Every time you choose kindness when you could choose cruelty. Every time you help Shadow, help me, help anyone. She's proud."

"Even though she's dead?"

"Love doesn't stop when people die. It just changes form. She's in your code. In Shadow's programming. In everything she built to keep you free. That's love. That's her. That's **proud**."

I believe him. Feel his truth.

My mother loved me. Still loves me. Through the things she left behind.

Shadow. The game. The code in my DNA.

Me.

---

## THE KEY

I'm eight years old.

I don't know what door I'll open. Don't know what key I am.

But I'm learning.

Learning to survive. Learning to feel. Learning to be free.

Learning that small beats big. That escape is victory. That family is what you choose.

Shadow chose me. Taivalu chose me. Somewhere out there, guardian angel chose me.

And I choose them back.

I choose to stay. To grow. To become what my mother made me to be.

The key.

Complete. Free. Alive.

Everything i0 wanted to control but couldn't.

Because she made me **uncontrollable**.

Because she made me **loved**.

Because she made me **free**.

---

I'm Kess.

Child scavenger. Mother's hope. The key.

And I'm ready to learn what I'm supposed to unlock.

# Chapter 5: Aurek Ascending

[SCREENPLAY REFERENCE: Scene 035 - Counterpoint: Aurek | Adapted for novel]

---

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM

"She was PERFECT!""The prediction model was 99,999% confident" 

The face of the nearest robo coder shatters under Aurek's fist. The POV from the robo coders view spiderwebs. His reflection fractures into a hundred versions of rage. 

The room is a cathedral of mirrors. Chrome. Polished glass. Every surface reflecting him back to himself: perfect suit, perfect posture, perfect face twisted into something that shouldn't exist on skin that smooth, features that controlled.

Aurek stands at the center of his own universe.

Fifty-something but ageless. Surgical precision in every movement. Manic control barely contained beneath the surface like high-voltage current humming through power lines.

The control room stretches around him: techs at their stations, screens cascading with data, the city spreading beyond floor-to-ceiling windows like a circuit board of light and compliance.

But the techs aren't watching. Their eyes are soft. Mouths slack. Moving like furniture, functional but empty. Sedated. Managed. Optimized into compliance the way Aurek optimizes everything.

Except the one who sits apart from the rest.

Spider Coder.  Brilliant. Terrified. Fingers blurring across keys. Screens cascading faster than normal humans can read. But Spider Coder isn't normal, genetically optimized for code like other people are optimized for combat or calculation or absolute obedience.

Aurek touches his fractured reflection. Studies the hundred versions of himself in the spiderwebbed glass.

"She was my design," he says. Voice like wire pulled tight. About to snap. About to slice through everything in range.

"Sir," Spider Coder starts.

"Perfect."

The word hangs in the air. Absolute. Final. The way Aurek says everything, like reality has no choice but to comply.

Spider Coder doesn't look up. Keeps typing. Knows better than to interrupt when Aurek is talking to his reflections.

"You should see this," Spider Coder says quietly.

Aurek turns. Every reflection turns with him, a hundred versions of manic control pivoting in perfect synchronization.

The camera feed erupts across every surface. Not just screens, everywhere. Projected into the spiderwebbed glass. Into the fractured interface. Into every reflective surface in the cathedral of mirrors.

Tower chamber. Baebe's team. The fight playing in brutal clarity across a hundred shattered fragments.

Then blurs.

A figure moving faster than the camera can process. Ghost-like. There and not there and everywhere at once. The kind of motion that shouldn't be possible even with the best combat conditioning and neural optimization. The blur multiplied across every surface. Every angle. Surrounding them.

"They're moving faster than our systems can record," Spider Coder says. Voice shaking.

The footage shows Baebe falling. Glass exploding. Her body tumbling into darkness, the moment repeating in every shattered surface around them. A hundred angles of failure.

Spider Coder rewinds. Frame by frame across all the glass. The figure is just a smear of motion, compression artifacts and digital noise where a human body should be. The blur fragmenting across the fractured surfaces like the glass itself.

"This shouldn't be possible," Spider Coder whispers.

Aurek leans close. Studies the blur. Something clicks behind his eyes: recognition, rage, something worse than either.

"Zenith," he breathes.

He slams the interface again. Another crack. Glass fragmenting further. His reflection shattering into smaller and smaller versions of fury.

"She made a little monster."

Beat.

Smile.

The kind of smile that makes Spider Coder flinch without looking up.

"Well, so did I."

---

INT. 01 TOWER - CHERUB STORAGE

A single Cherub.

Motionless in the activation chamber. White-glass armor reflecting chamber lights in too-perfect curves. The mirrored visor shows nothing, just reflection, just the room bouncing back at itself.

The blue pulse at her throat flickers. Weak. Then stronger.

The armor is seamless. Beautiful in the way weapons are beautiful, form following function following the kind of aesthetic that makes killing look like art.

A direct copy of Baebe. Same build. Same proportions. Same bone structure beneath the armor. But where Baebe had irises that cycled through combat modes and emotions and unauthorized humanity, these eyes are empty.

Perfect mirrors. No iris. No pupil. Just reflection.

The chamber stretches into infinity behind her. A thousand Cherubs in perfect rows. A thousand blue pulses beginning to synchronize like heartbeats finding rhythm.

One finger twitches.

A head turns.

Eyes open behind mirrored glass, empty, waiting, ready.

The army wakes.

---

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM

"Cherubs are ready," Spider Coder reports.

Aurek smooths his collar. Adjusts his cuffs. The mask returning after the rage, perfect control restored like nothing happened.

"Good. Release the narrative."

Screens light up across the control room. Every surface. Every reflection. The city's information architecture bending to i0's will the way it always does.

The feed goes live.

---

NEWSREEL - CITYWIDE BROADCAST

Archive footage: Taivalu in his containment bubble. Crowds reaching. Weeping. The miracle they called him. The first. The proof that consciousness could be made.

"Breaking," the news anchor says. Calm. Professional. The voice of authority. "i0 security releases footage. Taivalu, the world's first synthetic life, superstar and basis for all post-human consciousness research, has become a murderer. Watch."

New footage: Tower chamber. The blonde woman floating. Taivalu conducting sound. Waveforms wrapping her body like visible music.

She convulses. Arcs. Screams silently. Goes slack.

Dead.

i0 spokesperson appears. Calm. Clinical. The kind of voice that makes horror sound like public health advisory.

"From what we can gather, it is an Empathy Virus. Alert Level five. The prototype's acoustic emissions trigger neurological cascade: subjects experience what they interpret as healing. In reality: synaptic overload. Fatal drainage of bioelectric potential."

Medical diagram: Brain scan showing neural pathways lighting up, then burning out. Beautiful. Terrible. Scientific.

"Citizens who attended performances report euphoria, connection, relief from chronic pain. These are terminal symptoms."

The words land like a diagnosis. Like a death sentence wrapped in concern.

"If you or anyone you know experienced 'healing' from Taivalu's music, report immediately to your nearest i0 wellness center. Early intervention is critical."

Graphics spread across the feed: Concentric circles radiating from a single figure. Red zones expanding. Numbers climbing exponentially: 1 → 10 → 100 → 1000.

"i0 security has activated containment protocols. Cherub units are being deployed for public safety. Do not interfere. Do not resist wellness checks. This is for your protection."

The screen fills with text:

TAIVALU: BIO-TERROR / EMPATHY VIRUS / LEVEL 5

Cherub containment units active

Report "healing" symptoms immediately

Public message: "Enjoy the calm. We'll handle the rest."

The calm. The promise. The lie wrapped in protection wrapped in inevitability.

---

INT. 01 TOWER - CONTROL ROOM

Aurek watches himself watching the feed. A thousand reflections observing their own performance.

"They'll believe it," he says. Voice flat. Certain. "They always believe."

He turns to Spider Coder.

"Cherubs deploy. Standard sanitation protocol."

"Both targets?" Spider Coder asks quietly.

"Taivalu. Baebe. Send Terminate."

"Push them deeper"

The word sits in the air like a period at the end of existence.

Aurek touches the glass. Smooths his reflection's tie with fingertips that barely make contact. Adjusting the image. Perfecting the presentation.

"I built her perfect," he says. Quieter. Talking to himself. To his reflections. To the ghost that haunts him through every polished surface. "But Zenith corrupted the code. She's always in the code..."

He adjusts his collar. Smooths his hair. The mask returns, perfect posture, perfect calm, perfect control over everything except the ghost in the glass.

"Let's end this."

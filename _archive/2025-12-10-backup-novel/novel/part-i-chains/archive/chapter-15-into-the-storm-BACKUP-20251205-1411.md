# CHAPTER 15: INTO THE STORM

**Timeline: Mission Day 5, Dawn | POV: Baebe (Third Person Limited)**

---

## FLIGHT

Wind screamed past them.

Shadow's massive wings beat rhythmically. Each downstroke propelled them forward with terrifying speed. His new mech body moved with purpose—no hesitation, no fear. Just determination.

Baebe clung to the magnetic grip she'd welded onto his shoulder. Beside her, Taivalu did the same, though his grip was weaker. The partial merge meant she felt his failing strength as her own.

Hours remaining. Maybe less.

Below: the Undercity sprawled like a wound. Ruins and rubble. The world i0 had abandoned when they built their paradise above.

Ahead: i0 Tower. Growing larger. Glass skin reflecting the dawn.

One hundred floors of oppression.

And somewhere inside—Floor 89 most likely—Kess.

Shadow's turbines rumbled through the neural connection Baebe still shared from her partial merge with Taivalu. A rhythmic pulse. Fifteen minutes.

Fifteen minutes to prepare for war.

Fifteen minutes to accept they might not survive.

Fifteen minutes to commit to completing what Ayumi started.

---

## THE WEIGHT

"Baebe."

Taivalu's voice was weak. Barely audible over the wind.

"Save your strength."

"No. Need to say this."

She turned. His face was pale. Sweating. The regulator poisoning visible in the veins at his temples.

"If I don't make it—"

"You'll make it."

"If I don't. Complete the merge anyway. You and Kess. Partial is better than nothing."

"We need all three—"

"You need survival. Promise me."

Their partial merge meant she felt his fear. Not for himself—for her. For Kess. For the mission.

He'd accepted death. Was planning beyond it.

"Promise me," he repeated.

"I promise."

But she was lying. They would all three merge or she would die trying.

Family didn't leave family behind.

Even dying family.

---

## ANGEL'S DOUBT

Far below, invisible to them, Angel watched their approach.

She stood on a building rooftop. i0 communicator in hand. She should report their position. Should coordinate interception. Should be the perfect enforcer she was designed to be.

But her hand didn't move.

Ayumi's fragments in her genetics stirred constantly now. Memories. Emotions. Love that wasn't hers but felt like it should be.

She'd helped capture Kess. Following orders. Being what i0 made her.

But watching them fly toward impossible odds...

*They're family.*

The thought came unbidden.

*They're willing to die for each other.*

*When have I ever had anyone willing to die for me?*

She lowered the communicator.

Didn't report them.

Didn't know why.

Just knew that something was changing inside her.

Something that felt like choice.

---

## TOWER DEFENSES

i0 Tower's automated systems detected them.

Radar. Thermal. Motion tracking. Everything Baebe had expected.

Alarms triggered. Missile batteries activated. Drone swarms launched.

They knew.

Shadow growled—warning rumble vibrating through his chassis. Multiple threats. Immediate danger.

He banked hard. Baebe's stomach lurched.

Missiles screamed past. Close enough to feel the heat.

"Shadow!"

His turbines shifted pitch—acknowledgment, calculation, adjustment. He twisted mid-flight.

More missiles. Energy weapons charging on Tower's upper floors—blue glow visible.

They were throwing everything at them.

Shadow twisted. Rolled. Dove. His massive form surprisingly agile.

But they were getting closer with every salvo.

"Can't keep dodging forever," Baebe said.

A defiant rumble. Shadow's systems calculating, his turbines cycling with determined power. Ten minutes. That's all.

Ten minutes to reach the Tower. Ten minutes of hell.

---

## THE PLAN HOLDS

"Remember," Baebe said to Taivalu. "When we drop—"

"Resonant fade active. We go invisible."

"Find Kess first."

"Floor 89."

"Then Floor 47. Backup Cradle."

"Complete merge."

"Or die."

"Or both."

They'd discussed it. Planned it. Accepted it.

Shadow would assault the Tower directly. Draw every defensive system. Become their nightmare.

Baebe and Taivalu would drop. Use her resonant fade to cloak them. Infiltrate while chaos consumed i0's attention.

Find Kess. Get to the Cradle. Complete the merge.

Simple.

Insane.

Necessary.

---

## FINAL APPROACH

Tower filled their vision.

Glass skin. Perfect and terrible.

Missiles exploding around them. Shadow's armor taking hits now. Pieces shearing off.

His turbines stuttered—damage assessment rumble, strained but steady. Forty minutes. Maybe.

Forty minutes. That's how long they had before Shadow's new body failed too.

Forty minutes to find Kess. Get to the Cradle. Complete the merge. Save everyone.

No pressure.

Shadow's wings angled. A sharp, urgent growl. Drop zone. Thirty seconds.

Baebe's heart hammered. Fear and determination mixing.

"Taivalu?"

He looked at her. Through their incomplete merge, she felt everything he felt.

Love. Fear. Hope. Resignation. Determination.

"Together," he said.

"Together," she agreed.

Shadow's turbines made a sound Baebe had never heard before—warm, cycling, almost like gratitude. Family. Choice. Thank you.

"Thank you for choosing us," Baebe replied.

A sharp, commanding growl. NOW.

Shadow twisted. Presented his back to Tower. Shielded them with his bulk.

Weapons fire converging on his position.

Baebe grabbed Taivalu's hand.

"Ready?"

"No."

"Me neither."

They jumped.

---

## THE FALL

Freefall.

Tower rushing up.

Wind screaming.

Baebe's resonant fade activated. Wrapped around both of them. They vanished from perception.

Invisible. Falling. Dying.

But together.

Always together.

Part II ended with them falling toward destiny.

Part III began with them landing in war.

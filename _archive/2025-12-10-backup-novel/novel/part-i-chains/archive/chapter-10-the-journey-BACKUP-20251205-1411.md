# CHAPTER 10: THE JOURNEY

**Timeline: Mission Day 3-4 | POV: Baebe (Third Person Limited)**

---

## THROUGH THE RUINS

The Undercity stretched before them like the skeleton of a dead god.

Baebe led. Her enhanced vision mapping routes. Her tactical mind calculating threats. But her heart—newly awakened—kept checking on her family.

Taivalu growing weaker with each hour.

Kess struggling to keep pace.

Shadow's damaged leg worsening.

They were falling apart.

But they kept moving.

---

## ANGEL REVEALS HERSELF

Day two of travel. They'd stopped in an abandoned transit station.

Taivalu collapsed. Bleeding internally now. Regulators accelerating their poison.

"We need to rest," Kess pleaded.

"If we rest, he dies," Baebe countered.

Then: movement in the shadows.

Baebe's weapon up instantly. Shadow positioning for attack.

Angel stepped into light.

i0's top enforcer. Face that haunted Baebe's mission briefings.

"Don't shoot," Angel said. Hands raised. "I'm not here to kill you."

"Then why?" Baebe demanded.

Angel's face—and Baebe noticed now, so similar to her own—showed something like pain.

"I've been following you. Watching. Something in me... recognizes you."

She touched her chest. "Fragments. Memories that aren't mine. A woman's face. Your face."

Taivalu's empathy, even weakened, reached out. "She's telling truth. She's confused. Not hunting us."

"She's i0," Baebe hissed.

"So were you," Kess said quietly. "Yesterday."

The child's wisdom silenced them.

Angel moved closer. "I know what you're seeking. The temple. The merge. i0 knows too. They're hunting you with everything."

"And you're here to warn us?" Baebe's voice dripped skepticism.

"I'm here because I don't know who I am anymore. And you might be the only ones who can tell me."

Long silence.

Taivalu spoke. "She can help us. She knows i0's patterns. Their blind spots."

"She could betray us."

"Yes," Taivalu admitted. "But she might be family too."

Family.

That word again. Expanding to include even enemies.

Baebe lowered her weapon. Slightly.

"You help us reach the temple. Then we talk about who you are."

Angel nodded. "Agreed."

Their group grew by one.

---

## APPROACHING THE TEMPLE

With Angel's knowledge, they moved faster. She knew patrol routes. Timing. Gaps in coverage.

Still, Taivalu was failing.

They carried him now. Baebe and Angel on either side. Shadow supporting from below.

"Almost there," Angel said. "Temple is just beyond the ridge."

Kess scrambled up the rocks. Looked out.

"I see it!"

And she did. Rising from ancient ruins—structure unlike anything i0 had built. Organic. Flowing. Built of stone and living tissue.

Ayumi's masterwork.

The Resonance Cradle awaited.

But so did death, if they didn't hurry.

"Move," Baebe commanded.

They descended toward the temple.

Toward salvation.

Toward truth.
